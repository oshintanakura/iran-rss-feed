Honorable Peace: Seyyed Mohammad Khatami's support for an "honorable peace" has not pleased extremist circles. Some of them ask what is meant by an "honorable peace"? Mr. Khatami himself has explained it. An honorable peace means the revival and full implementation of the memorandum officially signed between the presidents of Iran and the United States. There are three alternatives to this memorandum. First, the resumption of war, whose costs and consequences are obvious. Second, the continuation of the current situation and the persistence of the economic siege of the country, whose consequences need no explanation. Third, accepting a unilateral formula that does not lead to the lifting of sanctions.
