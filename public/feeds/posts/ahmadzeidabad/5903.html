Checkpoints: In times of war or security threats, no government can be absolutely prohibited from establishing checkpoints. However, it is clear that the establishment of checkpoints, when security requires, must be fully calculated and professional in every way to cause the least inconvenience and trouble for citizens. The checkpoints established since the beginning of the 40-day war in cities across the country, including Tehran, do not follow a uniform process. Sometimes they are trouble-free and sometimes bothersome. A driver recounted that on one of the recent nights, while tired and exhausted around 11 PM, heading to his home in Naziabad, he was stuck behind a traffic jam related to the area's checkpoint on Shapour Street for about 40 minutes and was completely frustrated. It is expected from the officials of these stations that they make the mechanism of any inspection completely professional so as not to cause citizens' nerves to fray. #احمد_زیدآبادی @ahmadzeidabad
