Defective Structure of Consciousness! Why is there so much emphasis in Persian literature on learning lessons from history? For the obvious reason that we Iranians have the least interest in learning lessons from history; otherwise, why would this topic be repeated to such an extent? The experience of August 28, 1953 (28 Mordad 1332) is right before our eyes! No political force can be found that has learned the necessary lesson from that event. The descendants of all the forces that played a role, negatively or positively, in that catastrophe are not only repeating those mistakes but have intensified their errors. Is it, as Ahmad Shamlou said, that our historical memory as Iranians is weak? The weakness of historical memory is not without a role in this matter, but in my opinion, the "defective structure of consciousness" plays a much larger role. The structure of consciousness in any society, in my view, is much more important than other structures, and until it is reformed, the reform of political and social structures seems impossible. Political and social structures can be shattered by revolt and revolution, but if the structure of consciousness is not sound, those two structures will be reproduced in a more horrific way. Reforming the structure of consciousness of a society, however, is infinitely difficult, because first, reaching a collective agreement on the criteria for whether a thought or consciousness is defective or sound, especially in a society where everyone is infatuated with their own thoughts, is beyond difficulty. Second, the prerequisite for reforming the structure of society's consciousness begins with self-criticism and continues with the necessary patience to convince others through discussion and dialogue. In our country, self-criticism is considered a sign of weakness and is truly seen as self-flagellation. Hence, finding faults must be sought not in one's own camp but in the opposing camp. When we do not acknowledge any weakness for ourselves and see our own camp at the "tip of the arrow of historical evolution!" it is natural that we also do not find the patience for persuasive dialogue and consider the elimination, disregard, and belittlement of others as an easier path! In short, for historical reasons, we have developed a defective structure of consciousness, and again for historical reasons, we have shown and do not show any interest in reforming it. Hence, we repeat our fate in a closed circle. This time, however, the previously traveled paths are also blocked. Therefore, the most astonishing events in history await us. #احمد_زیدآبادی @ahmadzeidabad
