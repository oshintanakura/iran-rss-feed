Which massive base? The managing director of Kayhan newspaper, in his paper's "Note" column, addressed to Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, the Speaker of Parliament, wrote: "Some existing evidence suggests that a Western-oriented current and a number of reformist claimants have targeted changing Mr. Ghalibaf's 'social base' and are pursuing the transfer of his social base from among the vast and devoted masses of the people to the arena of political activity of reformist claimants and Western-oriented figures." Aside from what social base Mr. Ghalibaf has, what role he now seeks to play in the country's political developments, and what capabilities and limitations he faces, it is clear that the managing director of Kayhan, in his usual manner, is throwing dust in the eyes of the Speaker of Parliament to darken his view of the stark social realities in the country. The clearest evidence for this claim is the phrase "vast and devoted masses of the people" describing the social base of the faction affiliated with the managing director of Kayhan, which, in his view, Mr. Ghalibaf is supposedly going to leave behind to shift to the "arena of political activity of reformists"! Really, which "vast mass" in this country supports the faction affiliated with Hossein Shariatmadari and counts as its social base, that he warns Mr. Ghalibaf about losing this "vast popular base"? Iranian society and its diversity are before the eyes of every one of us, and any unbiased and healthy-minded person, by taking a look around this society, can discern the true size of the base of the force favored by Mr. Shariatmadari! Given this, why does the managing director of Kayhan distort reality? Is it because, due to being disconnected from society, he does not see reality? Or does he fundamentally have no interest in seeing reality? Or is he not so unaware of reality, but sees his own interest in distorting and presenting it upside down? I usually assumed that the country's decision-makers are themselves aware of these issues and dismiss claims like those of Kayhan, but some of them apparently not only do not dismiss Kayhan's claims but have come to believe that whatever it writes is exactly reality! If this point were not at stake, I would not spend my time responding to the managing director's quibbles.
