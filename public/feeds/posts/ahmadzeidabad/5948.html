There is no room left for denial! Khorasan newspaper, which usually supports the positions of Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, the Speaker of Parliament, in response to Masoud Pezeshkian's explicit references to the problems afflicting society and the country, wrote: "A large part of society, upon hearing these problems—especially from someone responsible for solving them—will surely feel bad about the future. This is exactly the outcome the enemy pursues in its media."
In reality, the story is precisely the opposite! When the president honestly and sincerely raises the problems of society, not only does a large part of society not feel bad, but they actually feel a bit of relief knowing that senior officials are aware of the multitude of problems in their lives and are informed of the dimensions of the difficulties!
The multitude of problems are too evident and tangible for the people to be ignored or covered up. Therefore, when an official tries to ignore or downplay the issues, society concludes that these people are trapped and imprisoned in their own closed bubble, unaware of what is happening at the societal level, and thus their comfort comes from a warm place!
For this reason, they feel extremely bad and bitter, thinking to themselves: When these people don't even know what situation we are in and how much we suffer from all these calamities, how can we expect them to solve any problem?
In contrast, when someone in the position of president acknowledges the problems, people think to themselves: Thank God they too know what is going on!
The truth is that the era of sweeping garbage under the rug is over. You can sweep one piece of garbage under the rug to hide it from guests, but a large number cannot be hidden. Just as the stench of accumulated trash seeps out from under the rug and exposes the homeowner's concealment, so too does the volume of society's big and small problems.
Those who, for whatever purpose, deny the volume of society's problems or downplay them do not create hope in people but rather fuel their anger and dissatisfaction.
When someone denies an object right before our eyes, the only feeling we get is that they are taking us for a fool! Human beings become provoked and angered when they are assumed to be fools.
