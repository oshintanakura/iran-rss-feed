The revolutionary current! Who exactly are the "revolutionary current" in the country, whose feelings and interests most officials are concerned about? Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, the Speaker of Parliament, has repeatedly emphasized that the hard core of power in the Islamic Republic is all 90 million Iranian citizens, but when it comes to responding to Hossein Shariatmadari's criticism, he introduces him as one of the "commanders of soft war" and "among the vanguard defenders of the Islamic Revolution"! The more I scrutinize the language of the media and official authorities, the more I conclude that, in their view, the "revolutionary current" refers to none other than those who share Mr. Shariatmadari's views and the supporters of the Front of Steadfastness! Well, what is the relationship of this current with the general public? If it is merely a subset of the people and has equal rights like other citizens, then on what basis are their sensitivities and interests preferred over other segments? And if it is opposed to the rest of the people, then what is that 90-million hard core of power that is being touted? That is, according to that sarcastic poem by Vahshi Bafqi, is it to be from the courtyard to the rooftop for the "revolutionary current," and from the rooftop to the Pleiades for the rest of the people? #احمد_زیدآبادی@ahmadzeidabad
