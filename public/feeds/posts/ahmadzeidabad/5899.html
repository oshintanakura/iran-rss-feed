Social Insecurity! Two nights ago, 21 stab wounds were inflicted on the head, neck, and body of my 55-year-old niece in my hometown. What saved her life truly resembles a miracle. For a long time, I have been warning about social insecurity and what weakens the role of the police in this regard. Using the police in an unusual role has caused society's distrust of this force, and this in turn has greatly diminished the legitimate authority of the police and its natural position as the protector of citizens' lives and property from any aggression. There is an endless amount of talk, words, and stories on this matter that I have written about before. Now, especially in the provinces and particularly in our city of Sirjan, the statistics of conflicts leading to murder and injury are beyond measure. My surgeon friends there are truly exhausted by the number of operations on the wounded. This is not the way to run a country! Citizens in many areas where they need help, support, and refuge have been left to their own devices, and instead, in matters where they should be left alone, they are controlled. The continuation of this situation leaves no security for anyone. The police must be a civil, powerful, healthy, and respected force so that it can both play its natural role in any circumstances and be appreciated in proportion to its services. In other words, the police must be removed from the circle of political conflicts, because regardless of citizens' views toward their government, people with any inclination, when their lives and property are threatened by others, have no choice but to resort to the police. I wish the opponents of the government would also understand this reality and not be happy about weakening the natural role of the police! #احمد_زیدآبادی @ahmadzeidabad
