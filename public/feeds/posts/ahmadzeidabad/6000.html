Mud-slinging? Sheikh Hamid Rasaei, protesting his ten-month prison sentence, wrote a letter of objection — and Tasnim News Agency labeled it "mud-slinging against a judicial ruling" and treated it as a crime! Setting aside any judgment about the form and content of Mr. Rasaei's sentence or whether his protest letter is accurate or not, the way Tasnim News Agency has inserted itself into this affair is wrong on every count. It's as if, in this news agency's view, every judicial ruling is inherently just — even sacred — and any criticism or objection to it amounts to mud-slinging and a crime! Every day, across the entire country, thousands of judicial rulings are issued by different judges, and one of the parties to the dispute considers it unjust and tyrannical and objects to it. Are all of them committing mud-slinging and a crime? The head of the judiciary, under Article 477 of the Criminal Procedure Code, identifies several judicial rulings each month as clearly contrary to Sharia and refers them to the Supreme Court for retrial. Should the head of the judiciary's determination that a judicial ruling clearly contradicts Sharia standards be considered mud-slinging against a judicial ruling and a criminal act? Judges are human too, and even if they care about fairness and justice, they may issue a wrong ruling for thousands of reasons. Meanwhile, some of them have no concern for justice at all and are mired in the very same corruption that afflicts other employees of various institutions! The clearest, most undeniable example is Saeed Mortazavi, who for a time held sway over people's lives, property, and reputation, and in the end the country's highest officials themselves branded him a liar and removed him from office. Unfortunately, during his tenure, the possibility of safe and free criticism of his rulings — by this very logic of Tasnim News Agency — did not exist, and this gave him such an ironclad immunity that he imagined himself all-powerful, and it ultimately ended in the Kahrizak disaster! And yet, someone worse than Saeed Mortazavi still exists, but those same false immunities prevent the country's community of legal experts from criticizing their rulings. In any case, the mindset of the likes of Tasnim News Agency unfortunately does nothing to help reform and improve the judiciary's performance, no matter who the other party may be. The purpose of this piece is not to judge the nature of Mr. Rasaei's sentence or to defend his actions, which in my view are mostly sensationalist and wrong. The aim is to defend his right to object to his sentence — a right that is natural and God-given, and that even current laws recognize for every single citizen, whatever their station, yet it seems some are trying to take it away. #احمد_زیدآبادی @ahmadzeidabad
