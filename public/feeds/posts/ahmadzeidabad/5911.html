In 1996, I interviewed Mohammad Javad Larijani when he was the head of the Parliament Research Center and a disciple of Sheikh Ali Akbar Nategh Nouri, in a building in Baharestan Square. The interview was supposed to be published in Hamshahri newspaper, but the newspaper's officials found it too challenging and set it aside. In that interview, I strongly challenged Mr. Larijani on the degree of democracy in the Islamic Republic system using the criterion that Karl Popper provides, and I put him in a tight spot! He spoke a eloquent chapter in denunciation of Popper, which I answered; to hell with Popper! You tell me, according to the criterion he proposed, how democratic is the Islamic Republic? In short, to accept a low level of my criticisms, he was forced to say; the members of the Guardian Council are not infallible! I said; did anyone even entertain the suspicion of their infallibility? I should not forget to mention that the transcribed text of that interview sat in the drawer of the newspaper office's desk for a long time until Saeed Laylaz became the head of a newly established section in Hamshahri called the interview section. One night when he was left empty-handed to fill the page and was searching desperately for content, I remembered the interview with Mr. Larijani and suggested publishing it to Mr. Laylaz! Happy and smiling, he took the interview text and printed it in Hamshahri over two issues, but the challenging and valuable parts of the interview were completely removed from the published text.
