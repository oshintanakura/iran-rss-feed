The Path of Return
Iranians abroad who see things differently from what is commonly called the "opposition" come under psychological and media pressure. Artists, in particular, are more exposed to such pressures and negative propaganda. Eventually, a time comes when they prefer returning to their homeland, despite all its problems, over enduring those conditions, and they set their sights on going back to their birthplace. With such a decision, the political atmosphere abroad intensifies against them, and they become the target of the darkest accusations. In fact, part of the opposition seeking the overthrow of the Islamic Republic, on the one hand, treats Iranian artists abroad with utter cruelty and intolerance at the slightest divergence from their own views, and on the other hand, blocks their path back to the homeland and out of that toxic environment with endless insults! Apparently, their goal is for these artists to remain in that foreign land and die of grief! Any artist or any intellectual who does not succumb to this atmosphere and chooses the path of return to their country should be congratulated and welcomed. Living in one's homeland is the natural and God-given right of every Iranian and has nothing to do with the government. Those who deny this right or tarnish its exercise are both cut from the same cloth, standing at the two extremes of the axis of villainy! #احمد_زیدآبادی @ahmadzeidabad
