A bitter and failed experience! The prosecutor of Shahriar has announced the arrest of one of the members of the Ferdowsiyeh City Council on charges of bribery! Hardly a day goes by without news like this—the arrest of a member or members of councils in various provinces of the country—being published in the media. City and village councils, which were supposed to pave the way for public participation in civic affairs and the cleansing of municipalities, have alarmingly turned into a playground for many to commit all kinds of financial corruption! Given the decline in political and social spheres, the phenomenon of city and village councils in its current form is a bitter and failed experience. Until a healthy, powerful, and free party system takes shape in the country, council elections not only fail to help improve the administration of the country's cities and villages, but, as has become evident, they add to the scope of corruption and misery. This is while the legal term of the current councils has expired more than a year ago, and the continued activity of the current councils and their elected mayors not only lacks any legal basis, but, given the worsening shortage of resources, increases the level and depth of abuse and corruption. Therefore, at least until the threat to the country is removed and the necessary stability is established, the government should prevent growing corruption in the administration of cities and villages by officially declaring the end of the legal term of the current councils and appointing new mayors by the Ministry of Interior.#Ahmad_Zeydabadi @ahmadzeidabad
