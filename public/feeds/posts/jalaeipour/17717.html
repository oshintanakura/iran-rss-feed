#Suggested_reading_for_Milad_night📌Read this 35-page article by Ali Shariati titled "The Face of Muhammad." 🔸According to Seyyed Ali Khamenei, Motahhari was so thrilled by reading this article that he read it three times and wept. It is the autumn of 1967. The Hoseyniyeh Ershad has just been launched with Morteza Motahhari as vice-chairman, and on the occasion of the fifteenth century of the Prophet's mission, he is seeking to publish a collection of articles about the life of the Messenger of God. It is in October 1967 that he raises the subject of the book with Ali Shariati (who has been teaching at Mashhad University for a year) in a letter. Shariati sends him two articles, one of which is this very "The Face of Muhammad." 🔸In this article, Shariati constructs three types—the Caesar, the Sage, and the Prophet—and undertakes a bold class analysis of the lives of the prophets, thereby comparing Islam with other religions. 🔸This article was among the first written texts that heralded the emergence of a new genius, laden with novel words and perspectives, among a crowd most of whom knew nothing but repeating concepts emptied of meaning.
