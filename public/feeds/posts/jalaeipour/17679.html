⭕️ In the current and complex issue of understanding/war and how to deal with the violation of understanding, which takes place in a chaotic and constantly changing context with numerous factors and considerations and high uncertainty, decisive and simple positions and answers are misleading. Fairness and the requirements of constructive dialogue based on the charitable interpretation of the other side's claims require that the parties accept that the matter is not a dispute between "peace-seeking" and "war-seeking"; in fact, the parties' disagreement is over the better path to achieving a "lasting end to war" and Iran's long-term security and flourishing. The answer is truly not simple. The aggregation of military, diplomatic, economic, and social considerations, relying on sometimes classified data, is also done in councils and institutions that are not publicly accessible. Recognizing the complexity of the issue and engaging in generative, problem-solving collective dialogue without labeling and without creating false dichotomies are prerequisites for discovering the better path. @jalaeipour
