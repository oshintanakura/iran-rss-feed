With Love and Without Love

"Duty without love becomes tedious; duty performed with love makes one steadfast. Responsibility without love makes one inconsiderate; responsibility borne with love makes one caring. Justice without love makes one hard-hearted; justice practiced with love makes one trustworthy. Upbringing without love becomes contentious; upbringing carried out with love makes one patient. Wisdom without love makes one cunning; wisdom exercised with love makes one understanding. Kindness without love becomes hypocritical; kindness shown with love makes one benevolent. Order without love leads to fault-finding; order upheld with love makes one open-minded. Knowledge and expertise without love make one self-righteous; knowledge and expertise applied with love make one trustworthy. Power without love makes one violent; power wielded with love makes one helpful. Dignity without love makes one arrogant; dignity upheld with love makes one humble. Wealth without love makes one miserly; wealth used with love makes one generous. Faith without love makes one fanatical; faith lived with love makes one peaceable."
