⭕️ Today, Friday, is the final day of "Tehran Art Month." "Tehran Art Month," an annual cultural event in Tehran organized with the stewardship of a number of Tehran's artists and galleries, is a successful example of a high-caliber collection of cultural events with low cost and leadership independent of the government. This year, during Art Month, more than 100 venues in Tehran hosted a series of programs, exhibitions, and artistic events. Among the several collections I managed to see, by far the highest quality was the "Rad Shahr" collection, with the admirable curation of Mehrouz Boladi at Khaneh Tehran (the Khaneh Ettehadiyeh Cultural Complex) in Lalezar, Ettehadiyeh Alley, which I recommend you not miss if you have the chance today. The historic 9,000-square-meter Ettehadiyeh House, which is an architectural treasure of Iran in the heart of Tehran, was fortunately purchased and revived by the municipality and restored by a competent team, and it is a very suitable space for the collection on display. @jalaeipour
