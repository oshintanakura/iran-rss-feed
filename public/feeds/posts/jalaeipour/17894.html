Saadi Pharmacy

One.
The owner of himself always grieves and melts.
We became the owner of a full-bodied kingdom and were freed.
(Saadi)
When we consider ourselves our own owner, we melt from grief. When we consider the kingdom and the owned to belong to that beautiful Unique One, we become free and liberated. "Inna lillah" means we belong to Him. We are not our own owners.

▫️Two.
Every foot that entered the house found a treasure;
No longer would all its life be at the market.
(Saadi)
There is a treasure that is found in the realm of the heart. One who finds the way to the inner treasure is freed from dependence on markets. Bayazid Bastami said about the meaning of dervishhood: "The one who, in the corner of his own heart, finds a foot entering a treasure..."

▫️Three.
One who became a stranger to wealth and greatness and lordship,
Is familiar to whomever he reaches.
(Saadi)
One who sees himself free from names and positions and passing powers can be familiar and close to everyone. If we are captivated by our own greatness and lordship and wealth, if we are bound by our position and name and reputation and intelligence and knowledge, we lose the felicity of "familiarity."

▫️Four.
Whoever does not know gratitude for today's blessing
Will eat regret upon the portion of tomorrow's mercy.
(Saadi)
One who does not appreciate the gifts of this world and does not welcome them with gratitude and joy will also be deprived of the mercy of the next world.

Whoever does not see today in the world the countenance of the Friend,
It is most likely that tomorrow he will have no vision.
(Saadi)
The beauty of nature and the movement and growth of the earth is a mercy of this world:
"Fa-unzur ila athar rahmatillah kayfa yuhyi al-arda ba'da mawtiha" (Surah Rum, verse 50)
"Look at these signs of Allah's kindness, how He revives the earth after its death." (Translation of Tafsir Kashf al-Asrar)

▫️Five.
Except the remembrance of the Friend, whatever you do is wasted life;
Except the secret of love, whatever you say is vanity.
(Saadi)
If life is not mixed with the remembrance of the Friend, and is not spent in longing for the Friend, it is wasted. It has passed in vanity:
Saadi, if he does not risk love, what shall he do with the kingdom of existence?
It is a pity that a whole life should go to waste.

The remembrance and recollection of the Friend has no end. It is not tiresome either. Because it is the food of the soul. The bread and water of the soul:
Our speech grew long and still remains,
For the remembrance of the Friend brings no weariness whatsoever.
(Saadi)
Therefore, his regret is for the times that passed unaware of the Friend:
Whatever we said other than the tale of the Friend,
In all our life we regret it.
(Saadi)
If there is any regret, it is nothing but this: why did we not breathe in His remembrance more? Why did we not throb in longing for Him more?
So that precious life does not end in regret,
All night Your remembrance was ongoing and repeated.

Speaking with Him, speaking of Him, and floating in the "tale of the Friend" is the meaning of life.
