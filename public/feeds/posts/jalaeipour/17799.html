42 self-sacrificing Iranian soldiers in the Aerospace Force were martyred on 16 Esfand in one of the missile tunnels near Garmdareh. Not under rubble or by a direct missile hit, but from lack of oxygen in the tunnel after enemy bombardment. Since I heard the news in Esfand, even the image of the oppressed suffocation of these brave men wounded my soul. I was waiting to see a photo of these valiant compatriots of mine and to take part in a national commemoration. But nothing happened. After several months, even now I have not seen anything except a few scattered photos of a few martyrs of this catastrophe and this same low-quality, poorly-made video on Fars News Agency, and the general Iranian public does not even know the name of one of these martyrs. Dear media people! Respected institutions in charge! All of us Iranians should have seen the portraits of these 42 martyrs. We should have heard the names of these heroes. This crime and the breathtaking glory of the bravery, courage, and life of these martyrs should have been the basis for all kinds of reports, documentaries, films, and impactful, high-quality texts. All of us Iranians are indebted to these brave children of Iran and their families. Alongside Minab and Lamerd and Dena and …, the name of the "Garmdareh" tunnel and the 42 martyrs of "Nafas" and their oppressedness and sacrifice must be immortalized in the national memory of Iranians.@jalaeipour
