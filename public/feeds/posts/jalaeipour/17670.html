A research report on the victims of Dey 1404 (December 2025-January 2026)
Karim Nikoonazar is one of the investigative journalists and narrative writers who works with data and documents, and his precision in collecting, selecting, arranging, and narrating them is rare. Nikoonazar, in collaboration with launching "Radio Tragedy" and publishing "The Tragedy Book," along with a group of journalists and writers, has delved into the lives of lesser-known figures or less-narrated aspects of the lives of well-known personalities. The stories in this collection are based on facts and are the result of journalistic research, and their topics range from political and social history to culture, cinema, and the lives of Iranian entrepreneurs. Mahmoud Afshar-Toos, Evance Ohanian, Arbāb Keykhosrow Shahrokh, Mohsen Azmaiesh, Moin al-Tojjar Bushehri, and Hassan Tafazoli are among those whose lives are narrated in this collection.
While the news atmosphere is influenced by irresponsible ideological journalism and foreign media projects, the presence of journalists like him is a blessing.
