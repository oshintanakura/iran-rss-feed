🔘"On Statesmanship" PublishedYale University Press has released "On Statesmanship" by Steven B. Smith. In this book, Smith explains that a statesman is something entirely different from a populist or a technocrat; he combines a set of leadership qualities—including the ability to persuade, courage, foresight, and sound judgment—to lead people where they need to go, without it even appearing that he is leading them. A true statesman focuses not on accumulating power, but on finding the best way to use power toward responsible ends. Most importantly, Smith argues that a statesman is a teacher; someone who, through language and argument, helps shape a nation's civic and political understanding.🆔 @mohammadreza_dadgostar
