📍The Speaker of Parliament, in a message, offered his condolences on the passing of Ayatollah Zainolabedin Ghorbani Lahiji, the former representative of the Guardian Jurist in Gilan Province.

In the Name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful

Indeed, we belong to God, and indeed, to Him we shall return.

📌The passing of the pious scholar, Ayatollah Zainolabedin Ghorbani Lahiji — the former representative of the Guardian Jurist in Gilan Province, the former Friday Prayer Imam of Rasht, the representative of the people of Astaneh-ye Ashrafiyeh in the second term of the Islamic Consultative Assembly, and the representative of the people of Gilan Province in several terms of the Assembly of Experts for Leadership — has caused sorrow and grief.

📌Throughout his blessed life, that precious scholar had an effective presence in the arena of political struggles before the Islamic Revolution, in the propagation and promotion of religious teachings, in legislative and cultural activities, and in the production of scholarly and religious works, and through his efforts in these arenas, he left behind lasting services on the path of elevating Islamic teachings and serving society.

📌I offer my condolences for this loss to his noble family, the seminaries, his students and devotees, and the noble people of Gilan Province, and I beseech the Exalted Court of God for vast divine mercy and forgiveness and elevated ranks for the deceased, and patience and reward for the bereaved.

Mohammad-Bagher Ghalibaf
Speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly
