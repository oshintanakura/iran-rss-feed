📍New details of the proposed mechanism by the Parliament Speaker for holding public plenary sessions
🔖 Mohammad-Saleh Jokar, Chairman of the Councils Commission, in a special news interview:
📌 The MPs sent a signed statement to the SNSC, and the Parliament Speaker also sent an official letter to the SNSC, requesting that the Parliament be allowed to reopen.
📌 Of course, this new mechanism has been designed in such a way that even if something happens, the quorum of MPs is not lost; because if the quorum is lost, the next election must definitely be held, and under the current circumstances, the quorum must not be disrupted. The quorum is two-thirds, which we must have in order to carry out legislative and oversight affairs, and currently only one-third are present in the public plenary.
