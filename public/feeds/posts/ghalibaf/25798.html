📍Message from the Speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly on the occasion of the birth anniversary of Hazrat Zainab (PBUH) and Nurses' Day

In the Name of God, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful

📌The auspicious birth of Hazrat Zainab Kubra (peace be upon her) — the epitome of patience and perseverance and the standard-bearer of loyalty in the most difficult arenas of divine trial — is a reminder of human glory that kept the lamp of hope alight amid suffering and affliction, and with faith and courage, etched an enduring epic of sacrifice and responsibility.

📌Nurses' Day, by the blessing of this auspicious birth, is an opportunity to honor those who, on the front line of defending people's lives and health — with kind hands, hearts full of faith, and steadfast will — bear the suffering of patients and become the refuge and hope of people in the most difficult moments of life.

📌The noble nurses of Islamic Iran are the inheritors of that same spirit that displayed brilliant manifestations of sacrifice and responsibility during the difficult days of the Sacred Defense and in times of crises and incidents. Those who at times silently and without fanfare forgo their own comfort and peace so that the pulse of life may beat in the souls of their fellow countrymen deserve the highest levels of honor and appreciation.

📌The Islamic Consultative Assembly also considers it its duty to pursue the concerns and legitimate demands of this devoted community and, by removing livelihood obstacles, to be the supporter and backing of the proud nursing community of the country.

📌I, while congratulating the auspicious birth of Hazrat Zainab Kubra (peace be upon her) and Nurses' Day to all nurses, sincerely appreciate their round-the-clock efforts and beseech the Almighty God for the health, dignity, and ever-increasing success of these dear ones.

Mohammad-Bagher Ghalibaf
Speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly
