🔖 Mohammad-Baqer Qalibaf, Speaker of Parliament, in his pre-agenda speech:

📌 Under such circumstances, the least and most obvious duty of us officials is the full and unconditional protection of people's livelihoods. In the provisions of the Seventh Development Plan law and budgetary obligations, we have laid the tracks for clear support mechanisms.

📌 To this end, with the approach of implementing the provisions related to the Seventh Plan law and in coordination with the honorable government, the executive roadmap for these reforms is being examined and pursued.

📌 In conclusion, it must be noted that this country, rich in material and human capacities, requires courageous decisions, timely action at sensitive moments, and the promotion of national solidarity and cohesion, so that — by the grace of God, and under the guidance of the Deputy of the Imam of the Age (may God hasten his reappearance) — we may pass through this historic pass with pride and victory.
