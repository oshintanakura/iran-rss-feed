📍Message from the Speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly on the martyrdom of the martyred Dr. Nusrat Eftekhari
The martyred Dr. Nusrat Eftekhari, in the most remote areas of Sistan and Baluchestan, pursued people's problems and untangled the knots in their lives | The perpetrators and those who ordered this crime, wherever they may be, will not escape the clutches of justice
In the Name of God, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful
📌The oppressed and cowardly martyrdom of the martyred Dr. Nusrat Eftekhari, Deputy for Cultural and Social Affairs of the Police Command of Sistan and Baluchestan Province, on the path of serving the people, is a bitter and sorrowful loss. A specialized, educated woman who rose from among the people and, instead of distancing herself from difficulties, went into the heart of deprivations and in the most remote and least-seen areas of the province, pursued the problems of the people, tribes and clans, as well as solidarity, trust-building, and untangling the knots in people's lives. This is a sign that sustainable security is rooted in deep connection with the people and untangling the knots in their lives.
📌This terrorist crime once again showed that the enemies of Iran's security and tranquility cannot tolerate servants who take steps to solve people's problems and strengthen social bonds. The perpetrators and those who ordered this crime, wherever they may be, are responsible before the pure blood of this martyred woman and the Iranian nation, and will not escape the clutches of justice. The responsible bodies must pursue this crime with all their power until the identification and decisive punishment of all its agents and those who ordered it.
📌The assassination of the people's servants is a sign of the enemy's strategic desperation in the face of the country's social capital, and aggression against this pure blood will carry a regrettable cost. Undoubtedly, the continuation of effective presence among the people, more serious attention to deprivations, and strengthening the unity and empathy of tribes and religions is the main path for continuing the way and manner of this martyred woman, and it shows that with these blind assassinations, neither will the lamp of service be extinguished nor will Iran's determination to safeguard the security and dignity of its people waver.
📌I, while offering my condolences and congratulations on the martyrdom of this striving woman to her esteemed family, comrades, colleagues, and the noble people of Sistan and Baluchestan and Islamic Iran, beseech from the presence of God Almighty elevated ranks for that exalted martyred woman and patience and tranquility for the bereaved.
Mohammad-Bagher Ghalibaf
Speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly
