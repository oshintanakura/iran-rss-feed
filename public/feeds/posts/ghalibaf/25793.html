📍Message from the Speaker of Parliament following the assassination of IRGC martyr Afsaneh Aghila

📌The innocent martyrdom of IRGC martyr Afsaneh Aghila, commander of the Women's Basij District of Mehrestan County, at the hands of criminal terrorists who violated the sanctity of her home and family and turned their weapons on a woman defending security and serving the people, has once again laid bare the ugly face of terrorism and its enmity toward the security and tranquility of the Iranian people.

📌The pure blood of martyr Afsaneh Aghila, like the blood of other martyrs of this land, bears witness to the Iranian nation's unwavering resolve to defend the country's security, dignity, and independence. I am confident that the path of this devoted woman will be carried forward by the will of the nation and the efforts of the children of this land, and that the perpetrators of this crime will be held accountable before the law and justice.

📌I offer my congratulations and condolences on the glorious martyrdom of this faithful and devoted woman to the Supreme Leader of the Islamic Revolution, to the esteemed family of this martyr, and to the noble and valiant people of Sistan and Baluchestan, and I beseech God Almighty to elevate the ranks of that exalted martyr and to grant patience and reward to the bereaved.

Mohammad-Bagher Ghalibaf
Speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly
