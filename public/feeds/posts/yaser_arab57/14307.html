1. Truly, the conditions inside the government and the issue of persons with disabilities and the situation of experts are very intertwined, lacking precise understanding, necessary executive backing, destructive parallel work, etc., such that I doubt the autism issue will be solved in a process-oriented and problem-based manner in the country anytime soon.
2. Ultimately, a National Autism Organization must be created, which neither the government will accept nor the Planning and Budget Organization.
