Work? Get it to the Social and Cultural Deputy of Tehran Municipality. Hello and without preamble! We are facing a serious issue in the country called autism and other intellectual and developmental disabilities. An issue that has no single, clear authority and is scattered among various bodies, from education and welfare to other institutions. The result of this scattering, for many families, is nothing but placelessness and lack of planning for their children; children who have no place to be present, play, communicate, and participate socially. In the meantime, neighborhood houses can be one of the closest and most accessible urban capacities for these families. However, families have gone to neighborhood houses, given their numbers, heard promises of a call, and then heard nothing. Sometimes the neighborhood house says: "Autism is specialized and not our job." The answer is that the neighborhood house is not supposed to be a therapist. It is enough for it to be an accessible public space for the neighborhood's families. Sometimes it says: "This issue is not defined in our program." The answer is: isn't the neighborhood house meant to deal with the real problems of the neighborhood's people? Sometimes it says: "Autism is the job of the Welfare Organization." The answer is that an autistic child, before being a file of any institution, is a member of this very neighborhood. And when this close and accessible door is also closed to the family, the result is not just an unanswered call; the family returns to the same wandering and loneliness as before, and the child loses another possibility of being in society. Hasn't the time come for the Social and Cultural Deputy of Tehran Municipality to take the role of neighborhood houses seriously in dealing with this issue? @yaser_arab57
