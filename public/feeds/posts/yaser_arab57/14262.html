An 11-year-old girl who lost her father in childhood lives with her mother and two school-age brothers in a home of less than 20 square meters. Her mother works in a sewing workshop to cover living expenses. On the eve of the new school year, her supplies for going to school were just a few pages from old drawing notebooks, some half-used pencils, and a blue pen... When we asked her, "What bag will you take to school this year?" she brought out a bag that had been donated to her in previous years; a bag whose torn parts her mother had repaired many times so her daughter could use the same bag for one more year. For some children, the start of the school year comes with buying a bag, notebooks, and pencils; but for this girl, even these few simple items are a dream.
Let's make the start of this school year sweeter for her and several other underprivileged students with a new bag and a few simple supplies.
The cost of each package for one student is 2,500,000 Toman.
