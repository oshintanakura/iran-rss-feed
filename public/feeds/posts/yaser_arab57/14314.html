A suggestion for teachers and educators of children and adolescents: One of the most important skills and lessons that should be introduced in our educational system is rhetoric, oratory—or what we might call the art of presentation. Our children should be able to present any speech, data, or content in a completely eloquent, fluent, straightforward, engaging, and concise manner. The essay-writing class in our time was perhaps a minimal exercise in this very area, but it carried no weight and was treated as a recreational sideshow! Today, presentation skills are a key skill worldwide, and many institutions and companies have their own brand ambassadors or specialized presenters. Now that this skill is not part of the educational curriculum, we ourselves can practice these things and, alongside conversations and class materials, carry out exercises practically and jointly with ourselves and our students. The largest and most difficult meetings in our country typically fail to reach a conclusion precisely because of this problem and lack of capability. That is not the whole problem, but it is a significant part of it.
