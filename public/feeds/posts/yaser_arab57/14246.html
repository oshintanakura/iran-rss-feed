What is presented here is the #KareKheir report for Mordad 1405, and it reaches you, dear ones, with the aim of letting you know how and where the funds you entrust to #KareKheir with various intentions have been spent. In the past month, the total amount spent in this regard was 240,562,397.5 Toman, which has grown by about 2 billion Toman compared to the previous month. Also, this month saw 3,063 recorded instances of assistance, which is about 350 more than the previous month. Furthermore, in Mordad, 9,732 deposits were made to the introduced accounts, which is about 600 more deposits than the previous month. In Mordad, the matter of school supplies was a priority, and we were able to secure and deposit half of the required budget for procuring these items; naturally, our priority in Shahrivar will be to secure the remaining half. Additionally, we owe about 500 million Toman for the reconstruction of 18 schools damaged by the war, which we must also settle in the coming month. The details of the actions and activities are presented in the attached charts, broken down accordingly. I am grateful to each and every one of you, dear ones, who have kept the light of this path burning. With respect.
