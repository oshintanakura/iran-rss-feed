Buying a mobile library car: Delivering more stories to more people 5,000 shares of 500,000 tomans, for every person. We are an NGO called Choli Qazak. Choli Qazak is the name of the rain-seeking doll in Khorasan. We deliver books, poetry, games, theater, words, and stories to 15 villages in the city of Qaen in South Khorasan province and 400 children, and this work has become our life's mission. We've been walking this path for several years, and we had a Mazda double-cab vehicle on loan that we used to travel to various villages, and a while ago we returned it to its owner. But we continue this path, whether by vehicle or on foot! Currently, we reach the children through various methods; for example, by taxi, motorcycle, or our friends' cars. Now we've decided, for ease and better access to the children of Iran, with your accompaniment and through crowdfunding, to buy a car and equip it so we can load up people, stories, theater, books, games, and whatever is needed, and go anywhere without geographical limits—not just Qaen, not just Khorasan, but anywhere on this small planet. If 5,000 people each pay a 500,000-toman share, this car will be purchased, and more people will benefit from the land of Choli Qazak.
