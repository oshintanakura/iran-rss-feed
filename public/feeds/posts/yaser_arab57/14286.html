From a bad school to a good school!
✍ Yaser Arab
✅ We all know that teachers' livelihoods are not good. The gap between many teachers' salaries and real living costs and the poverty line is serious and worrying.
✅ The protests, campaigns, and demands being made by teachers these days are understandable, and the demand for a dignified life is undoubtedly their indisputable right.
✅ But the issue of education is not just teachers' livelihoods. Even if all teachers' salaries were increased tomorrow, big questions about schools and education in Iran would still remain.
✅ What should be done with the content of textbooks? What exactly should our children learn, and what do they not learn? What new training and skills do teachers need to face today's world? How is the school managed, and to what extent does our school management structure align with the needs of today's child, family, and society? What is the student's share in school? What is the family's share? And what world is the school supposed to prepare the student for? What is the idea on the ground, and what is the possibility of movement under the current conditions of blockage?
✅ These are issues whose answers cannot be found solely in the government's annual budget. The government may have more or less money. Teachers' salaries may increase or not. But many fundamental issues of education will remain in place. The quality of education, educational content, teacher training and empowerment, school structure, and the relationship between school and children's real lives are issues we must think about independently of the perennial fight over the budget.
✅ This Thursday, September 10 (19 Shahrivar), alongside the demand for "a better livelihood for teachers," we will raise another question more seriously: How can we go from a bad school to a good school?
✅ For this reason, we have decided to hold a meeting with the presence of teachers and those concerned about education; a meeting to listen!
✅ We want to sit together, see the issues more precisely, hear experiences, and think about ways that might improve the state of education in Iran by one step.
✅ We invite all teachers, researchers, education activists, school principals, students, and all those concerned about education to participate in this conversation.
Time: Thursday, 19 Shahrivar 1405 (September 10, 2026), at 4:30 PM
Location: Palestine Street, Italia Street, No. 36, Ensaaf News Base. We look forward to seeing you.
