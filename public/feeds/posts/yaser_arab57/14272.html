Subject: National demand for transparency, enforcement of rights, and real support for people with disabilities and autism
We, the signatories of this campaign, a group of people with disabilities, individuals with autism, their families and caregivers, mothers who are heads of households, and citizens who support the rights of people with disabilities, call for a transparent, expert, and documented review of the performance of the State Welfare Organization of Iran in the field of disability and autism, and the full implementation of the legal rights of the target community.
The purpose of this campaign is not to undermine or accuse anyone. We seek transparency, accountability, enforcement of the law, fair support, and performance review based on real results.
