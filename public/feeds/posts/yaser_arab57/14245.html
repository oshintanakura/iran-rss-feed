For a society where the #energy_imbalance has reached a life-or-death threshold, dialogue about any plan related to changing gasoline and energy policy is vital. A conversation with economist Dr. #Mohsen_Yazdanpanah, published on the IranTalk media platform under the title "Gasoline Reforms: Without Fireworks," was an attempt to open the door to dialogue about a plan some in the government are pursuing, critique it, and highlight its consequences.Watching "Gasoline Reforms: Without Fireworks" — any plan can be critiqued, including the current government's plan; and any critique can also be critiqued, including those Mohsen Yazdanpanah levels against the current plan. What matters is that for any chronic and complex issue — such as energy in today's Iran — there is no better path than policy transparency, social dialogue, and building social and political consensus around the best solutions. All those interested in energy policy discussions and debates on price and non-price reforms in gasoline and other energy carriers can be invited to watch this conversation and engage in the social debate about it.
