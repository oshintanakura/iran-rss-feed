First of Mehr Festival Held in Memory of Minab's Student Martyrs

Coinciding with the start of the new school year, the Municipality's First of Mehr Festival was held in Tehran schools, centered on honoring the memory of the student martyrs of Shajareh Tayyebeh School in Minab.

The event aimed to honor the memory of the student martyrs of Shajareh Tayyebeh School in Minab and to tie the start of the school year to the culture of sacrifice and martyrdom — children whose absence is felt on this year's first day of school in the classrooms of their friends and classmates.
