Tehran's Milad Tower has been renamed
Per a decree issued by Alireza Zakani, Tehran's Milad Tower was renamed Milad Tehran Tourism and Recreation Company.
In the Tehran mayor's decree, organizing and developing urban tourism, showcasing the capital's historical, cultural, tourism, and recreational capacities, and revitalizing and making optimal use of cultural and historical heritage were set out as the company's missions. Abbas Heydari, who had previously served as CEO of Tehran's Milad Tower, was also appointed CEO of Milad Tehran Tourism and Recreation Company under this decree.
