Yaminpour's account of his trip to Lebanon on the anniversary of Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah's martyrdom

After the Ramadan war and the martyrdom of His Eminence, this is the first time I have come to Lebanon. The enemies have done everything in their power to make travel between Iran and Lebanon difficult. They have dug pits in our path; they have built walls between us. In their foolish imagination, they have laid siege to us.

First they banned direct flights, then they added the visa obstacle, they declared our ambassador a persona non grata, and now they are even canceling connecting flights... But the bond between the two nations of the Resistance is a divine blessing. We share the same pain, the same tears... the same destiny.

I haven't seen my Lebanese friends for months. They are eager to hear news from Iran. The image of the nightly gatherings has been exciting for them.

Dr. Mohammad is a member of Hezbollah. He says, I think the people of Iran have grown a great deal from the time of the Imam's passing to the time of His Eminence's martyrdom. Is that right?

I confirm it.

He says this next to the grave of Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah and points to a sturdy man who was Sayyed's bodyguard. He says this sturdy man underwent open-heart surgery after Sayyed's martyrdom. And I remember the dawn of the 10th of Esfand, when I was struggling between life and death
