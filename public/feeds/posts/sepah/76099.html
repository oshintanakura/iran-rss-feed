🔴 Handwritten note from the Supreme Leader addressed to Sardar Seyyed Majid Mousavi, commander of the IRGC Aerospace Force

My mujahid and brave brother, His Excellency Sardar Seyyed Majid Mousavi, may God preserve him and grant him support. After greetings and peace;

1) Praise be to God, the report presented — a copy of which I had also received previously — is heartening and pleasing. You have written very little about your own struggles and those of your anonymous and oppressed comrades; such has always been the way of the sincere. But the effect that, by divine wisdom, arises from this characteristic is, God willing, the love and esteem that the Almighty Lord, glorified and exalted, places for the possessors of sincerity, and various kinds of blessings and victories.

2) Regarding the supply chain, God willing, let the efforts continue and let reports on it be reflected to me regularly.

3) Safeguarding your own precious life and that of all the brothers is my emphatic request. It is hoped that, with the blessed and abundant prayers of our master, may God hasten his relief, affairs will be set in order.
