Amirkabir Petrochemical Overcomes Production Restrictions

The CEO of Amirkabir Petrochemical announced the improvement of the company's production, sales, and financial indicators after overcoming the restrictions of the early months of the year 1405, saying: The company's production and commercial activities have been on an upward trajectory in recent months.

Hesam Khoshbinfar, referring to the shutdown caused by the American-Zionist enemy's aerial attack and the limited supply of certain ancillary services and electricity in the early months of the year, stated: With the easing of wartime conditions and the return of relative stability, employees' efforts to maintain and enhance operational capacity have continued. According to the report published on the Comprehensive Publishers System (Codal), the company's production in Mordad reached 29,880 tons, with total five-month production reaching 113,749 tons; Mordad sales were also recorded at 25,178 tons, with total five-month sales at 107,763 tons.

A board member of Amirkabir Petrochemical Company announced the sales value for Mordad at 37,078 billion and 150 million rials and total five-month sales at 151,339 billion and 979 million rials, adding: During this period, domestic sales of heavy polyethylene grew by 50 percent and light polyethylene by 100 percent compared to the same period last year, reaching the highest recorded sales figures. Khoshbinfar emphasized that the continuation of this trend depends on stable operational conditions, sustainable supply of feedstock, ancillary services and electricity, and a focus on productivity and removing production-related restrictions.
