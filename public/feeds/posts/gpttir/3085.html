🔰 Where does the strategy of unity of fronts stand in the new regional environment?

👤 Mohammad Amin Hozrati, senior researcher at the Center for Elite Diplomacy of the Sharif Governance Think Tank, assessed the conceptual and functional dimensions of the "unity of fronts" strategy at a meeting of the Association of Political Analysts and Experts of Lebanon:

🔹 A regional deterrence strategy requires a precise distinction between the two concepts of "unity of fronts" and "unity of war." Strategic interconnectedness among the axes of resistance is a source of power; however, the automaticity of intervention turns strategic ambiguity into a predictable pattern for the enemy.

🔸 The security challenges facing the Axis of Resistance in the post-Al-Aqsa Storm era necessitate a fundamental transition from the logic of "accumulation of power" to "flexibility of power."

🔹 Communications security, the distribution of decision-making centers, decentralization at the tactical level (while maintaining unity of strategic decision-making), and protecting the command structure against strikes are essential conditions for restoring previous deterrence.
