🔰IRIB and the Mission of Developing the Comprehensive Audio and Video Market in the New Era

👤Seyed Mohammadreza Dadgostar, Director of the Regulation Group at the Sharif Governance Think Tank, has written a note in Farhikhtegan as part of a special feature titled "Politics in the Jame-Jahannama":

🔹The Iranian regulator suffers from the affliction of viewing the development of the sector it regulates as a threat to its own authority and exercise of governance. The growth of businesses and the increase in their customer base is not good news for this regulator, as it signifies the expansion of those companies' sphere of influence and power. This challenge is felt even more acutely in the case of comprehensive audio and video media, because the regulator is a subsidiary of an institution that directly engages in content production and distribution and is itself a major player in this market.

🔸Promoting competition policy in the domestic comprehensive audio and video market can take various approaches. Supporting local cultures lays the groundwork for developing the VOD market with the aim of strengthening regional hubs, which also helps leverage local talent and create jobs.
