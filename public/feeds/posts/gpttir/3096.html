🔰Economic Literacy: A Mission for the National Media

👤Amirhossein Gholipour, a researcher in the Economy Group, has contributed a note to Farhikhtegan as part of a special feature titled "Politics in the Mirror":

🔹Beyond news, entertainment, and coverage of daily controversies, the national media can see itself as an institution that grows society's stock of knowledge—a stock that cannot be summoned overnight when an economic crisis hits, energy prices are reformed, a currency decision is made, or the pension system is overhauled.

🔸Perhaps the measure of success for such an approach is precisely this: not that the audience necessarily ends up agreeing with the government once the program is over, but that they can say, "Now I have a better grasp of what the problem is, who bears the costs, who stands to gain, and what consequences the options before the country would have." If the national media can make that sentence part of the everyday experience of millions of Iranians, then raising awareness will no longer be a mere byproduct of the media; it will become one of its most vital public services and one of the foundations of reform and better governance.
