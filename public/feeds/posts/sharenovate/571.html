The Ashura of "Oppressed Iran"!
Ashura and the mourning of #Moharram are not merely a collective religious ritual; they are also the reaction and narrative of Iranian society about itself in every era. The reinterpretation of Ashura differs according to the characteristics of each time, and this affects its rituals.
It seems that the observance of Ashura rituals over the past 50 years has followed four completely different patterns:
1- Revolutionary Ashura (from the 1950s to 1979)
2- War Ashura (the 1980s)
3- Carnivalesque Ashura (the 2000s and 2010s)
4- The Ashura of Oppressed Iran (the 2020s)
