🖋The Many Kinds of Artificiality of an Intelligence🧠
Every new product, one step to life, one step to death!
🔸Amir Nazemi, a researcher, has written a note for "Shargh Daily" about the development of artificial intelligence by major tech companies, noting that developments in this field seem like an exciting movie to the public, but for tech and digital economy activists, it has become a lightning-fast (blitz) game of chess.
🔸In the rest of this note, he introduces Grok, the AI developed by X, and Helix, introduced by Figure AI, noting that these two AIs entered the scene a few weeks after all the hype around DeepSeek.
🔸In the final section of this note, he also addresses the challenges of AI development for Iranian startups. While enumerating these challenges, he believes that actors in the country's startup ecosystem should take future scenarios in AI development more seriously.
🔸In this regard, Nazemi has written: "The fact that the strategy of most actors in an ecosystem suffers from a strategic mistake indicates deeper roots of a wrong orientation in a society. Something I consider to be disconnection from the world, due to sanctions and filtering. But the main pain is that with the emergence of each product, the chance of success for Iranian startups decreases, even though they themselves are thrilled by these new products."
