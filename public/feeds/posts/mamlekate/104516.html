📝 Faculty members of Shiraz University to strike starting September 28
The Faculty Syndicate Council of Shiraz University announced in a statement the suspension of all educational, research, and administrative activities at the university from Saturday, September 28 until further notice.
According to the Faculty Syndicate Council of Shiraz University, this decision was made after repeated follow-ups regarding the legal rulings and salaries of faculty members and the failure to resolve the related problems. The council has announced that the Organization for Planning and Budget has no intention of resolving these issues, and the suspension of activities is being carried out in accordance with the resolution of the general meeting held in Khordad.
The Syndicate Council of Shiraz University has also declared its support for the statement by the Iranian University Professors' Syndicate Association regarding the suspension of university activities from the start of the new semester, and has called on the university's board of directors to honor previous agreements.
