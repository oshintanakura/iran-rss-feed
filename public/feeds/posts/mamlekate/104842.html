📝 Ahvaz Metro Tremors Threaten Achaemenid and Sassanid Archaeological Layers

Across the Iranian plateau, thousands of ancient and historical sites are scattered, each one a part of the history and civilization of this land. At some of these sites, different historical layers have formed on top of one another, becoming the earliest cores around which today's metropolises took shape. Ahvaz is one such metropolis — a city whose historical and civilizational record stretches back centuries before Islam. Despite this significance, the city's ancient heritage has been at risk of destruction over the past two decades because of metro excavations.

The project, which had been suspended since 1394 owing to financial problems and disputes between the municipality and the contractor, is now set to restart with a new contractor along the same route as before. Yet archaeologists had repeatedly warned that this area is likely linked to the ancient cities of Taryana and Hormoz Ardashir, and the artifacts discovered during metro excavation in these areas only strengthen that likelihood. But as the evidence suggests, city officials intend to resume the project without taking these warnings into account.
