📝 UK warns about Iranian malware used to spy on dissidents and journalists

The UK's National Cyber Security Centre, in a joint report with the US Federal Bureau of Investigation and the Netherlands' General Intelligence and Security Service, warned about malware that these agencies say Iranian state cyber actors have been using since at least 2025 to target individuals in various countries, including the UK, the US, and the Netherlands.

The malware, named "CHOSEN BRICK," can gather information about targets' contacts, emails, and social media messages — information that could also be used to track their movements and activities.
