📝 Pezeshkian's meeting in New York, attended by Robert Malley, Trita Parsi, Negar Mortazavi, and Farnaz Fassihi

Masoud Pezeshkian held a meeting on the evening of Thursday, the second of Mehr, at the residence of the Islamic Republic's delegation in New York with a number of figures active in American media and think tanks.

According to images released from the meeting, Robert Malley, the former U.S. special envoy for Iran, Trita Parsi, founder of NIAC, and Farnaz Fassihi and Negar Mortazavi, Iranian-American journalists, were among those present.

This meeting with media and think tank figures is held on the sidelines of their trips to the annual United Nations General Assembly.

The Wall Street Journal had reported before this year's trip by Pezeshkian that he was seeking dialogue with analysts from American and European think tanks.

The newspaper wrote that some of those invited to meet with the Islamic Republic's delegation had reservations due to restrictions stemming from U.S. sanctions on informal talks with Tehran officials.

An expert on Iran affairs who had previously received an invitation told the newspaper: "No one wants to be seen going there."
