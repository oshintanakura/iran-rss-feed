According to a US official and another informed source, Marco Rubio, the US Secretary of State, on Monday, after negotiations reached an impasse, asked the delegation of the Islamic Republic of Iran at the United Nations General Assembly to leave the country immediately.

Why does this matter?
Although members of the Iranian delegation were expected to leave American soil soon, Rubio's move is seen as a harsh and unusual diplomatic reaction and, amid futile efforts to end the conflicts, lays bare the depth of mistrust between the two countries.

Among those ordered to leave New York was Abbas Araghchi, Iran's Foreign Minister and Rubio's counterpart.

What happened behind the scenes?
On Monday morning, the White House hoped that progress would be made in the negotiations with Tehran later in the day; however, by late afternoon it became clear that the negotiations had reached an impasse.
