📝 Siavash Jamshidi Kheirabadi, one of those detained in the Dey protests, was executed. Mizan News Agency, affiliated with Iran's judiciary, reported on Saturday, 11 Mehr, the execution of Siavash Jamshidi Kheirabadi, one of those detained in the Dey 1404 protests. In its announcement, this outlet reported his "repeated presence" in the Dey 1404 protests and stated his charge as "waging war through drawing a firearm and firing it, and assembly and collusion against the country's security." The judiciary's news agency claimed that Mr. Jamshidi had a "criminal record" and that on 18 Dey of last year on Naser Khosrow Street in Shahrekord, he fired at officers and then fled, but was arrested two days later. In the judiciary's announcement, it is stated that the execution sentence of this protester was carried out after being confirmed by the Supreme Court. However, the announcement provided no explanation about the time of the court hearing, the judicial process, or his access to a lawyer of his choosing. The Islamic Republic has so far officially announced the execution of more than 30 detainees from the Dey protests and has also issued execution sentences for dozens of other imprisoned protesters.@mamlekate
