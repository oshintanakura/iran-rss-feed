🔰 Associated Press: Cost of Iran war for American households has reached about $2,000

🔸In an October 17, 2026 report, the Associated Press wrote that more than seven months after the start of the US-Iran war, rising costs of fuel, air travel, and freight have placed further strain on American households.

🔸Mark Zandi, chief economist at Moody's Analytics, estimates the war's cost to each American household at an average of nearly $2,000: about $1,100 in higher energy costs, $540 in increased interest costs, and $360 in military costs.

🔸According to American Automobile Association figures, the average price of gasoline reached $4.33 per gallon on October 16 — about 45 percent higher than pre-war levels. Diesel prices also rose 68 percent, to $6.33.

🔸Higher jet fuel costs have pushed up airline ticket prices as well; tickets in September were about 22 percent more expensive than the same period last year. The cost of transporting goods by road and sea has also risen.
