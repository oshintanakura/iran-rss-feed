🔰Second Anniversary of Yahya Sinwar's Martyrdom

🔸October 16, 2026, marks the second anniversary of the martyrdom of Yahya Sinwar, known as "Abu Ibrahim," former head of the political bureau of the Hamas movement — a figure whose life was intertwined with captivity and the political and military developments of Gaza.

🔸Sinwar was born in 1962 in the Khan Younis camp, into a family displaced from the city of Al-Majdal. The experience of living in the camp and the aftermath of the Nakba formed the social context for the shaping of his political outlook. He studied Arabic language at the Islamic University of Gaza and expanded his political activity in the student environment — a path that was accompanied by repeated arrests at the hands of the occupation forces.

🔸His longest period of captivity began in 1988 and continued until his release in 2011. In prison, Sinwar became one of the organizational officials of Hamas prisoners, learned Hebrew, and followed developments in the society and politics of the Zionist regime. His release in the "Wafa al-Ahrar" agreement, which led to the release of 1,027 Palestinian prisoners in exchange for the Israeli soldier "Gilad Shalit," brought the issue of prisoners more prominently into his political life.

🔸In 2017, he assumed the leadership of Hamas in the Gaza Strip and was elected for another term in 2021. After the assassination of the martyr Ismail Haniyeh in Tehran, Hamas announced on August 6, 2024, the election of Sinwar as head of the movement's political bureau — an election in the midst of war that entrusted the responsibility of political leadership of the movement to the leadership based in Gaza.

🔸Sinwar was martyred on October 16, 2024, during a clash with the forces of the occupation army in Tel al-Sultan, Rafah. The army of the Zionist regime announced his martyrdom the following day, and Hamas confirmed his martyrdom on October 18. According to published reports, the forces involved were initially unaware of his identity, and identification was carried out after the clash.

🔸Images of the final moments of the martyr Sinwar in a destroyed building in Tel al-Sultan became one of the most enduring scenes of the Gaza war — images that showed him wounded and throwing a piece of wood toward an Israeli drone.
