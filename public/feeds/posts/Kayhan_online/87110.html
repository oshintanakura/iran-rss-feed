🔰Iran's preliminary agreements with Pakistan to reopen the Rimdan border

🔸Following problems in the export process through the Rimdan border and changes announced by the Pakistani side in tariff and banking conditions, consultations began to remove trade obstacles and return exports to normal conditions.

🔸In this regard, consultations have been held with officials of Pakistan's Gabd Customs to review the existing issues and obstacles and pave the way for facilitating the export of goods. Negotiations to reach practical solutions are still ongoing.

🔸At the insistence of the Governor of Sistan and Baluchestan, efforts to follow up on the problems that have arisen along the export route through this border and to find solutions for facilitating the movement of goods have been placed on the agenda.

🔸In light of the preliminary agreements with the Pakistani side, it was decided that, given the limited shelf life of perishable products and the risk of losses for traders and exporters, shipments of vegetables and other perishable goods should be given priority in processing.
