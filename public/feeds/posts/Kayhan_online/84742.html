🔰 Documenting the Lives of 500 Martyrs on Radio

🔸On the eve of commemorating Sacred Defense Week, a ceremony was held to unveil the 200-episode radio documentary series "Yaqut," produced by the Health Radio Network.

🔸According to a Kayhan reporter, Ahmad Pahlavanian, Deputy of Voice of the Islamic Republic of Iran, spoke at the ceremony, honoring the exalted status of the families of martyrs: "So far, the lives of more than 500 exalted martyrs have been documented and recorded on radio, and this path continues with strength."

🔸Paying tribute to the radio martyrs of the "Ramadan War" — martyrs Sadra Redaei and Farzad Mahdavi from Radio Namayesh — he emphasized: "Narrating the lives of martyrs in series such as 'Yaqut' is not merely a retelling of past memories, but an opportunity to come to know human beings who made great choices at critical moments. The national media has a duty to revisit these true role models for today's and future generations through accurate and artistic narration."

🔸Hojjat al-Islam Seyyed Ramazan Mousavimoghaddam, representative of the Supreme Leader in the Foundation of Martyrs and Veterans Affairs, also expressed appreciation for the national media's work in finding subjects and producing magnificent documentaries, saying: "Many untold dimensions of the martyrs' sacrifice remain that must be explained. The martyrs, by giving their lives, gifted health of spirit and body to society, and from this perspective, all martyrs are 'health martyrs.'" In continuation of this ceremony, Vahid Jalili, Cultural Deputy of the Head of the Islamic Republic of Iran Broadcasting, stating that narrating the martyrs is in truth a manifestation of the remembrance of God, added: "Keeping alive the memory of the martyrs injects true vitality and hope into society, because the path of the martyrs is free of fear and discouragement."

🔸At the end of this ceremony, while honoring the Bahanar movement (child of the precious martyr Ayatollah Mohammad Javad Bahanar), the families of the exalted martyrs of radio and the production crew of the documentary, the collection of 200 radio documentaries "Yaqut" was unveiled.
