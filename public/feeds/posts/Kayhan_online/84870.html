🔰 Iran Cultural Week to Be Held in Four Russian Cities

🔸The Deputy for Scientific and Cultural Cooperation at the Organization of Culture and Islamic Communication announced that the Cultural Week of the Islamic Republic of Iran will be held in four cities — Moscow, Saint Petersburg, Kazan, and Astrakhan — featuring 13 cultural, artistic, scientific, and specialized programs.

🔸Hossein Divsalar, noting the scope of the event, said: This edition of Iran Cultural Week has been designed to present a comprehensive picture of "historical Iran, cultural Iran, and contemporary Iran," and efforts have been made to introduce not only the country's civilizational and cultural heritage but also Iran's present-day capacities in art, knowledge, technology, and cultural products to Russian audiences. He described the geographical reach and diversity of the programs as hallmarks of this edition, adding: In addition to Moscow, programs will be held in Saint Petersburg, Kazan, and Astrakhan. A performance by the Iranian National Music Orchestra at the historic Bolshoi Hall in Moscow, a film and animation week, special programs for children and teenagers, scientific and academic sessions, and a large cultural and artistic exhibition are among the planned events.

🔸The Deputy of the Organization of Culture and Islamic Communication also announced a special focus on creative industries at this event, saying: Areas such as cinema and animation, toys, computer games, and fashion and apparel have been given attention in this edition, and alongside product showcases, specialized sessions and trade negotiations between Iranian and Russian professionals will be held to pave the way for joint cooperation and the entry of Iranian creative products and services into the Russian market. Divsalar, emphasizing that Iran Cultural Week should not be limited to a series of one-off programs, stated: One of the main goals of this event is to build lasting connections among artists, academics, cultural figures, and professional organizations of the two countries.

🔸To that end, the negotiations and connections established during the Cultural Week will be followed up after the programs conclude, in order to lay the groundwork for joint projects and the continuation of cultural cooperation between Iran and Russia.
