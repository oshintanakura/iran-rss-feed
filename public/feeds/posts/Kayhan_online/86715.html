📹The story behind the idea of making life expensive in Tehran during the reformist era🔸Alireza Zakani: The idea I raised in the government during Martyr Raisi's administration was that in the 70s and especially the second half of the 90s, an approach of making Tehran expensive prevailed as a way to solve problems like air pollution. Based on this class-based view, Tehran had to become so expensive that not just anyone could afford to live there.🔸On the contrary, we believed that making it expensive not only failed to solve Tehran's problems, but also pushed people to the city's outskirts and, by turning Tehran into the benchmark for the rest of the country, spread high prices across the entire nation—while Tehran's problems remained as they were. This approach was a one-dimensional and unjust way of looking at the issue.🔸Our proposal was to make Tehran cheaper and extend that affordability to the whole country through the provision of services. On that basis, a 7-member working group was formed under Mr. Mokhber's supervision, and the matter reached the implementation stage.
