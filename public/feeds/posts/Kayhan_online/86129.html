A theater director warned 🔰 The danger of vulgar plays for theater

🔸 A playwright and theater director in Khuzestan province believes that using vulgar and immoral situations and behaviors to attract audiences in theater will gradually cause the true audience of performing arts to turn away!

🔸 "Mostafa Bouazar," a performing arts activist in Khuzestan province, told a Kayhan reporter about the current state of theater in the country: Iranian theater, due to the wrong track-laying and policymaking of some officials in this field in recent decades, has distanced itself from its mission, which is the production of thought and intellect, and has moved toward low and vulgar matters. For example, due to the lack of support for sublime and thought-oriented works, some artists have turned to vulgarity and sexual jokes to attract audiences and succeed at the box office. Certainly, this is a vicious cycle for sustaining the theater economy and a wrong path for attracting audiences. Because even if it works in the short term, employing immoral jokes will gradually cause the true theatergoer to turn away from this field.

🔸 The secretary of the Seventh National Street Theater and Arbaeen Hosseini Ta'ziyeh Mourning Festival also commented on the performance of aristocratic plays: Fortunately, the luxury and flashy theater approach devoid of structure and content has not yet reached the cities. I hope this type of play does not become a model for young people in the provinces, because we will not have a bright outlook in this field.

He reminded: Professors and those who care about this field have repeatedly warned about the destructive impact of aristocratic theater; this type of play is not an appropriate response to the needs of our society. Aristocratic plays are incomplete and luxurious copies of European examples and have no aspect other than the economic and entertainment dimension!
