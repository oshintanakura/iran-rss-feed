Kayhan investigates 🔰 Securing gas for the northern provinces is one of the president's goals on his trip to Turkmenistan

🔸With the onset of the cold season, the gas imbalance will become apparent, especially in the north of the country; therefore, the president, who is heading to Turkmenistan today, must pursue the import and swap of gas from the northern neighbor.

🔸According to our reporter, our country, due to its vast geography and the unparalleled coverage of the household gas network, along with the dependence of power plants and various industries on gas fuel, has faced an imbalance between production and consumption. The enemy's attack on South Pars during the 40-day war, although it met with Iran's decisive and unexpected response to the gas facilities of "Ras Laffan" and caught the enemy off guard, part of the country's gas production was also damaged and the imbalances worsened.

🔸Damage to five phases of the South Pars refineries during the Ramadan war reduced the country's daily gas production capacity by about 230 million cubic meters. So far, according to the National Gas Company, 95 million cubic meters of gas have returned to the grid, and currently there is a deficit of 135 million cubic meters of gas.

🔸If the upcoming winter is similar to last year's winter in terms of temperature and consumption, and only 95 million cubic meters of the damaged capacity is restored, the gas deficit will be about 130 million cubic meters, which, combined with the previous imbalance figure, will total about 380 million cubic meters per day.

🔸The gas shortage problem is not limited to homes, and power plants, in order to prevent power outages for household subscribers, are forced to turn to alternative fuels, namely diesel and fuel oil. The increased consumption of fuel oil, or mazut, also leads to an increase in pollutants and worsening air pollution.

🔸Of course, gas imports from Turkmenistan also face obstacles. The story of gas imports from this country began with a 25-year contract concluded between the parties in 1997, but due to financial problems that arose during Bijan Zangeneh's ministry in Hassan Rouhani's government, it ran into trouble in subsequent years. Of course, the other side also had breaches of commitment, which ultimately, with the weak defense of the Iranian side in the legal dispute, resulted in our country being fined. This fine and its consequences have cast a shadow over oil transfers between the two countries in recent years.

🔸Despite all these problems, during the era of the martyred Raisi in the Thirteenth Government and with Iran's personal follow-up, Iran was able to receive the right of oil swap between Turkmenistan and Azerbaijan in the form of gas. Now the Fourteenth Government must exert all its power to renew this matter — whether in the form of swap rights or direct import.
