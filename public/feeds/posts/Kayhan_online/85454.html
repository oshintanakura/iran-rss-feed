🔰The Special Place of Minab's Children at the 38th Children's Film Festival
🔸The 38th Children and Adolescents Film Festival, taking place in Isfahan from 11 to 16 Mehr, will give special attention to the martyrs and the children of Minab.
🔸According to a Kayhan reporter, Hamed Jafari, CEO of the Farabi Cinema Foundation and secretary of the 38th Children and Adolescents Film Festival, stated at the event's press conference: Contrary to expectations of a possible drop in participation due to the aftermath of the recent war, which had disrupted production, filmmakers' turnout for this edition has fortunately been unprecedented, with registrations nearly double those of previous editions. Regarding this edition's special focus on the second and third imposed wars and the children of Minab, he explained: Out of respect for the martyred children of Minab, all of the festival's usual special sections have been eliminated, and only the special Minab section and award will be held. A dedicated poster and statuette have been designed for this section. Additionally, in the Minab screenwriting competition, which drew widespread participation, 4 top proposals were selected from among 423 submissions and their screenplays were completed, with the winners to be announced during the festival.
🔸The secretary of the 38th Children and Adolescents Film Festival continued, noting structural changes and the addition of new sections to the event, and added: In order to recognize the efforts of the technical crew in children's cinema, who are less visible at festivals, awards have been added for technical categories such as acting, editing, and music. New sections have also been added to this edition, including artificial intelligence, to address emerging technologies; a documentary section in workshop format; and the Mirror section, aimed at reviewing and analyzing works broadcast on platforms and television, with a focus on audience engagement and product development.
🔸He added: The Adolescents Filmmaking Olympiad, which was halted last year due to special circumstances, will also be held this year alongside the festival in Isfahan.
🔸Jafari announced the names of the 15 feature-length fiction films selected for the competition section of this edition of the Children's Festival as follows: Badbadak-ha, Pelk-e Parishan-e Parvaneh, Do Tofangdar, Shootinga, Arous-e Cheshmeh, Amu-ye Bacheh-ha, Kakaluti, Kuch, Gorgi Khan, Man Jasouzan Nistam, Nabegheh va Div-e Sangi, Negahbanan-e Khorshid, Noor, Varkana, and Yeki Miad Donbalam. #Adab_va_Honar🔘 Bale | Eitaa | Soroush+ | Instagram | Telegram@Kayhan_Online
