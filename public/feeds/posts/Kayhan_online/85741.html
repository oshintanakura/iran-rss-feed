A look at the series "Asbab-e Zahmat" 🔰 An artless comedy
👤 #Arash_Fahim
🔸The series "Asbab-e Zahmat" has gone on air at a time when we are facing an inflation crisis of fake and cheap comedies, especially in cinema, home entertainment, and theater; works that, if until recently they relied on double entendres, have now become completely one-sided and have openly fallen into the abyss of immorality. Amid this flood of fallen comedies, "Asbab-e Zahmat" is a windfall; a sweet and warm series that, by distancing itself from the usual buffoonery and vulgarity, is pasteurized and at the same time, in each episode, enjoys an acceptable volume of comedic situations.
🔸"Fatherhood" is another prominent theme of this series. Unfortunately, in the past decade, "father-bashing" has become a widespread syndrome in dramatic works — especially cinema and home entertainment networks. "Asbab-e Zahmat," however, through the character of Ahmad, reconstructs and rehabilitates a father's concerns, love, suffering, and joys in various situations.
