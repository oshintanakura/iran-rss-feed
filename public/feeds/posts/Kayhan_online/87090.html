🔰 "Toward the Light": Sheikh Zakzaky's Wife's Account of the Islamic Movement and Prison in Nigeria

🔸The book "Toward the Light," written by Zeenat Ibrahim, wife of Sheikh Ibrahim Zakzaky, is an account of the history of Nigeria's Islamic movement, written under prison conditions and after the martyrdom of her children, with the aim of recording memories and preventing the distortion of the movement's history.

🔸In this work, alongside narrating her life with Sheikh Zakzaky and the ups and downs of Nigeria's Islamic movement, Zeenat Ibrahim recounts her personal experiences of detention, political pressure, and the martyrdom of her children. She began writing the book in prison, and in the introduction, referring to the circumstances of its writing, she says: "I am writing these words in prison."

🔸By linking the history of a movement to the lived experiences of a family, "Toward the Light" seeks to offer a valuable historical portrait of contemporary developments in Nigeria. This narrative is an effort to preserve historical memory and pass on experiences whose story should not be forgotten over time.
