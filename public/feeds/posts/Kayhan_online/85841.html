🔰Dowry: A Woman's Price or a Financial Right?!👤 #Meysam_Soleimani🔸Dowry (mahr) is one of the well-known rulings in the Islamic marriage system, but sometimes in public perception, the existence of a financial obligation on the man's part creates the impression that the woman has placed herself at the man's disposal in exchange for money. This perception becomes even stronger when marriage is analyzed solely through the lens of an economic or sexual relationship—such that dowry is regarded as a kind of "price" and the marital bond as a kind of "transaction."🔸Buying and selling is based on the transfer of ownership of property from seller to buyer in exchange for consideration; but in marriage, the woman does not become an object of the man's ownership. What the marriage contract establishes is the marital relationship and a set of mutual rights and obligations—not the transfer of ownership of one human being to another.🔸In Islam, dowry is not the price of the woman, because in marriage, the woman is not an object of purchase or ownership. The Quran has introduced marriage as a firm covenant and the dowry as a gift belonging to the woman herself.
