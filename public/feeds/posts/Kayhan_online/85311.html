📷 Family of the warrior martyr Khamenei

🔸The letter of His Eminence Ayatollah Sayyid Mojtaba Khamenei, Supreme Leader of the Revolution, to his brother Ayatollah Sayyid Mostafa Khamenei during his adolescence and during his presence on the frontlines of the Sacred Defense has been published:

🔺In His Exalted Name

In the service of my dear brother, the esteemed warrior Sayyid Mostafa, finally after a long time during which I had intended to write a letter, I succeeded in doing so.

🔺Wish you were here — we in Mashhad, after pilgrimage and visiting, went to visit Sabzevar. The people's welcome was very good and encouraging. But well, our place too is empty in that atmosphere of purity and sincerity and love for God. I wish we could again find the opportunity and be present in that divine place. After we came to Tehran, I have constantly been trying to prepare textbooks and register at the Warriors' Complex, until yesterday I succeeded in registering.

🔺Everyone is supposed to write a few lines in continuation of these two letters, and I send greetings on behalf of Bashar and Hoda as well. I hope that under the shelter of the successes granted by the Almighty, you will fulfill your duty (in the best manner). I will not take up much of your time and I entrust you to God.

🔺And peace. Sayyid Mojtaba
Wednesday, 9 Mordad 65
