📹 Shahid Moeidi Bridge reopened after 55 days of reconstruction

🔸 Minister of Roads and Urban Development in Hormozgan:
Shahid Moeidi Bridge was reopened along with seven other bridges — the Kahurestan outbound and inbound bridges, the Sarzeh outbound and inbound bridges, Manba Ab Bridge, Nimeh-Kareh Bridge, and Shur Bridge — all of which were targeted in a US military attack in July of this year.

🔸 After 55 days of round-the-clock efforts by specialists, Road Maintenance and Transportation Organization jihadi workers, and private-sector contractors, these bridges were rebuilt and made ready to serve.

🔸 This once again demonstrated the capability of the country's specialists and jihadi workers to rapidly rebuild damaged infrastructure and maintain service to the people.
