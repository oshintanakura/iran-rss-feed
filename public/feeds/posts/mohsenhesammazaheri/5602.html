These days, insulting Shariati and other religious intellectuals is, of course, in vogue, and anyone who comes along hurls insults at them. But one must be fair: regarding the Holy Prophet, at least in Persian, no work has yet been written that matches "Muhammad, the Seal of the Prophets," and especially Shariati's essay in it titled "The Face of Muhammad." My knowledge of and devotion to the Prophet I owe to this book and to Shariati's "Islamology"; just as my interest in Quranic studies I owe to Engineer Bazargan and his "The Evolution of the Quran." @mohsenhesammazaheri
