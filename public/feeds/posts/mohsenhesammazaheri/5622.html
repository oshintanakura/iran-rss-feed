🔴 The 12-volume collection "Global Experiences of Post-War Reconstruction" has been published (Examining global experiences of social, cultural, and scientific reconstruction in the post-war period) 🔸The 12-volume collection "Global Experiences of Post-War Reconstruction" was published with the aim of understanding the dimensions and consequences of war and drawing on the experiences of different countries in reconstructing post-war societies. It is the product of collaboration between the Iranian Anthropological Association, the Research Institute for Culture, Art, and Communication of the Ministry of Culture and Islamic Guidance, the Institute for Cultural and Social Studies, and the Policy-Making Office of the Cultural and Social Deputy of the Ministry of Science, edited by Leila Ardabili and Jabbar Rahmani with the participation of 25 translators.🔸This collection addresses the experiences of various countries in confronting the consequences of war and the path of reconstruction that follows. In these studies, post-war reconstruction is not viewed merely as the rebuilding of physical and economic infrastructure, but is examined as a multidimensional process — one that, alongside economic and developmental reconstruction, requires the repair of social capital, the rebuilding of public trust, the revival of hope, the strengthening of social participation, the rebuilding of institutional and cultural capacities, and the reconfiguration of state-society relations.https://www.ricac.ac.ir/news/5891@asiorg
