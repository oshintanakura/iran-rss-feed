#News 💠 Director of the National Archives and Library of the Central Region of the Country Visits the Center for Documents of the Seminary and the Clergy

🔸 Mohsen Hesam Mazaheri, Director of the National Archives and Library of the Central Region of the Country and a writer and researcher in social studies of religion and Shiism, visited the Center for Documents of the Seminary and the Clergy on Thursday, 23 Mehr 1405. During the visit, the center's mission and activities were explained, along with the history of its establishment, its development process, and its archival capacities, and various sections of the exhibition hall were toured.

▫️ Exploring areas of common cooperation in records management—including synergy in digitalization processes and the organization and archival description of resources—was among the topics discussed at the meeting. The exchange of specialized experience and the use of mutual capacities can help raise the standards of document processing and facilitate researchers' access to historical resources.

▫️ The need to expand interaction among like-minded archival and library centers and to make the fullest use of their specialized capabilities in delivering archival services was also emphasized.
