There's also a smaller group of religious people who think about religion and care deeply about it. For this second group, we can envision a spectrum ranging from religious old-thinkers to religious new-thinkers.

It's clear that everyone who spends their time thinking about religion and religious matters can be placed somewhere on this spectrum — no one is left out.

P.S. "Religious intellectual" was from the start a nonsensical, incorrect term — an incompatible, discordant compound.
