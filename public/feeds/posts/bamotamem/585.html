Strategy at the Individual Level

You've heard me say this a lot, but since I brought up resource constraints, I'd like to repeat it once more:

Whenever someone tells me, "Starting today, I want to do such-and-such" (study a language, read books, exercise, ...),

my first question to them is:

"What are you going to stop doing starting today?" Because your day wasn't 23.5 hours before, that you'd now want to add half an hour to it. Until yesterday you also had 24 hours, and now you want to add a quarter hour or half an hour of a new activity to it. So something has to give.

Now either you're going to watch fewer movies,
or go to fewer parties,
or sleep less,
or exercise less,
or check Instagram and Telegram less,
or ...

If someone hasn't clearly answered this second question, that first thing they said is just a passing fad. Sooner or later, you should expect them to rattle off all sorts of excuses for "it not working out" and "giving up."
