If Hitler had been killed... On March 13, 1930, just as the car carrying Hitler entered the intersection, a large truck came speeding into the intersection from the right and slammed into the right side of Hitler's car. The exact side where Hitler was sitting in the front seat, next to his driver. The truck pushed the car to the other side of the intersection and came to a stop just before it could run over the car and crush it along with everyone inside. Hitler asked loudly, "Is everyone still alive?" Everyone survived. Though Hitler and two other occupants were left with bruises and contusions. Three years later, he became Chancellor of Germany, and what followed was the carnage of World War II and the Holocaust — a slaughter that claimed tens of millions of lives. If the truck had crushed the car and Hitler had been killed, would those deaths still have happened?
