In a conversation I had with one of the Motamem members under one of the complex systems courses (in the emergence course), I needed to refer to and cite Damasio's new book titled "Natural Intelligence and the Logic of Consciousness." It came out three or four days ago. For those who wonder how concepts like "self" and "identity" take shape in living beings (and more than anything in humans), it offers fascinating answers. This debate is still an unfinished one in the world and will probably never be settled (because it is more a debate in the realm of language and words than in the physical world). But engaging with this debate and its surrounding discussions bears much fruit. I used to introduce Damasio's book Descartes' Error in my in-person classes and teach parts of it. But later, I don't know why, no opportunity came up to mention it in my writings. Damasio, compared to his peers (of whom there aren't many), is not as recognized in our country as he deserves. Perhaps his new book will create an opportunity for him to be discussed more.
