The B&R Program – Part Two
The Chinese government launched the Belt & Road Initiative in 2013. "Belt" refers to the land route. "Road" here doesn't mean a road; it means a route — a maritime route.
The idea behind B&R is China's development of land and sea routes to the West (specifically Europe), and roughly 150 countries are involved. That is, China went and talked to countries one by one and worked out a framework with each of them regarding what kinds of cooperation it could have with them along this route.
