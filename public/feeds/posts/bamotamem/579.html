Large World Models (LWMs)

This conversation I had with ChatGPT isn't an ideal example — it's even a bit misleading.

But it can, to some extent, illustrate the difference between a language model and a world model.

The current wave of AI will presumably die down once the current bubble bursts (and of course its amazing achievements and capabilities will stay with us).

In the next wave, we'll be dealing with large models of the world — LWMs, or Large World Models — that have a better grasp of what's possible and impossible in the physical world.

Of course, even now, the models Tesla uses in the automotive industry and Palantir uses in warfare are of the World Model variety. But even so — whether in terms of the technology and system architecture, or in terms of commercial and widespread access — we're still a very long way from "artificial intelligence capable of understanding the physical world."

This gap — contrary to all the hype — isn't just a matter of scale.
