📢 The JCPOA is still a factor in the Security Council's calculations

▶️ The newspaper "Sazandegi" reported that despite the political weakening of the JCPOA and the expiration of the timeframe stipulated in Resolution 2231, the legal ramifications of the agreement are still evident in disputes over Iran's nuclear file.

▶️ According to Sazandegi, Russia and China on Thursday vetoed a US-proposed resolution to extend the mandate of the Panel of Experts on Iran sanctions, citing the legal framework established after the 2015 nuclear deal and Resolution 2231.

▶️ Moscow and Beijing argue that the process for reimposing and implementing Security Council sanctions must align with the provisions and timeframes set out in Resolution 2231 — an interpretation that puts them at odds with the US and European countries.

▶️ Sazandegi concluded that although the JCPOA has been severely weakened politically, its legal legacy remains a central point of contention among major powers over the Iran file.
