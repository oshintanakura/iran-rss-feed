📢 The three-urgency plan for withdrawal from the NPT has not been announced to the National Security Commission

◀️ Sardar Mohammad Esmail Kosari and Hojjatoleslam val-Moslemin Vahid Ahmadi, members of the National Security and Foreign Policy Commission of the Parliament, in reaction to the publication of news about the preparation of a three-urgency plan for withdrawal from the NPT, announced that such a matter has not been announced to them or the Commission so far.

🗣️ Hojjatoleslam Vahid Ahmadi also said: "Nothing has been announced to us in this regard yet," and if such a matter is true, it should naturally be presented to the National Security and Foreign Policy Commission, as the specialized commission for this matter. Ahmadi also stated that the Parliament has no session this week and the representatives are present in their constituencies to conduct inspections.
