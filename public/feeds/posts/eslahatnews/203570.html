📢 Qaem-Panah: We hope there will be no blackouts next year; the expansion of solar and renewable energy is being pursued seriously

🗣 Mohammad-Jafar Qaem-Panah, Executive Deputy to the President, today, 23 Mehr, at the inauguration ceremony for water and wastewater projects and electricity infrastructure in East Azerbaijan, noting the imbalances in various sectors across the country, stated:

◀️ Water and electricity were among the areas that faced a serious crisis last year. Due to low rainfall, especially in cities like Tabriz, Tehran, and several other major cities, we encountered problems in supplying water, but fortunately, thanks to God's grace and the prudence of officials, people did not feel the water shortage.

◀️ Water from wastewater treatment can be very helpful, at least for industrial and agricultural uses, particularly for irrigating non-fruit-bearing trees.

◀️ Life today is not possible without electricity, and during peak consumption hours, we face a shortage of about 6,000 to 7,000 megawatts of electricity.

◀️ With the measures Dr. Pezeshkian has initiated and is pursuing in developing renewable and solar energy, we hope this serious problem will be resolved. Over the past two years, more than 6,200 megawatts of electricity have been added to the country's power grid through renewable energy.

◀️ We hope the government's efforts to develop renewable energy will progress in such a way that we have no blackouts next year, or at least that blackouts are reduced to a minimum.

◀️ The projects we are inaugurating today have cost a total of about 13,000 billion tomans, equivalent to 13 Hemmat, and are being brought into operation as two wastewater projects and 10 electricity projects.
