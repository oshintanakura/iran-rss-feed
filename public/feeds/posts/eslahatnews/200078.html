📢 Baghdad will not accept Iranian flights starting at midnight; talks underway to redirect flights to Najaf

◀️ Majid Akhavan, spokesperson for the Civil Aviation Organization, announced: As of 24:00 tonight local time, Baghdad Airport will not accept flights operated by Iranian airline companies.

◀️ According to him, the Civil Aviation Organization is negotiating to transfer flights destined for Baghdad to Najaf Airport.

◀️ Akhavan emphasized: This restriction currently applies to Baghdad only, and other international flights are operating according to schedule.

◀️ However, some specific routes to Oman and Turkey had already faced restrictions; among them Mahan, which has suspended its flights to Muscat, Istanbul, and Ankara until further notice.
