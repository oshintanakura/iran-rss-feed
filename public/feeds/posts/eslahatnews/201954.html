📢 Wrestling Federation's prize for medalists: Gold 3 billion, Bronze 300 million
◀️ Following the performance of Iran's national team members at the Asian Games in Japan, the Wrestling Federation increased the prizes for medalists.
◀️ The prize for gold was raised to 3 billion tomans and for bronze to 300 million tomans.
◀️ Previously, the prize for gold had been set at 2 billion, silver at 300 million, and bronze at 100 million tomans.
◀️ These amounts are paid separately from the prizes awarded by the Ministry of Sport and Youth and the National Olympic Committee.
