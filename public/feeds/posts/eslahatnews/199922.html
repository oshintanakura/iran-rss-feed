📢 New regulations for Tehran concerts; from identity registration to ban on bringing liquids◀️ The Headquarters for Enjoining Good and Forbidding Evil of Tehran Province has announced the issuance of new directives for concert organizers and music performance halls.◀️ Under this directive, all halls must be equipped with surveillance cameras, and police must have access to the footage. Additionally, when issuing tickets, individuals' identity details and mobile phone numbers will be recorded.◀️ Forming a "Council for Enjoining Good" at the venue, appointing a designated person in charge for each event, banning liquids from entering the hall, and strict monitoring of buffets are among the other provisions of this directive.◀️ According to the published text, violations of the regulations will be referred in writing to Branch 38 of the Prosecutor's Office, which specializes in moral security crimes.
