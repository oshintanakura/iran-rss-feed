📢 Will pensions increase with the amendment of Article 106? Hossein Rahmanian, spokesperson for the Employment Organization: ◀️ The bill to amend Article 106 of the Civil Service Management Law is before parliament to narrow the gap between the salaries of employees and retirees. ◀️ Under this bill, deductions would also be made from items such as welfare benefits and bonuses during employment. ◀️ The goal is for pensions to be determined in proportion to the deductions made during service. ◀️ This plan has not yet been finalized and is awaiting parliament's decision. ◀️ Under current conditions, a person's income after retirement may drop to as little as half of what they earned while employed. ◀️ If the bill is approved, this gap is supposed to be narrowed and pensions increased. _Eslahat News ✅ @Eslahatnews 🔗 Eslahatnews.com
