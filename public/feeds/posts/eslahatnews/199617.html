📢 The inevitable shift in Iranian tastes; red meat consumption drops to one-third

◀️ The Donya-e-Eqtesad newspaper has reported a significant change in the meat consumption patterns of Iranian households. According to the report, per capita red meat consumption has fallen from about 14 kilograms in the mid-1380s to roughly 5 kilograms in recent years.

◀️ At the same time, households have increasingly turned to chicken to make up for reduced access to red meat, with chicken's share of total meat consumption rising from about 30 percent in the 1360s to around 60 percent. That figure is said to be less than 30 percent globally.

◀️ Total per capita meat consumption has also dropped from about 35 kilograms in 1383 to 25 kilograms in 1403 — a decline of roughly 30 percent.

◀️ Donya-e-Eqtesad stressed that this trend cannot be attributed solely to a "change in taste"; the decline in households' real income and the surge in food prices have also played a major role. According to the report, meat prices rose about 5.8 times between 1400 and 1404.
