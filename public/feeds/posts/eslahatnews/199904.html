📢 Siroos Ebrahimzadeh, a veteran of radio and television, has passed away
◀️ Ehtram Boroumand wrote on her social media page: With the deepest regret, we have lost our country's cultured artist and a veteran of radio, television, theater, and cinema, Mr. Siroos Ebrahimzadeh.
◀️ Siroos Ebrahimzadeh, a theater actor and director, was born in 1316 in Ardabil.
◀️ He began his career in 1334 in theater, working with the late Mostafa Oskouei at the Anahita Theater. He appeared in films such as Kamal-ol-Molk, Hotel Carton, A Candle in the Wind, Passenger of Rey, Chehreh, Hamsar, Tohfeha, Mojassameh, and others.
