📢 Member of Parliament's Economic Commission: Ban on home appliance imports on verge of being lifted
Hakem Mamkan, member of Parliament's Economic Commission:
◀️ The ban on importing four categories of home appliances must not lead to price hikes and the exclusion of low-income groups and young couples from the market for these goods.
◀️ In the Article 4 working group, it was decided that manufacturers would be allowed to import 1.2 billion dollars' worth of needed components via Kolbari and Malvani routes, in order to support domestic production.
◀️ Annually, around 500 to 600 million dollars' worth of four categories of home appliances were being imported, and it was decided that Kolbars and Malvans could import goods equivalent to twice that amount.
◀️ If it becomes clear that manufacturers are not importing the 1.2 billion dollars' worth of needed components via Malvani and Kolbari routes, we will reconsider the decision.
◀️ In that case, there is a possibility that the ban on importing four categories of goods will be lifted and import permits issued for up to 500 to 600 million dollars through Kolbari and Malvani procedures.
