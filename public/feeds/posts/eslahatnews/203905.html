🎥 Unveiling of the Neo1 humanoid robot with a 500 kg payload capacity at CIIF 2026

◀️ The Neo1 humanoid robot, built by the Chinese company NeodraDynamics, has returned to CIIF 2026. This 4-meter, 1.2-ton robot is designed for heavy and high-risk tasks such as heavy assembly, maritime operations, material handling at ports and mines, nuclear power plant repairs, and search and rescue.

◀️ The Neo1 robot features a hybrid hydraulic-electric actuation system with 41 degrees of freedom. Each arm can carry 300 kg, and the two arms combined can carry 500 kg. Its dexterous hands are claimed to deliver a grip force of 500 kgf and fingertip precision of 0.01 mm.

◀️ An operating temperature range from minus 50 to 250 degrees Celsius and an operating depth of up to 10,000 meters are among the other announced specifications. NeodraDynamics is deploying this robot to the supply chain and industrial sites of JD Industrial for real-world testing.
