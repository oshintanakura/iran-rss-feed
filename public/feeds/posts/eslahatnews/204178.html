📢 Official announcement by the Office of the Preservation and Publication of the Works of the Martyr Leader of the Revolution opposing the renaming of places with established and commonly used names to the name of the Martyr Imam

◀️ The Office of the Preservation and Publication of the Works of the Martyr Leader of the Revolution, in an official response to a letter from the Municipality of Mashhad, announced that it does not agree with naming places that have commonly used and established names after His Eminence Ayatollah al-Uzma Seyyed Ali Khamenei, may God be pleased with him.

◀️ Following requests and follow-ups by officials across the country to name streets and places after the Martyr Leader of the Revolution, in the latest correspondence, the proposal of the Mayor of Mashhad to name the Kuhsangi and University streets of this city after him was received in a letter to the Office of the Preservation and Publication of the Works of the Martyr Leader of the Revolution.

◀️ In response to this proposal, this office has announced: "This office does not agree with the naming of places having commonly used and established names after the blessed name of the Martyr Mujahid Imam, His Eminence Ayatollah al-Uzma Seyyed Ali Khamenei, may God sanctify his pure soul."
