📢 Twelve water and electricity projects inaugurated in East Azerbaijan with a budget of 124,500 billion tomans

◀️ In the presence of Mohammad Jafar Qaempanah, Executive Deputy to the President, during a visit to East Azerbaijan province today, 23 Mehr, 12 projects — including 2 water and wastewater projects and 10 electricity infrastructure projects — with a total announced budget of 124,500 billion tomans were inaugurated.

◀️ Among the inaugurated projects are a water supply project serving 150 villages in the province with a budget of 665 billion tomans, and the Azarshahr wastewater treatment plant with its transmission and collection network, Module One, with a budget of 1,740 billion tomans, both implemented by the East Azerbaijan Water and Wastewater Company.

◀️ The water supply project for 150 villages includes drilling and equipping 25 wells, building 19 reservoirs, laying 165 kilometers of transmission lines and 137 kilometers of water distribution network, rehabilitating 12 kilometers of transmission line, and constructing 6 pumping stations. With this project coming online, a stable water supply will be provided for 150 villages comprising 10,427 households and a population of over 36,745.

◀️ The Azarshahr wastewater treatment plant, using the advanced SBR method with a daily treatment capacity of 8,100 cubic meters, will serve a population of over 84,000.

◀️ Additionally, 10 electricity infrastructure projects under the responsibility of the East Azerbaijan Regional Electricity Company have been inaugurated. These projects include a 230-kilovolt double-circuit cable line to supply the central GIS substation of Tabriz via the energy tunnel, the 230-kilovolt section of the central GIS substation of Tabriz, and eight line and substation projects.

◀️ The total expenditure for the electricity projects is announced at 11,095 billion tomans, and their implementation has created jobs for 400 people. The goals of these projects include increasing the reliability of electricity supply to central Tabriz and its subordinate counties, and meeting the electricity needs of industrial parks.
