📢 Farewell to the fuel card by the end of 1405; gasoline quota to be transferred to bank cards
◀️ Reza Sepahvand, spokesman for the Parliament's Energy Commission, announced a plan to transfer the gasoline quota from the fuel card to the bank card of the vehicle owner.
◀️ According to Sepahvand, the technical groundwork for the plan has been laid, and its pilot implementation is set to begin by the end of Mehr in five provinces. If fully implemented, the gasoline quota will be usable directly through the vehicle owner's bank card.
◀️ Sepahvand also announced plans to collect the free fuel cards at stations and the possibility of increasing the gasoline quota at a rate of 10,000 tomans.
◀️ According to the spokesman for the Parliament's Energy Commission, the phase-out of the current fuel card is set to be completed by the end of the year 1405./Didban Iran_ Eslahat News
