📢 Change in salary payments for contract workers starting in Mehr; salaries to be paid directly through the government system

◀️ Starting in early Mehr, registration of contract workers' information for covered entities will begin in the integrated administrative system, and once confirmed, these workers' salaries will be paid through government systems — a plan aimed at increasing oversight and reducing problems with salary and insurance payments.

◀️ Under a Council of Ministers resolution, covered entities have until 9 Mehr to register information on contract staff, contractors, and related payments. Responsibility for the accuracy of this information rests with the senior and financial managers of each entity.
