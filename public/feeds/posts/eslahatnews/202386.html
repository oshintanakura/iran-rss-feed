📢 Death sentence for a 23-year-old girl in the "Sab al-Nabi" case; case heads to the Supreme Court
◀️ Khorasan newspaper has reported that a 23-year-old girl in Mashhad was sentenced to death by Branch Six of the Criminal Court One of Razavi Khorasan on charges of publishing insulting content against the Infallible Imams (AS) in cyberspace.
◀️ According to this report, the defendant said in her defense that when she published the content, she and her friends had consumed a type of hallucinogenic mushroom and she was under its influence. Her lawyer also tried to argue that the published statements stemmed from the defendant's particular circumstances, but the court did not accept these defenses.
◀️ The defendant also stated that she regrets her behavior and has expressed remorse and repentance. The sentence is not final, and if appealed, the case will be sent to the Supreme Court for judicial review.
