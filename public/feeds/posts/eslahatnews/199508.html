📢 Proposal to increase the Kala-Barg (commodity voucher) amount to 2 million by the Parliament's Social Commission

Babaei, Head of the Social Commission:
◀️ In joint meetings with the Minister of Cooperatives, Labor, and Social Welfare, as well as on the open floor of the Parliament in the presence of Qalibaf, the proposal to raise the Kala-Barg amount to 2 million tomans has been put forward. The necessary reports were submitted so that appropriate measures could be taken based on the current circumstances.
◀️ According to the Vision Plan and the budget projections for the year 1405, the Kala-Barg amount per person is estimated at approximately 2,500,000 tomans.
