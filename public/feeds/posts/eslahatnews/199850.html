📸 Minab Children's Exhibition Held in Front of the United Nations Headquarters in Geneva
◀️ The Minab Children's Exhibition was organized by two Iranian civil activists residing in Geneva, in front of the United Nations headquarters in Geneva.
◀️ The exhibition opened on Saturday, 28 Shahrivar 1405, at Place des Nations, in front of the main entrance of the United Nations headquarters and adjacent to Geneva's famous "Broken Chair" — a well-known symbol of the consequences of war and violence against civilians — and will run through Tuesday, 31 Shahrivar.
◀️ The exhibition displays images depicting the scale of this humanitarian catastrophe, paintings by the martyred children of Minab, as well as several of their school bags and a number of belongings they left behind, for diplomats, staff of international organizations, citizens, and tourists to see.
