The Islamic Republic of Iran Broadcasting (IRIB) news agency reported hearing the sound of several explosions in Qeshm on Tuesday evening and wrote: "Moments ago, the sound of more than 5 explosions was heard around the Masen village of Qeshm."
The news agency wrote: "Moments ago, the sound of 4 explosions was also heard from the direction of the Strait of Hormuz in Qeshm."
Iranian media have also reported hearing the sound of explosions in Bandar Abbas, Sirik, and Chabahar.
The political, security, and social deputy of Hormozgan province's governorate says that so far no impact or incident has been reported in Hormozgan.

Ali Khalilabadi, the security and law enforcement deputy of Sistan and Baluchestan's governor, in an interview with the state news agency IRNA, reported the impact of four projectiles in Chabahar and Konarak counties.
