China's Foreign Ministry, in response to the latest US sanctions against Iran, announced that Beijing and Tehran's cooperation is carried out within the framework of international law and "should not face interference or disruption." Lin Jian, the ministry's spokesperson, said on Tuesday that China is closely monitoring the developments and will take "all necessary measures" to defend its rights and interests. He further emphasized that China has always expressed its opposition to the US unilateral sanctions and deemed them illegal. According to him, economic warfare and maximum pressure "only fuel more tension and conflict." The US on Monday imposed sanctions on 60 individuals, entities, and ships linked to Iran, stating its aim was to cut off the Islamic Republic's "economic lifeline." US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, at a press conference on Monday about the new pressure campaign against Iran, said: "Across the world, our goal is to cut off all the economic arteries that keep this authoritarian regime alive until Tehran is completely isolated."
