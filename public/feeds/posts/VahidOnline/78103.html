The UAE Ministry of Defense, in a statement issued before noon on Monday, August 31, denied media reports that Al Minhad Air Base was targeted with "missiles." Hours earlier, Iran's Islamic Republic Army had reported a "drone" attack on the U.S. base on Emirati soil.
