Duolingo has announced that its language test is not available in Iran or for holders of Iranian identity documents. At the same time, reports have emerged of TOEFL test cancellations and no new dates being announced for the exam in Iran. These developments come a few days after the suspension of one of the US sanctions waivers related to educational services for Iranians. On August 24, the US Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) suspended for an indefinite period the general license known as "G," which since 2014 had permitted certain academic exchanges and the provision of educational services to Iranians. Duolingo, the American company behind the language-learning app whose online English test is accepted by many universities, has now stated on its official support page that the test is not available in Iran or for individuals using Iranian identity documents. Meanwhile, a number of Iranian users on social media have shared images that they say show TOEFL test cancellation messages and the absence of test centers or dates in the ETS registration system (the organizer of the TOEFL exam). Radio Farda cannot independently verify the authenticity or origin of these images. Some candidates have also said that TOEFL tests were being held in Iran until recently, but after OFAC's new decision, cancellation messages were sent to some applicants. As of the time of this report, the institution that administers the TOEFL exam had not issued an official statement about halting the test in Iran. On the institution's website, Iran still appears on the list of countries where the TOEFL iBT test is offered, and contact information specifically for Iranian applicants is also visible. Therefore, it remains unclear what scope the reported restrictions have and whether they are directly caused by OFAC's decision. The general license G, issued by OFAC in March 2014, among other things allowed accredited American universities to have academic exchange programs with Iranian universities and to provide certain educational services to Iranian students.@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
