Artin Khajouinia, a 16-year-old computer science student, was killed by a live bullet on the evening of January 8, 2026, during protests in front of the governor's office in Qods City, Qaleh Hassan Khan. Artin's mother has released a video of collecting her son's shoes; shoes from different periods of his life that now remain as mementos. The mother of this slain teenager wrote: "I review your steps from the first to the last, my hero son. From your moment-by-moment growing up, now only memories remain for me that pass before my eyes every second." "From that beautiful baby in that yellow dress in my arms to that tall man whose face I had to look up to see, you will always remain a memento of my motherhood." She called her son "my eternal hero" and wrote: "Every moment and everywhere, your memory is eternal and your path is full of light."@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
