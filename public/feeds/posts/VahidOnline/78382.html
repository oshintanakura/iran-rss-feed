A US federal court ruled on Monday, September 14, against the implementation of the Donald Trump administration's order to limit the length of stay for foreign students and journalists in the United States. Under the law the Trump administration is trying to implement, the visas of foreign students and of individuals residing in the US under cultural programs would be limited to four years. The law also states that journalists' visas should not exceed 240 days. All three groups, under the law whose implementation the court has now blocked, would have to reapply and renew their visas in order to stay longer.
