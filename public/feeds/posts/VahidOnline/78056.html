Kaveh Madani, an Iranian water researcher and expert, and director of the Institute for Water, Environment and Health at the United Nations University in Canada, received the 2026 Stockholm Water Prize, known as the "Nobel Prize for Water," from Carl XVI Gustaf, the King of Sweden, on Wednesday. The prize was awarded to him at the official ceremony of World Water Week in Stockholm in recognition of his research and activities in water resource management, water governance, and providing innovative perspectives for addressing the water crisis. Kaveh Madani had previously been announced as the winner of this prize in March, and the prize committee had praised his research in water resource management and linking science with policy-making, diplomacy, and public communication. The Stockholm Water Prize has been awarded annually since 1991, and its ceremony is organized by the Stockholm Water Foundation in cooperation with the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences.
