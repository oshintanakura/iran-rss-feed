The Harana news agency has reported that Hossein Nazari, a 25-year-old citizen and one of the detainees of the January 2026 protests, has been sentenced to execution by the First Branch of the Mashhad Revolutionary Court. According to this report, the judiciary of the Islamic Republic tried Mr. Nazari on charges such as "communication with hostile governments and groups" and "assembly and collusion to commit crimes against national security," and his execution sentence was issued in July of this year. Nazari, born in 2001, was arrested by security forces during the January protests and, after interrogation and judicial proceedings, was transferred to Vakilabad Prison in Mashhad. He remains held in this prison.
