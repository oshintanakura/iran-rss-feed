The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations center, UKMTO, reported on Thursday evening Washington time that several "projectiles" struck two vessels near the coast of Oman. According to this report, the strikes occurred four nautical miles west of the city of Khasab, in the Musandam Governorate of Oman. According to the report, the captain of one vessel stated that he witnessed four unidentified projectiles striking two unidentified vessels. Following these strikes, one of the vessels caught fire, and the status of the second vessel is unknown. Omani authorities are investigating the incident.@VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
