Demolition of the "Russians' Caravanserai" in Sabzevar: quotes. The historically valuable house "Toumanian," known as the "Russians' Barracks," in Sabzevar was demolished on Wednesday in broad daylight with a loader, sparking widespread protest from cultural heritage activists. Images published on social networks show that a loader on Wednesday, September 18, demolished part of a historical building known as the "Russians' Barracks" in Sabzevar. The "Russians' Barracks" or the "Toumanian" house in Sabzevar, although it was not registered on the national heritage list, had been declared by the Cultural Heritage Organization as a building of historical value. The architecture of this building belongs to the first Pahlavi era, and during the occupation of Iran by the Russians, the Russian army was stationed in this building for a time, and for this reason it became famous as the "Russians' Barracks." Mojtaba Kavian, Director General of Cultural Heritage and Director of the Sabzevar Historical Fabric Base, said in an interview with Sedaye Miras: This work was demolished without coordination and without permission from Cultural Heritage, and filing a criminal complaint against the demolishers of this historically valuable work is certain.@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
