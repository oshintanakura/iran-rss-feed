The price of the dollar in Iran's free market took an upward trend again on Monday and crossed 202,000 tomans in morning trading. Meanwhile, the price of the Emami coin reached 222 million tomans, and gold prices were also traded at unprecedented levels. According to the latest recorded rates, the US dollar reached 202,600 tomans in the Tehran market. The Emami coin also fetched 222 million tomans. At the same time, the price of one mithqal of melted gold reached 96,200,000 tomans, and the price of one gram of 18-karat gold reached 23,207,860 tomans. @VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
