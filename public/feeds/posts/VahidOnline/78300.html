Benjamin Netanyahu, Israel's Prime Minister, while appearing at Mount Hermon (Jabal al-Shaykh) and referring to control over the border areas of Syria and Lebanon, described the main goal of the country's ongoing military campaigns in the region as defeating and toppling the Iranian regime. In a video message, Netanyahu referred to the presence of Israeli military forces in the border areas of Syria and Lebanon and said: We will not allow any terrorist group to establish itself on our borders. This is one of our great achievements, but the main work still remains. The Israeli Prime Minister, expressing confidence in achieving this goal, added: Our main task is to defeat and completely weaken the Iranian regime.
