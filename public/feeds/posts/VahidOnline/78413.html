Abbas Araghchi, Foreign Minister of the Islamic Republic, met with Wang Yi, China's Foreign Minister, on Wednesday, 25 Shahrivar, during a trip to Beijing, and stressed the expansion of Tehran-Beijing relations within the framework of the comprehensive strategic partnership.

Araghchi attributed the current conditions in the region to US and Israeli military attacks on Iran and expressed gratitude for China's positions in condemning the actions of these two countries.

He said: "The Islamic Republic, while fully prepared to defend its national sovereignty and territorial integrity with strength and to safeguard Iran's security and national interests against aggressors, welcomes diplomatic solutions that uphold the rights of the Iranian nation."

Araghchi also said that the region's conditions have changed since the Iran war, and that in the new regional order — one marked by dialogue and cooperation among regional countries — there is no place for the presence or interference of foreign forces.

Referring to what he called the United States' repeated violations of its commitments, he said the Islamic Republic seeks the return of calm to the region and friendly relations with its neighbors, and to that end has begun dialogue with regional countries.
