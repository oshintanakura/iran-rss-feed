Branch One of the Alborz Province Court of Appeals has upheld a total sentence of 18 years in prison for Manouchehr Bakhtiari, the grieving father of Pouya Bakhtiari, one of those killed in the November 2019 protests. According to the ruling, Bakhtiari was sentenced to 10 years in prison on the charge of "forming and running a group in cyberspace with the aim of disrupting national security," five years for "assembly and collusion to commit crimes against national security through cooperation with one of the groups opposed to the system," two years for "spreading falsehoods with the intent to disturb public opinion," and one year for "propaganda activities against the system." The upholding of this ruling comes less than three weeks after Branch One of the Bandar Abbas Revolutionary Court sentenced Manouchehr Bakhtiari to an additional 10 years in prison in a separate case. In the Bandar Abbas case, he faced charges including "propaganda activities against the system," "inciting people to war and killing," and "sending videos to foreign virtual networks."
