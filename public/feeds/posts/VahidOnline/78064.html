Donald Trump, the President of the United States, answered reporters' questions at the White House after signing an executive order to rename "Lake Ontario" to "Lake America," regarding how secondary sanctions would be applied against countries with economic ties to the Islamic Republic of Iran. Emphasizing Washington's reciprocal stance toward other powers, he added: "You have to consider that for everything they do, we do too." The US President also responded to concerns about Beijing's actions, saying: "Someone was saying about China that we've heard they're spying; we spy on them too.
