Scott Bessent, the U.S. Treasury Secretary, said on Monday, August 31, on the sidelines of the G20 meeting in Asheville, USA, that Washington will continue to apply economic pressure on Tehran and that the results of this pressure may become apparent in the coming weeks or months. In response to a question about when Iran's economy might collapse, Bessent said: "The point is we have the blockade and we will continue to apply pressure. We've had very good conversations here already, and I think this could happen within weeks or months." The U.S. Treasury Secretary added: "The economy doesn't necessarily have to collapse; the Iranian government just needs to come to its senses." The American official added that Bessent will meet with his counterparts on the sidelines of the G20 meeting and will work to increase economic pressure and isolate Iran. Scott Bessent, referring to Iran's attack on U.S. military bases in Jordan, said: "I think they are reacting militarily because they are failing economically."@VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
