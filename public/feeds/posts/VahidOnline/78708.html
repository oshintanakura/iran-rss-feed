Asadollah Jafari, the head of the judiciary of Isfahan province, has cited the release of an audio file of the marriage contract of Alireza Sepahi and Mahshad Kashani as one of the reasons for the arrest of Amirsalar Davoudi, a lawyer. He has called the file "fabricated" and accused Davoudi of publishing material that, according to him, caused "public confusion."

Jafari, on Tuesday, 21 Mehr 1405, in remarks published by the public relations office of the Isfahan judiciary, claimed that the publication of this file on Davoudi's Instagram page paved the way for "propaganda exploitation" by media opposed to the government.

Alireza Sepahi, one of the defendants in the "Meydan Alikhani" case in Isfahan, was executed in the early hours of 13 Mehr. Before the sentence was carried out, Davoudi had published the audio file of the recitation of the marriage contract between Sepahi and Mahshad Kashani, his imprisoned fiancée.
