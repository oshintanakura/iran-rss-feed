The spokesman for the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) called on the US to stop the war and threats, Israel to withdraw from Lebanon, the siege of Yemen to end, $24 billion of Iran's frozen assets to be released, and interference in the Islamic Republic's nuclear and missile programs to cease, in order to end the current situation and reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Hossein Mohbi, the IRGC spokesman, said on Wednesday, September 9, that if the US wants an end to the current situation, it must, while "completely stopping the war," refrain from threatening again. In another part of his remarks, Mohbi threatened that if attacks continue, the IRGC's response would be broader, saying: "If the enemy hits two or three of our targets, we will respond firmly with 20 targets." He also said that the current war has, for the first time, directly transferred "strategic damages" to the US. These statements come as, with the continuation of Iran's naval blockade, oil exports through the Strait of Hormuz have stopped, and the pressure of the additional sanctions of the Trump administration has caused unprecedented inflation in Iran, with the dollar reaching 233,000 tomans. @VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
