During last night's US strikes on the village of Kohestak in Sirik, in addition to a telecommunications tower, at least two residential homes were also targeted. Kohestak was hit five times last night, and it appears that four missiles struck one location. Based on CCTV footage, the first three missiles hit the house where a wedding was taking place. The fourth missile appears to have struck the Hamrah-e Aval telecommunications tower, and the fifth missile hit the wedding venue again. The telecommunications tower was about 112 meters away from the wedding house, and several surrounding homes were also damaged. @VahidHeadline According to a Mehr News Agency report, the residential house hosting the wedding was 136 meters from the telecommunications tower targeted by the US missiles. Rescue officials of the Islamic Republic and Iranian state media announced that the attack killed 4 people and wounded 68 others. The youngest victim of the attack was four-year-old Amirali Karimi. @VahidOOnLine 📡 @VahidOnline
