UK Maritime Trade Operations: Casualties reported in attack on oil tanker north of Qatar

The UK Maritime Trade Operations announced on the evening of Wednesday, 15 Mehr, that an oil tanker in the waters north of Qatar, 51 miles from Madinat ash Shamal, was targeted by several projectiles.

According to the organization, casualties have been reported in the attack, but no details on the number of dead or wounded have yet been released. Authorities are investigating the incident and have asked ships in the region to proceed with caution and report any suspicious activity.
