Masoud Pezeshkian, President of the Islamic Republic and Chairman of the Supreme Council of the Cultural Revolution, has issued for implementation the approval to allocate a quota to university entrance exam candidates affected by the 12-day and 40-day wars. Under this approval, candidates who lost their homes during these wars or the incidents that followed, provided they meet specific conditions, will be granted a 5 percent veterans' quota for two years. According to the text of the Supreme Council of the Cultural Revolution's approval, this benefit applies to candidates seeking admission to universities and higher education institutions whose residential buildings were destroyed during the 12-day war, the 40-day war, or the incidents that followed, up until the time of the exam, such that they can no longer be lived in. Per this approval, the damaged building must be in a condition requiring reconstruction, structural reinforcement, or major repairs. Therefore, mere damage to a home or minor damage alone is not sufficient to qualify for this benefit.
