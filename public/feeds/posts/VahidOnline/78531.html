Qom Province Appeals Court upheld, without modification, the sentence of 74 lashes for Parastoo Ahmadi and eight others from among the musicians and crew of the "Caravanserai Concert."

Lawyer Abouzar Zaman wrote on Friday on the social media platform X that, according to the ruling of Branch 16 of the Qom Appeals Court, Parastoo Ahmadi, four musicians, and four others have been sentenced to 74 lashes in addition to a two-year ban on activity in audiovisual affairs and a ban on leaving the country.

The Qom Province Criminal Court had previously convicted these nine individuals on charges of "outraging public decency through the production and publication of vulgar and immoral content on social media."

In Azar 1403, Parastoo Ahmadi published on YouTube the video of the "Caravanserai Concert," which was performed without mandatory hijab and with the accompaniment of Ehsan Birghdar, Soheil Faghih-Nasiri, Amin Taheri, and Amirali Pirnia.

After the publication of this performance, the Judiciary announced charges against those involved, and Ahmadi and two musicians accompanying her were also detained for a period.

In the preliminary ruling, the court had cited Parastoo Ahmadi's attire as well as the production, filming, and public publication of this performance on social media as among the grounds for issuing the verdict.
