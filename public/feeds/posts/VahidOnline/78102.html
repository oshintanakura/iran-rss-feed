Lawyer Payam Derafshan announced on Monday that the death sentence for his client, Ali Asghar Peyghambari, one of the protesters from December 2025 (Dey 1404), has been upheld by the Supreme Court. Derafshan told the Emtedad news website: "The death sentence for Ali Asghar Peyghambari was previously issued by the Tehran Revolutionary Court, citing the law on intensifying punishment for espionage and cooperation with the Zionist regime." This is while, according to the lawyer, "there was no organizational or any other connection, in any way, between the client and any of the hostile groups," and Peyghambari was only present at the protests.@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
