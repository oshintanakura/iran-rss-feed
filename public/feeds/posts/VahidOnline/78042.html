Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, the Speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly, by sharing a portion of the video of US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's press conference on X, criticized his statements about new sanctions against Iran with sarcasm. In the video, a reporter, referring to Bessent's claim of the start of an "economic D-Day," asks him why sanctions are not being imposed immediately, and Bessent replies: "Why would I want to blow up the global financial system?" Ghalibaf, mocking this contradiction in the US Treasury Secretary's remarks, wrote: "He first says economic D-Day! But five seconds later he says why would I want to blow up the global financial system? Sir, this is not the shores of Normandy; this is a comedy show, and you've even forgotten your own script!" @VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
