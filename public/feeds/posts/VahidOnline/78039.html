Ali Nikzad, the deputy speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly (Iranian parliament), in an interview with the ISNA news agency, spoke of a reduction in two fuel quotas based on the latest decisions of the parliament. According to him, the 60-liter quota at 1,500 tomans will be maintained, but the 70-liter quota at 3,000 tomans will be reduced to 50 liters. Also, the 30-liter quota at 5,000 tomans is set to be reduced to 15 liters. He also said, "Based on the latest decision made regarding gasoline, it was decided that the price of gasoline would not increase." He was referring to the 1,500-toman gasoline. Mr. Nikzad rejected the setting of a fourth fuel price. Yesterday, the chief of staff to Masoud Pezeshkian, Iran's president, also said that fuel quotas would definitely be reduced. @VahidHeadline 📡 @VahidOnline
