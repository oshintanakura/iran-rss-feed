Tasnim News Agency, close to the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), reported the deaths of three Iranian Army pilots in Tuesday night's US strikes on Iran. The agency, publishing the names and photos of these pilots, said two were Navy pilots and one was an Air Force pilot, but did not state where or how they were killed. However, the announced names are three of the seven people reported on Wednesday, September 2 (11 Shahrivar), to have been killed in US attacks on the cities of Ahvaz and Aghajari. During the Tuesday night US strikes, according to Iranian officials, areas including Jiroft Airport in southern Kerman Province, Asaluyeh, Kermanshah, areas in Khuzestan Province, the cities of Chabahar and Konarak in Sistan and Baluchestan Province, and Sirik, Lavan, Qeshm, and Bandar Abbas in Hormozgan Province were targeted. The Ministry of Health spokesman reported on Thursday morning that '18 people were killed and 142 injured' during the recent US attacks.@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
