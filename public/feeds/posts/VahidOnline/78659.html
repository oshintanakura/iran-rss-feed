Saudi Arabia is pursuing the development of the Marjan oil field under a plan worth about $21 billion to boost its oil production and gas processing capacity. In the latest phase of Marjan's development, Saudi Aramco has signed a contract with the American company KBR to upgrade the field's facilities.
