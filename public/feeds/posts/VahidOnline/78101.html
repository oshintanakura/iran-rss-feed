The IRGC announced on Monday in a statement broadcast on the Islamic Republic's state television that a giant oil tanker struck two naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz, caught fire, and came to a complete stop. In its statement, the IRGC claimed the tanker intended to "illegally" pass through the southern part of the Strait of Hormuz. Following the U.S.-Israel war with Iran, the IRGC claims that ships passing through the southern part of the Strait of Hormuz, near Oman's coast, is illegal. This Iranian claim is inconsistent with international law. The IRGC Navy's statement did not mention the tanker's name, crew, ownership, or the time of the incident. The military body also warned other military vessels that if they do not follow the "security regulations" of the Strait of Hormuz, they will "face no other fate." The IRGC statement was released after new U.S.-Iran military clashes.
