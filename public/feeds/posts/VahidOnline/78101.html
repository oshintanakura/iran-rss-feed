The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps Navy announced on Monday morning, August 31, that a "supertanker" caught fire with "massive blazes" and came to a halt after hitting "2 naval mines" while passing through the southern route of the Strait of Hormuz. The British maritime trade monitoring center had reported a ship in the Strait of Hormuz hours before the incident. In this statement, the IRGC threatened that ships that do not comply with the Islamic Republic of Iran's regulations on the Strait of Hormuz will be targeted. The IRGC statement was released hours after CENTCOM's strikes on missile launchers on Lark Island. @VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
