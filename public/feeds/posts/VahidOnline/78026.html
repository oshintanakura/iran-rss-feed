Ali Pishavarzadeh, one of those detained during the January protests in Rasht, has been sentenced to death by the city's Revolutionary Court. The committee following up on the situation of detainees reported that the Second Branch of the Rasht Revolutionary Court, headed by Judge Mohammad Ali Darvishgoftar, issued the verdict in the initial stage. Pishavarzadeh was detained during the protests on January 8 and 9 and is currently held in Lakan Prison in Rasht.@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
