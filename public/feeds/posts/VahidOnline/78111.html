The European Union announced on Monday that it will continue cooperating with the United States and other international partners and the G7 "to maintain pressure on Iran and help reduce tensions and regional stability." The statement said: "The European Union welcomes efforts to ensure that Iran ceases its destabilizing activities and engages in peace negotiations in good faith, including through further economic pressure, including the U.S.-led economic exclusion operation." "Economic Exclusion Operation" is the name U.S. government officials have given to the new economic pressure program against the Islamic Republic. The EU statement was issued ahead of the G20 summit hosted by the United States. U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told Reuters that at the meeting, he will ask finance ministers and central bank governors from around the world to cut their economic ties with Iran; otherwise, they will face U.S. secondary sanctions.@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
