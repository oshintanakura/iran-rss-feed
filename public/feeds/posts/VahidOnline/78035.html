Summary of statement: We discussed that negotiations should continue. Following the visit of Badr bin Hamad Al Busaidi, Oman's Foreign Minister, to Tehran and consultations with his Iranian counterpart Abbas Araghchi, the two countries issued a joint press statement regarding the resumption of safe navigation through the Strait of Hormuz. According to the statement, the foreign ministers of the two countries, emphasizing the preservation of their sovereignty and sovereign rights, discussed a phased and implementable framework to address the current situation in the Strait of Hormuz and the consequences of the recent war. The proposed framework includes establishing a temporary joint maritime passage through the Strait of Hormuz and implementing a joint project to clear the strait of mines. Under this agreement, technical negotiations between Tehran and Muscat will continue to achieve a permanent corridor, traffic management, information exchange, and the provision of maritime and security services. The two sides also emphasized the importance of joint talks with countries bordering the Persian Gulf, adherence to international law, and respect for the sovereign rights of coastal states. @VahidOOnLine 📡 @VahidOnline
