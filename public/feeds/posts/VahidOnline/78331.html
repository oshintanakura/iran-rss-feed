The American company "Anthropic" has announced that it identified and disrupted three operations linked to the Iranian government in which the "Claude" AI model was used to produce and disseminate propaganda content, design surveillance systems, and gather information related to targeting US naval forces. This company on Thursday, Shahrivar 19, in its latest threat intelligence report, described a set of cases of misuse of Anthropic's AI models. This report covers identified and disrupted activities from December 2025 to August 2026, and in addition to Iran, it also examined cases related to China, Russia, and other countries. According to this report, Anthropic identified and blocked accounts that used "Claude" to carry out influence operations aimed at affecting public opinion. Three of these operations were linked to elements aligned with the government of the Islamic Republic. Anthropic says each of these operations was carried out by an individual or group that either directly worked in an Iranian government propaganda institution or worked on behalf of such an institution.@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
