Amir Raisian, the lawyer for Hossein Shanbehzadeh, has announced the issuance of a first-instance verdict for his client in a new case. According to this verdict, Shanbehzadeh has been sentenced to one year in prison for "spreading false information with the intent to disturb public opinion." Raisian wrote on his X social media account that this case relates to a message Shanbehzadeh sent from prison to his friends on the occasion of his birthday. Criticizing the issued verdict, he wrote: "Just imagine a prisoner being sentenced to prison based on 'the manner of publication in a hostile media outlet in cyberspace.'" @VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
