The Judiciary of the Islamic Republic says it executed a "member" of the Jaish al-Adl group on Monday. Mizan, the news agency of the Judiciary, says that Omar Dorostaran was tried and sentenced to death on charges of "baghy" (armed uprising) and membership in Jaish al-Adl. Mizan says some of Mr. Dorostaran's charges relate to attacks carried out on the police headquarters of Rask County, the IRGC headquarters of Rask County, the Paroud base, and military headquarters in Farvardin 1403 in Chabahar County. According to this news agency, preparing accommodation for members of the group, facilitating the entry of these individuals into the country, moving operational teams to carry out armed attacks, procuring ammunition and weapons, and identifying military headquarters were among Omar Dorostaran's actions. According to human rights activists, Omar Dorostaran was 27 years old and a resident of the village of Cheraghan in Rask County in Sistan and Baluchestan, and was a student at the religious school of the city of Pishin and a former student of Mawlawi Naqshbandi, and had been imprisoned in Iran since Farvardin 1403.
