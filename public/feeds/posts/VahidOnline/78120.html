Axios reported, citing three American officials, that Donald Trump, the US President, and his senior advisers are considering conducting limited strikes in the Strait of Hormuz to prevent the Islamic Republic from rebuilding its radar and missile capabilities to attack ships. According to the report, this plan, which was prepared by US Central Command over the past week and supported by Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth, had not been approved by Trump before this weekend's exchange of fire with Iran. However, he may agree to it after the new escalation of tensions. One American official said the main idea of the plan is to reduce the risk of Tehran's attacks on oil tankers, US Navy vessels, and US Air Force aircraft; according to this official, the goal is to "mow the grass." One White House official said: "The President has all options on the table. The Iranians want to reach a deal, but they always come up a day late and a dollar short." Trump told Fox News on Monday evening that the US will respond to the Republic's attacks and will "hit them very hard." @VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
