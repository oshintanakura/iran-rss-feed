Various reports and videos have been published on social media about the "closure" of a number of fuel stations in Tehran and the formation of long queues in front of gas pumps. Some domestic media, including Khabar Online, the Student News Agency (ISNA), and Asr Iran, have also confirmed the closure of several gas stations in Tehran. Meanwhile, Fereydoun Yassemi, the Tehran district manager of the National Iranian Oil Products Distribution Company, while confirming the closure of several gas stations in Tehran, cited "a sudden increase in demand and traffic on access routes" as leading to "delays in the delivery of shipments and the depletion of gasoline at a limited number of stations, causing them to close for a few hours." According to him, gasoline distribution in Tehran has increased by "30 percent" in recent days. Yassemi claimed that "fuel supply to Tehran is being carried out sustainably." Agence France-Presse (AFP) also reported on Tuesday, August 25, from Tehran, about long queues forming in front of gas stations. @VahidHeadline 📡 @VahidOnline
