The US Secretary of Defense says announcing a new economic campaign against Iran does not mean the military option is off the table. Pete Hegseth, who spoke Monday evening after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's press conference, stressed that "we are by no means setting aside the option of using military strikes in the Strait of Hormuz or around Iran." The US Secretary of Defense also expressed the opinion that Iran cannot withstand the new economic pressure. On Monday, the US Treasury Secretary committed to depriving the Islamic Republic's economy of its vital resources during a press conference about the new pressure campaign against Iran. @VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
