Reports from websites announcing currency and gold rates in Iran show that the price of the US dollar fell on Wednesday, August 25, and returned to below 200,000 tomans. At the time of publishing this news, the dollar price was reported at 198,500 tomans, and the price of the gold coin known as "Emami" was 210 million tomans. This happens after several days of significant increases in foreign currency and gold prices in Iran. The US dollar price had risen to 205,000 tomans during these days.
