The Supreme Court has upheld the sentence of 12 years, six months, and one day in prison, confiscation of all assets, and a two-year ban from running cafés for Sadegh Saedinia, the manager of the 'Saedinia' chain of cafés. Mizan News Agency, the media arm of the judiciary, announced on Thursday, September 3 (12 Shahrivar 1405) that this sentence was issued due to Saedinia's support for the protests of January 2026 (Dey 1404) and the closure of the union units under this brand. According to the judiciary, Sadegh Saedinia was sentenced to 12 years, six months, and one day in discretionary prison and the confiscation of all his movable and immovable property in favor of the government on charges of 'media and propaganda activity against national security in favor of opposition groups.' The court also sentenced him to a two-year ban from café management after his prison term ends. The judiciary has cited posting protest content on Instagram, supporting calls for protests, closing the cafés and stores of the chain, and encouraging employees to participate in protests as instances of his charges.
