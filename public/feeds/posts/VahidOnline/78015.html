Oil prices fell more than two percent on Monday, ahead of the announcement of details of the new US plan to increase economic pressure on Iran. US President Donald Trump has described the plan as the "most crushing" financial operation against the Islamic Republic and has called on Washington's allies as well as China to join it. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is scheduled to provide more details of the plan at a press conference on Monday.
