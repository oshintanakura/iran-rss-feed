The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic, in response to the new round of US economic pressure, has called on countries around the world to refrain from implementing Washington's unilateral sanctions against Iran. On Friday, August 27, the ministry issued a statement calling America's "Operation Economic Outcast" "state terrorism" and claiming that Washington's new sanctions contradict the UN Charter and the principles of international law. In this statement, the Islamic Republic accused the United States of using the role of the dollar in the international financial system to pressure other countries to cut economic ties with Iran. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic considered this action a violation of the national sovereignty of countries and the principle of sovereign equality of states. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic also cited UN General Assembly resolutions on the prohibition of intervention in the internal affairs of countries and the principles of friendly relations among states, stating that governments should not recognize the effects of US unilateral sanctions or participate in their implementation. In another part of this statement, Tehran described the new US sanctions as a continuation of the "economic war" against the Islamic Republic and claimed that these measures are aimed at imposing pressure and economic harm on the Iranian people. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Islamic Republic also criticized the United Nations and its member states for what it called "complacency" in the face of US and Israeli actions. This position was taken after the United States announced on Monday, August 23, the launch of a new campaign called "Operation Economic Outcast" against the Islamic Republic. The stated goal of this campaign is to intensify pressure on Iran's economic relations with other countries through threats of secondary sanctions and restrictions on access to the US financial system.
