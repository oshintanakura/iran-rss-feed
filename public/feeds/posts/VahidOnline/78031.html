The price of the dollar in Iran's free market reached 205,000 tomans on Tuesday, August 25, 2026, and the Emami coin was traded at 224 million tomans; new records that show the continued fall of the rial's value and increased turmoil in Iran's financial markets. According to the announced prices, the British pound also reached 279,000 tomans. The dollar was around 186,500 tomans at the beginning of the week and crossed the 200,000-toman mark for the first time on Sunday. Accordingly, the dollar's price has increased by nearly 10 percent in a few days. The Emami coin, which was traded at around 191 million tomans at the beginning of the week, has reached 224 million tomans, an increase of more than 17 percent. The surge in currency and gold prices occurred one day after the announcement of a new sanctions package by the United States against the Islamic Republic. @VahidHeadline 📡 @VahidOnline
