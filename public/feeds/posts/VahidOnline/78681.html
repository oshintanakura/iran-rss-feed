The Human Rights Activists in Iran collective announced in a report marking the World Day Against the Death Penalty that over the past year, 2,003 people were executed in Iran, at least 69 of whom faced political and security charges. The number of executions during this period rose by about 29 percent compared to the same period previously, climbing from 1,553 to 2,003 — the highest figure recorded across 10 reporting periods. According to the report's statistics, executions increased from 505 in the 2016–2017 period to 2,003 in the 2025–2026 period. Broken down by type of charge, 57.56 percent were for murder and 36.25 percent for drug offenses.
