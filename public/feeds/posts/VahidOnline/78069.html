Informed sources have told US media that John Ratcliffe, the director of the US Central Intelligence Agency (CIA), during his secret trip to Moscow, asked Russian intelligence officials not to provide Tehran with information that could help Iran. It is also said that the issue of Russia's support for Iran was one of the main topics of his talks with the Russian side. This meeting is considered the first publicly revealed visit of a CIA director to Moscow since 2021. The Kremlin has confirmed that Mr. Ratcliffe met with Russian intelligence officials, but did not meet with Vladimir Putin, the Russian president. According to reports, in addition to the Iran issue, John Ratcliffe also discussed US concerns about the security of NATO member countries with Russian officials.
