Two informed sources about Marco Rubio's, the US Secretary of State, talks with officials from various countries have told Israel's Channel 12 that Washington currently does not expect new offensive attacks against Iran, and the Trump administration's focus has shifted to increasing economic pressure on Tehran and securing shipping in the Strait of Hormuz. According to these sources, Rubio has not ruled out the possibility of US military action if Iran resumes conflict. This shift in approach coincides with the imposition of new sanctions against the Islamic Republic and Donald Trump's claim about clearing the Strait of Hormuz of naval mines. According to this report, the Trump administration intends to increase economic pressure on Iran at the current stage and prepare the conditions for normalizing the passage of ships through the Strait of Hormuz.
