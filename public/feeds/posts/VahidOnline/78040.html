Israel's Channel 12 reported on Tuesday, August 25, citing two informed sources, that U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has informed officials of various countries that Washington does not currently expect new offensive attacks against Iran, and that the Trump administration's focus is on increasing economic pressure and normalizing shipping in the Strait of Hormuz. Rubio, however, did not rule out the possibility of military action if the Islamic Republic of Iran initiates a new round of hostilities. According to the report, this approach will continue at least until the U.S. midterm elections in early November, after which the option of a broader military operation may be reconsidered. This report comes amid increasing U.S. sanctions pressure and American efforts to increase ship traffic in the Strait of Hormuz.
