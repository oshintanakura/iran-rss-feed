JD Vance, the US Vice President, said in an interview with Newsmax that despite the Islamic Republic's efforts to close the Strait of Hormuz, the US has managed to move between seven and 15 million barrels of oil per day through this route. Vance said Washington is trying to prevent an energy crisis that, according to him, the Islamic Republic is seeking to create. He added that one of America's most powerful tools is "forcing Tehran to pay the cost of trying to choke off the oil and gas trade." The US Vice President stressed that the Islamic Republic does not have the ability to cut off international trade routes, and this reduces Tehran's leverage. The US Vice President said Washington has multiple tools at its disposal to deal with the Islamic Republic, which he said are some "decisive" and others economic. Vance also stressed that the first and fundamental goal of the US presence in the Middle East is to prevent the Islamic Republic from obtaining nuclear weapons. @VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
