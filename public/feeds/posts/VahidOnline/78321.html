Rafael Grossi, Director General of the International Atomic Energy Agency, announced on Thursday, September 19, in an interview with Bloomberg that the agency has observed construction activity at the highly fortified "Pickaxe Mountain" site south of Iran's main enrichment complex, based on satellite imagery. Grossi, noting that the agency's inspectors have not yet managed to inspect inside these deep tunnels, said: "There are signs of life in the activity around this construction site, but there is no precise information about what is happening inside it." He recalled that Iran had previously declared its intention to move equipment under the mountain to "protect it against attacks." These remarks come following the referral of Iran's nuclear file to the United Nations Security Council. According to reports, the agency has been unable to verify the status of highly enriched uranium stockpiles since June 2025, after U.S. and Israeli military strikes on Iran's nuclear facilities. Donald Trump, President of the United States, once again referring to this underground site, warned against any action by Iran and said at a campaign rally: "We have noticed minor activity at Pickaxe Mountain. I advise Iran not to make a wrong move, because we will be forced to strike them very hard."
