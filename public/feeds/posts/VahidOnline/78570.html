Iran's judiciary announced it executed two people early Wednesday who had been arrested during last year's Dey protests in Mashhad. According to the judiciary's media center, Ali Hemmati Sistanian and Majid Nikandish were executed after the Supreme Court upheld their sentences. The judiciary had accused them of involvement in the killing of four security personnel in an area of Mashhad. The judiciary's statement claimed the two defendants confessed during interrogation and in court to attacking a chain store, setting it on fire with Molotov cocktails, setting fire to a bank, and destroying public property. No information has been released about the legal proceedings, the defendants' access to a lawyer of their choosing, or the conditions under which the confessions were obtained.
