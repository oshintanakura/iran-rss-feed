The court also sentenced her to two to five years in prison, deprivation of certain government services, and confiscation of property. The death sentence of Soudabeh Ebrahimi Shamsabadi was communicated to her lawyer on the first of Shahrivar. According to information received, Ebrahimi Shamsabadi was held in solitary confinement for 20 days during her detention and was under intense pressure during interrogation. Her family had no information about her place of detention and her condition during this time.
