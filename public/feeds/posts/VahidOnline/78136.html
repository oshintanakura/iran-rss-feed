Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, the Speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly, in a video message on Tuesday, emphasizing that a naval blockade is considered a military action under international law, said that if they intensify the blockade, we will definitely respond militarily and everyone will lose.
Ghalibaf said: "If the enemy's will is that we do not export oil from the Persian Gulf, no one will be able to export oil."
He said: "America wants to cross from the southern route of the Strait of Hormuz, contrary to the memorandum of understanding, which we will not allow."
The Speaker of the Parliament added: "The enemy is currently focused on the psychological aspect of the economic war."
Ghalibaf said that the enemy, after "failing in the military and diplomatic arenas," turned to economic and cognitive warfare and added it to its military war.
Ghalibaf added: "The enemy's goal in the hybrid war is to start unrest inside the country, along with assassinations and short military attacks."
