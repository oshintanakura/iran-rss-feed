Ejei threatened protesters with a firmer crackdown. Following the escalation of the economic crisis and the U.S. dollar reaching 210,000 tomans, the head of the Islamic Republic's judiciary said the institution is "more decisive than ever" in punishing "elements who want to disrupt the country's security." This threat came after government agencies predicted public protests. Gholamhossein Mohseni Ejei added that strengthening security and decisively confronting anti-security elements are matters on which the public and officials agree. This approach has been intensified by a written message from Mojtaba Khamenei during Government Week.
