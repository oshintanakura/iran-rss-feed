Donald Trump, the US President, by posting an image on the social network Truth Social, compared the total volume of oil and liquid gas that passed through the Strait of Hormuz before the war with its current level and wrote: "The volume of Hormuz oil is back!"
In this image, Trump considered the total volume of oil and liquid gas passing through the Strait of Hormuz before the war to be about 20 million barrels per day and declared the current flow of these liquids to be 18 million barrels.
This is while the global tanker tracking system has reported the average flow of oil and liquid gas over the past month at 7.54 million barrels per day.
According to this system's data, the maximum daily flow has been 10 million barrels.
