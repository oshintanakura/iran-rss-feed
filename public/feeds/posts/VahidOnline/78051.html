Donald Trump, in an interview with Al Jazeera on Wednesday, August 25, stated that both economic measures and military options are "effective" and that he is in "no hurry" regarding negotiations with Iran. In response to questions from Tanya Nouri, the network's correspondent, he added: "I have no timeline; there's no rush."

Donald Trump, the US President, said in an interview with Glenn Beck's radio program on Wednesday, August 25, that he does not think Mojtaba Khamenei, the leader of the Islamic Republic, has been killed. The US President stated: "He was severely wounded; the left side of his body, his arm, his leg, all those parts were badly damaged." Trump also added that even if Mojtaba Khamenei were dead, the Islamic Republic would put on a "good show." Trump said: "The Islamic Republic still talks about referring to their leader for final approval on various matters." The US President also added that reaching an agreement with the Islamic Republic is not easy and they are "not very committed to principles."
