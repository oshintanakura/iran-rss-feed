Reza Zangeneh, a 27-year-old from the village of Kalilabad in Malayer, residing in Karaj, has been sentenced to execution on charges of "moharebeh" (enmity against God). His case has now been sent to the Supreme Court for review of the appeal, and if the sentence is upheld, this prisoner will face the risk of execution. According to received information, Reza Zangeneh returned from Malayer to Karaj on April 2, and the next day, April 3, when he had opened his shop, agents raided his workplace and arrested him. A number of shopkeepers and local business owners witnessed his arrest. Zangeneh is a repairman for luxury and foreign cars and is currently held in Qezel Hesar prison in Karaj. He has had a court-appointed lawyer since the beginning of the case, but his family has also introduced a lawyer of their choice to pursue the case during the judicial proceedings. @Tavaana_TavaanaTech 📡 @VahidOnline
