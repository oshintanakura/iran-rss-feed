The General Staff of the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic claimed in a statement on Monday that the United States is using the airspace or territory of some Middle Eastern countries to target Iran and warned that it will target them. The statement was released hours after Donald Trump, the US President, announced that the US military would hit Iran very hard in retaliatory strikes for Iran's attack on American forces in Jordan the previous night. In its statement, the General Staff of the Iranian Armed Forces said, "While respecting the national sovereignty of neighbors," if US attacks continue, the Islamic Republic's military forces will give a "heavier response" than last night's attacks. The US military announced that on Sunday evening it targeted "IRGC mine-laying forces" on Lark Island. Following this attack, the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps reported missile strikes on two military bases in Jordan. @VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
