Donald Trump, the President of the United States, said the Islamic Republic was probably responsible for the airstrike on Saudi Arabia that led to the closure of the East-West pipeline. On Saturday in Dublin, in response to reporters' questions about Iran's responsibility, he said: "I think they're responsible, it's probably them." Trump added that he had spoken with Mohammed bin Salman, the Crown Prince of Saudi Arabia, and called him a "good friend." The US President also said the Houthis aligned with the Islamic Republic had contacted his administration and stated they do not want the US to become directly involved in the conflict.
