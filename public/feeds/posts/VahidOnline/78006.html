The judiciary of the Islamic Republic has announced the execution of "Majid Adineh," another detainee from the protests of Dey 1404 (January 2026) in Karaj. According to a Tasnim report citing the judiciary, the sentence was carried out on the morning of Monday, Shahrivar 1, 1405 (August 23, 2026). Majid Adineh had been arrested on Dey 19, 1404 (January 9, 2026) in the Mohammadshahr area of Karaj. Judicial officials have claimed that at the time of his arrest, he was carrying a semi-automatic pistol, three magazines, 30 rounds of ammunition, two electric stun guns, two canisters of tear gas, a cordless saw, and a bottle of gasoline. The judiciary has described the Dey protests, according to the Islamic Republic's official narrative, as a "coup" and accused Adineh of cooperating with the United States, Israel, and what it called "hostile groups." The Revolutionary Court of Karaj had sentenced him to death and confiscation of assets on charges of "moharebeh (enmity against God) through intentional arson" under the so-called law on "aggravating punishment for espionage and cooperation with the Zionist regime and hostile countries." No information has been published about Adineh's access to a lawyer of his choice, the judicial process, the timing of the trial, or how his statements were obtained. The execution of Majid Adineh follows the ongoing implementation of death sentences against detainees from the Dey protests. On Mordad 21, 1405 (August 12, 2026), more than 30 countries issued a joint statement condemning the continued issuance and execution of death sentences against Iranian protesters as a tool to "silence the voices of opponents."
