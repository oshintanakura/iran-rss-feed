❇️Mother of two slain protesters arrested in Lahijan 🔹 The mother of two victims of Iran's protests, Nozhat Mirrazi, known as "Maman Nozhat," the mother of Ali and Emad Shoosh, two of the victims of Iran's nationwide protests, was arrested on Friday, September 4, in Lahijan and transferred to an unknown location.🔹 Security forces arrested Nozhat Mirrazi while no information has yet been published about the arresting agency, her place of detention, or possible charges against her.🔹 The arrest of this justice-seeking mother came one day after she released a video to mark the birthday of one of her slain children, Emad Shoosh. Ms. Mirrazi's arrest coincided with increased security pressure in Gilan Province and the prevention by intelligence and law enforcement forces of a birthday ceremony for Houman Sabbagh at his grave in Lahijan.🔹Nozhat Mirrazi has lost two of her children during two rounds of Iran's nationwide protests. Ali Shoosh, a poet, theater actor, and musician from Lahijan, died during the "Woman, Life, Freedom" nationwide protests in 2022. Hengaw says he was killed by government forces during protests in Isfahan.🔹Emad Shoosh, Ali's brother, was also an active member of justice-seeking families and, according to reports, had a history of arrest during the 2022 protests.🔹 Emad Shoosh died on January 8, 2026, during protests in Lahijan after being directly shot by government forces and hit by four bullets.🔹In the past week, Jafar Panahi had also visited the mother of this family. @MahmoudianMehdi
