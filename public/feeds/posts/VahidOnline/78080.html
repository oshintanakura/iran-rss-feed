Donald Trump, the President of the United States, announced on Truth Social that America has reached an agreement with Venezuela, which he called "the biggest oil deal in the history of the world." Trump said that under this agreement, with the participation of the private sector, the US will take control of the majority of more than 65 billion barrels of Venezuela's proven oil reserves without imposing any cost on American taxpayers. He added that Marco Rubio, the US Secretary of State, and Pete Hegseth, the US Secretary of Defense, along with Delcy Rodríguez, the interim President of Venezuela, played a role in reaching this agreement. Trump said this deal will more than double America's oil reserves, significantly increase oil supply, and in the long term help lower gasoline prices for Americans. @VahidOOnLine Marco Rubio, the US Secretary of State, on Friday, referring to the new oil agreement between Washington and Caracas, announced that this deal, in addition to ensuring stable reserves and reducing gasoline prices in the US, will bring nearly $100 billion in private sector investment to Venezuela. Rubio wrote on X: "For the people of Venezuela, this agreement will bring nearly $100 billion in private sector investment, support thousands of high-paying jobs, and will be the driver of rebuilding Venezuela's economy." @VahidOOnLine 📡 @VahidOnline
