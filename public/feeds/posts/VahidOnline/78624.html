The Islamic Republic has executed Alireza Sepahi, one of those detained during the Dey 1404 protests.
- The "Mizan" news agency, affiliated with the judiciary of the Islamic Republic, reported that Alireza Sepahi Badjani, known as Alireza Sepahi, was executed at dawn on Monday, 13 Mehr 1405, in Dastgerd Prison in Isfahan.
- Alireza Sepahi's lawyer had warned the previous day of the imminent danger of his execution, announcing that Sepahi's family had been summoned to meet with him and that he had been transferred to solitary confinement.
- Before his execution, Alireza Sepahi married his fiancée by telephone. Mahshad Kashani, a 22-year-old student living in Isfahan, was also arrested during the Dey 1404 protests and sentenced to five years of discretionary imprisonment; she is being held in the women's prison in Dolatabad, Isfahan.
- Alireza Sepahi had been scheduled to be executed at dawn on Tuesday, 6 Mordad 1405, alongside Abolfazl Sepahi Badjani — his cousin — and Amirhossein Safari Hosseinabadi, in Malek Shahr, Isfahan, in a public execution, but he suffered a heart attack from stress before the sentence could be carried out, and his execution was postponed.
- Alireza Sepahi is the fourth citizen arrested during the Dey 1404 protests to be executed over the past week, following the issuance of a statement by 46 countries condemning executions in Iran. Siavash Jamshidi Kheirabadi was executed on Saturday, 11 Mehr, in Shahrekord, and Ali Hemmati Sistani and Majid Nikandish were executed on Wednesday, 8 Mehr, in Mashhad.
- The case known as the "Midan Alikhani" case is connected to the protests on the evening of 18 Dey 1404, which took place in the area of Midan Alikhani, between Malek Shahr and Kaveh in Isfahan. The security institutions of the Islamic Republic claimed that four Basij and special unit forces were killed during these protests.
- With the execution of Alireza Sepahi, six defendants in the "Midan Alikhani" case have now been executed. Erfan Esfandiari and Golmohammad Mohammadi were executed on 28 Tir in prison. Abolfazl Sepahi and Amirhossein Safari were hanged in public on 6 Mordad in "Midan Alikhani," and Ghaem Hosseini was executed on 29 Mordad in the Central Prison of Isfahan (Dastgerd).
