📝 What do we know about the attack on the wedding ceremony in Sirik?
🔹Coinciding with US airstrikes on Sirik county on the night of September 1, 2025 (10 Shahrivar 1405), an explosion destroyed a house in Bandar Kohestak where a wedding ceremony was being held. According to published reports, so far five people, including a four-year-old child, have been killed and 65 people injured.
🔹Images from the scene, the sound of multiple explosions in the CCTV video, CENTCOM's statement, and the fact that the attack was not denied by its spokesperson strengthen the attribution of that night's attacks to the US.
🔹Meanwhile, on social media, it was claimed that the house explosion was the result of an "unsuccessful IRGC missile launch"; however, no official report or credible evidence has confirmed this claim so far.
🔹Some accounts have shared old or unrelated videos to prove this claim. The only similar report about an unsuccessful IRGC launch that same night pertains to Khomein in Markazi province and has no connection to Sirik in southern Iran.
🔹Despite evidence pointing to a US attack on Sirik, it is still unclear exactly what projectile struck the house where the wedding was held.
🔹Meanwhile, in the CCTV video, the sound of a drone can be heard, and the bride's father also mentions hearing drones in a video interview; evidence that strengthens the possibility of a simultaneous use of missiles and drones in the operation.
🔹Meanwhile, fragments of a SLAM-ER cruise missile have been seen in the area, but the extent of the house's destruction does not seem consistent with the full detonation of this missile's 360-kilogram warhead.
🔹It is possible that the house was targeted with smaller munitions (for example, a Lucas drone with a warhead of about 18 kilograms), and the SLAM-ER fragments may be related to another strike in the same area (a telecommunications tower about 130 meters away).
