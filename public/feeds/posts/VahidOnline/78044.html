John Healey, the UK's Defense Secretary, announced that his government, alongside the US and other partners, will continue to apply economic pressure on the Islamic Republic of Iran and will take action to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and counter "Iran's dangerous activities." Healey, on August 25, 2026, said in a statement that the UK government has imposed more than 240 sanctions on Iran since it took office; sanctions that, he said, were imposed in response to actions threatening the security of the people and the UK. The UK Defense Secretary added that London is determined to prevent the Islamic Republic from using the global economy or the UK's financial system to advance its nuclear program and destabilizing activities. He also supported US efforts to reach a diplomatic solution and said the UK welcomes increased pressure on the Islamic Republic, including through the US "economic exclusion" operation. Healey stressed that the UK will continue to cooperate with its partners to protect its interests and will take necessary actions to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and counter what he called Iran's dangerous activities in the region. The UK Defense Secretary called on the Islamic Republic to halt its destabilizing activities in the region, including in the Strait of Hormuz, and to enter diplomatic talks. @VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
