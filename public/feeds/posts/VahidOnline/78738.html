Fourteen political prisoners signed a statement warning of the risk that a death sentence could be handed down against Amirhossein Mousavi, a social media activist known as "James Bidin." In the statement, released on Thursday, 23 Mehr, the political prisoners pointed to the role of security agencies and IRIB in fabricating a case against Amirhossein Mousavi and announced that after his forced confessions were broadcast on television, the number of charges against him rose from five to 9. Among the charges added to Mr. Mousavi's case are "moharebeh" and "corruption on earth," which could put him at risk of a death sentence. The signatories of the statement stressed that the heavy charges now threatening this prisoner's life "essentially did not exist in his case before the IRIB report was broadcast."
