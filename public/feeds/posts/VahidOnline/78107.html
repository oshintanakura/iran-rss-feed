Donald Trump, the President of the United States, on Monday, August 31, officially called the Islamic Republic of Iran a "failed state" on his social network "Truth Social" and called for the international prosecution of its leaders. Trump described the state of Iran's economic and military structures as "total collapse" and wrote: "Iran no longer has a navy or an air force; their currency is lost, soldiers and police are not being paid, and inflation has reached 300 percent. Their leadership is in absolute chaos and incapable of running the country." The U.S. President, accusing Tehran of bloody suppression of internal protests, added: "The only thing they know how to do is kill their own protesters, with the death toll now exceeding 100,000.
