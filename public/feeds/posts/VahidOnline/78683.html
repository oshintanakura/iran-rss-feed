Saudi Arabia's General Authority of Civil Aviation confirmed on Saturday, 18 Mehr, that operations at King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh have been suspended following an attack on the airport that wounded a number of people. A diplomatic source told Agence France-Presse that Yemen's Houthis attacked the airport with a missile at around three in the afternoon local time. According to initial reports, dozens of people were wounded in the attack. The Wall Street Journal also reported, citing Saudi officials, that missiles fired by the Houthis struck Terminal 3 of the airport directly. A hospital source told Agence France-Presse that five of the wounded are in intensive care. An eyewitness also said that while at Gate 403 of the domestic flights terminal, he saw a missile strike the area of Gate 401 and passengers being wounded. The witness told Agence France-Presse: "I saw Gate 401 being hit and people wounded before my eyes. Everyone was fleeing." Saturday's attack came after Riyadh airport had already been targeted by Houthi attacks on Wednesday and Thursday. Those attacks left a total of four dead and several wounded, and caused serious damage to a passenger plane belonging to a Saudi airline. Three Saudi citizens, including a pilot, were killed in Thursday's attacks, and one Sudanese citizen in Wednesday's attack. The Houthis have claimed responsibility for the attacks.
