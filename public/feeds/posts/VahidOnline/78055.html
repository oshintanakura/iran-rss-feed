Leila Abolhassani, one of those detained during the December 2025 protests, has been sentenced to execution on charges of "moharebeh" (enmity against God), and her case is currently under review by the Supreme Court after an appeal against the verdict. Leila Abolhassani, approximately 43 years old and mother of two teenagers, has been held in Isfahan's Doulatabad prison since January 8, 2026. A source has said that Abolhassani was arrested on January 8 in Shahin Shahr while taking photos of a fire at one of the "Ofogh Koroush" stores. According to this source, the judiciary has accused her of involvement in setting fire to this store; an accusation that led to the death sentence against her. Currently, the Supreme Court is reviewing her appeal against the death sentence. Since her arrest, Leila Abolhassani has spent more than seven months in Isfahan's Doulatabad prison. @VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
