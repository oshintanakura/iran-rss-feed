The United States military announced in a video released on Thursday, August 26, that American forces have cleared naval mines from the Strait of Hormuz and that international shipping lanes are open. Rear Admiral Brad Cooper, commander of US Central Command (CENTCOM), emphasized in a video message posted on social media that "today, international shipping lanes are open and traffic is increasing." Referring to the mine-clearing operation in the Strait of Hormuz, Cooper said: "The conditions were, shall we say, challenging and dangerous. But we got the job done." Earlier, Donald Trump, the US president, had also announced the clearing of the Strait of Hormuz from mines laid by Iran. However, the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, rejecting these statements, has repeatedly emphasized that the Strait of Hormuz remains closed. @VahidHeadline 📡 @VahidOnline
