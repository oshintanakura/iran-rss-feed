On Tuesday, September 1, following the start of a new round of US military attacks on military positions in Iran, Axios news agency considered this action as confirmation of its report about the US plan for continuous and periodic attacks on positions in cities along the Strait of Hormuz.
Axios news site, citing US officials, reported that Donald Trump and senior officials of his administration are examining plans for limited attacks in the Strait of Hormuz and surrounding areas. The main goal of these attacks has been stated as preventing the rebuilding of Iran's radar systems, air defense, and missile capabilities.
According to Axios, this plan, which was developed by the US Central Command (CENTCOM) and supported by Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth, seeks to curb Iran's recent efforts to threaten vessels and oil tankers.
