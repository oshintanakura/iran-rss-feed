At least 100 protesters across 13 provinces of Iran are at risk of execution. The organization "Human Rights in Iran" announced that at least 100 people arrested during the Dey protests in 13 provinces of Iran are facing execution sentences; the largest number of these individuals, 46 people, is in Isfahan province. According to the published list, after Isfahan, 22 people are in Tehran and Alborz provinces. Additionally, 10 people in Fars, seven in Razavi Khorasan, five in Markazi, three in Yazd, and two in Semnan are included on this list. In the provinces of North Khorasan, Gilan, Ardabil, Ilam, and Qazvin, one person each is also facing an execution sentence.
