On Monday, August 31, 2026, a number of job seekers in Gachsaran County gathered to protest the hiring process at the Lishtar Refinery. In the video released from the protest, security forces can be seen firing shots to disperse the protesters. Some reports also indicate that one person was injured during the shooting. The protest was held in opposition to the recruitment and hiring process at the Lishtar Refinery, which was recently inaugurated.@VahidHeadlineSecurity forces and police dispersed Arab youths protesting unemployment in front of the "Da'bal Khuzai" sugarcane company in Ahvaz with beatings and gunfire. In the video, a man filming says: "These youths are all hungry, no one hires us. No one values us. Each of them chased the youths with Kalashnikov rifles. We want work. The youths are hungry. We have nothing left. We want work." The human rights organization "Karoun" reported on Monday, August 31, 2026, that "the protesting youths and families, representing the residents adjacent to the company, gathered and stated their demands, saying that the Da'bal Khuzai sugarcane company operates on land of about 12,000 hectares, a significant portion of which belongs to the region and its native people. Nevertheless, despite the presence of local youths with academic degrees and various specialties, the company's management has prioritized hiring non-local workers, leaving the region's youth deprived of initial employment opportunities."@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
