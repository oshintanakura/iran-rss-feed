The United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations announced: A tanker was targeted by an unknown projectile 63 miles from the Saudi port city of Yanbu. The organization announced the time of the incident as Monday and noted that a part of the ship's deck caught fire due to the impact of the unknown projectile, but the crew is in full safety. The UKMTO also announced that no environmental damage has been reported so far as a result of this incident. The name and flag of the tanker have not been announced, and no group has yet claimed responsibility for the attack. Yanbu is Saudi Arabia's main energy export terminal on the Red Sea. Yemen's Houthis announced a maritime ban on Saudi and Saudi-affiliated ships on July 20, and since then have claimed responsibility for numerous attacks on tankers. @VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
