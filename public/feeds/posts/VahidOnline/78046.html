Source: @VahidOnline · 2026-08-26 13:51 UTC

Masih Shahverdi, a former player of Sepahan Club's youth teams, was hit by a military bullet fired by government forces during the January 8, 2026 protests in the "Khaneh Isfahan" area and lost his life. He was 19 years old and had only two months left until the end of his military service. Masih Shahverdi was shot in the right flank and kidney on the evening of January 8 in the Khaneh Isfahan area. The bullet caused severe internal bleeding. According to an informed source, the security atmosphere prevailing in the area and the conditions of that night prevented his friends from immediately transferring Masih to a medical center. After several hours, they took him home on foot. Masih Shahverdi died in his brother's arms around 1 AM. Despite his death, his family transferred Masih to the hospital because they still hoped he could be saved. According to received information, after examination, the medical staff stated that there was no active bleeding when he was transferred to the hospital and that his death had occurred earlier.
