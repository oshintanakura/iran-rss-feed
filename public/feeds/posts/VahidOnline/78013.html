'Atousa Jafari', a 27-year-old woman from Sanandaj, was murdered with knife blows in front of her family home on Sunday, August 23, 2026. Local media and the Kurdistan Human Rights Network have reported that Atousa Jafari was attacked and killed with multiple knife blows while leaving her house and before getting into her car to go to work. According to these reports, the perpetrator of the murder was Atousa Jafari's husband or ex-husband. Local sources have reported that she was killed with eight knife blows. Different accounts have been published about Atousa Jafari's marital status at the time of the murder. The Kurdistan Human Rights Network reported that she had separated from her husband two years ago and was living with her mother, but local media have written that Atousa had been referring to court over the past three years to separate from her husband, and her divorce request was not accepted. According to local sources, Atousa Jafari had been subjected to violence, beatings, and threats by her husband many times during this period. Once, as a result of the beatings, her arm was broken. The Kurdistan Human Rights Network wrote that Atousa Jafari was a postal office employee, held a master's degree in criminal law, and was an instructor and referee in the sport of 'Kung Fu Toa'. This is the second reported case of femicide in Kurdistan within a few days.
