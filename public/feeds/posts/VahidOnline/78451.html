The Tehran Prosecutor's Office announced it has filed charges and opened a judicial case against the organizers and those involved in holding a running race in Velayat Park. The Prosecutor's Office claims that "legal and Sharia standards were not observed" in this competition. The 10-kilometer race was held in the early hours of Friday, 27 Shahrivar, with both women and men participating. The publication of images showing a number of female participants without hijab turned the competition into a topic of discussion on social media. According to a Fars News Agency report, the organizers have stated that the race was held with a permit from the Ministry of Interior and the Tehran Province Athletics Board. The Tehran Athletics Board said that before the competition began, written commitments were obtained from participants to observe "hijab and Islamic norms."
