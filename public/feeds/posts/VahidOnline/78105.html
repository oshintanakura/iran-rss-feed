The foreign and defense ministers of Turkey, Saudi Arabia, and Pakistan, along with the military commanders of the three countries, held their first defense pact meeting, known as the Mecca Pact, in Istanbul on Monday, August 31. Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, and Turkey signed the agreement in Mecca on Friday, August 7. According to the three countries' statement, an armed attack on any of them will be considered an attack on all members; a principle clearly resembling Article 5 of the North Atlantic Treaty (NATO). Hakan Fidan explained on Saturday, August 8, in an interview with the state-run Anadolu Agency that the new coalition was not formed against Iran or any other country, and its purpose is to provide a general commitment to support the security of the three member states. On Monday, reports emerged about the possibility of seven other Arab countries joining the pact.@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
