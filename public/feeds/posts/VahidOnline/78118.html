Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs announced in a statement on Monday, August 31, that Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and Pakistan have agreed to establish a secretariat in Saudi Arabia under the framework of the "Joint Mecca Defense Pact." According to the statement, the secretariat will be headed by a Secretary-General from Pakistan for the first three years. In this regard, Turkey's Ministry of Foreign Affairs also announced that mechanisms for other countries to join the pact are under consideration.@VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
