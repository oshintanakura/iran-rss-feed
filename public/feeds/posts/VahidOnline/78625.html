The Islamic Republic has executed Alireza Raeisi, one of the detainees from the Dey 1404 protests.

- Alireza Raeisi was executed at dawn on Monday, 13 Mehr, alongside Alireza Sepahi, another detainee from the Dey 1404 protests, in Dastgerd Prison in Isfahan.

- Yesterday, some news sources reported that Alireza Raeisi's family had been summoned to Dastgerd Prison in Isfahan, and said that this political prisoner had been transferred to solitary confinement for the execution of his death sentence.

- Alireza Raeisi is the son of the daughter of the paternal uncle of the late Ramin Raeisi, one of those killed in the Dey 1404 protests. Ramin Raeisi was killed on 19 Dey by gunfire from government agents during the crackdown on protests. His body was handed over to his family on 28 Dey, and he was buried in "Bagh-e Rezvan" in Isfahan.

- Alireza Raeisi was arrested the day after Ramin Raeisi's burial. Alireza's family had no news of him for 20 days after their son's arrest. During those three weeks, he was subjected to the most severe torture and pressure to force a confession against himself, and was even threatened with an air injection.

- Alireza Raeisi and Alireza Sepahi are among the defendants in the "Meydan-e Alikhani" case in Isfahan, which is related to the protests on the evening of 18 Dey, and security institutions claimed that four Basij members and one Special Unit agent were killed during these protests.

- In the "Meydan-e Alikhani" case, 12 citizens were sentenced to execution. With the execution of Alireza Raeisi and Alireza Sepahi, the number of executed defendants in the "Meydan-e Alikhani" case has reached seven.
