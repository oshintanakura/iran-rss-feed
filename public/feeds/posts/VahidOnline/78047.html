Netanyahu: A diplomatic agreement cannot be reached with the "savages" ruling Iran. Benjamin Netanyahu, the Israeli Prime Minister, said on the evening of Tuesday, August 24, regarding the possibility of the US reaching a diplomatic agreement with the Islamic Republic that Israel does not oppose a "good agreement" in principle, but seriously doubts the possibility of reaching such an agreement with Tehran's rulers. During a speech, Netanyahu, referring to his conversation with Donald Trump, said: "I told him one option, of course, is reaching an agreement; a good agreement. We have no objection to that." He then added in a sharp tone: "But I doubt we can reach an agreement with that group, with those savages. I tell you: an agreement cannot be reached."
