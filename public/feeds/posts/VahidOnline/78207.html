Israel Katz, Israel's Defense Minister, announced on Thursday, September 3 (12 Shahrivar), at a Rosh Hashanah ceremony with Ministry of Defense staff that an attack by the Islamic Republic on his country would free Israel from all constraints, and it would target even energy infrastructure. Israel's Defense Minister said: 'We will target all national, military, and civilian infrastructure, including energy infrastructure, and will return Iran to the depths of the Stone Age and darkness.' Katz also added: 'Economic pressure and fear of an uprising and the fall of the regime may push the Islamic Republic to desperate actions.' He said: 'The regime of the Ayatollahs in Iran knows well why it does not attack Israel after we dealt them two heavy blows, destroyed the nuclear program, killed Khamenei, and severely damaged their strategic capabilities.'@VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
