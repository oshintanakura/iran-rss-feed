Saipa Automobile Manufacturing Company, on Thursday, September 3 (12 Shahrivar), a few days after the start of registration for its special sales plan, issued an official amendment significantly raising the approved prices of four offered products, citing 'increased costs of scrappage certificates for old vehicles and other legal registration fees.' According to the newly published table, the consumer prices of the 'Quick S' and 'Sahend S Dual-Fuel' each increased by 330 million tomans, reaching 1 billion 32 million 510 thousand tomans and 1 billion 123 million 688 thousand tomans, respectively. In the assembled and imported vehicle segment, the price of the 'Citroen C3-XR V1 version' increased by 115 million 500 thousand tomans to 3 billion 389 million 322 thousand tomans, and the price of the 'Changan CS55 Plus' jumped by 198 million tomans to 5 billion 819 million 12 thousand tomans. This is while applicants had registered based on initial rates in recent days, and now these products, with delivery times of 90 to 120 days, will be delivered at new rates. On Wednesday, September 2 (11 Shahrivar), the open market also saw a new wave of price increases, with several other domestic cars jumping in price. For instance, the automatic Tara broke records, rising about 100 million tomans to around 3 billion 75 million tomans. According to Fararu's report, on the same day, the automatic Dena Plus increased by 25 million to 3 billion 190 million tomans, the automatic Peugeot 207 Panorama reached 2 billion 980 million tomans, and products like the automatic Shahin Plus and dual-fuel Soren Plus were traded at price levels of 3 billion 30 million and 2 billion 410 million tomans, respectively.@VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
