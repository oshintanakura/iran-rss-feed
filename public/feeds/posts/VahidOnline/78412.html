Reuters reported on Wednesday, September 16 (25 Shahrivar), citing five informed sources, that United States officials met with representatives of the Iran-backed Houthi rebels last weekend (Saturday or Sunday).

According to the report, three of the sources, who asked not to be named, said the undisclosed meeting was held at the U.S. embassy in Muscat. Two other sources noted that the Omani government, as a long-standing regional mediator, helped arrange the meeting.

Donald Trump, after returning to power in 2025, placed the Houthis on the list of "Foreign Terrorist Organizations" and criminalized any support for the group.

Trump said on Saturday that the Houthis had phoned his administration and asked the United States to stay out of the Yemen war. Vice President JD Vance also stressed on Monday, without providing details, that the United States is in direct contact with the group.

Two informed sources said that at the meeting — which, according to one of them, was held on Sunday — the Houthis told American officials they do not intend to attack American vessels and are committed to the 2025 ceasefire with the United States.

One of the sources, a Yemeni, said the group also stated it will not attack Israeli ships or any other commercial vessels, except ships belonging to Saudi Arabia.
