Axios website, citing U.S. officials, reported that the U.S. military, during the wave of strikes on Tuesday night, September 1, on targets in southern Iran, also targeted "two state-owned oil tankers" of that country. According to the report, these two tankers were anchored near Iran's coast and north of the U.S. naval blockade line, and U.S. drones fired missiles at their engine rooms. The U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM), in its official statement after Tuesday night's strikes, did not specifically mention attacks on tankers, but in the video footage it released of the strikes, a scene of a missile hitting a tanker is also visible. Axios says this is the first time the U.S. military has targeted Iranian tankers not to prevent violations of the naval blockade, but in response to Iran's attacks on ships passing through the Strait of Hormuz. A U.S. official described the move as part of a new policy called "tanker for tanker," which, according to him, Donald Trump has approved to deter further Iranian attacks on ships.
