Scott Bessent, the United States Secretary of the Treasury, in a message posted on the social network X, criticized the Islamic Republic's financial policies and called for allocating the country's financial resources to the Iranian people. In his message, Bessent wrote: "While the Iranian people struggle to meet their basic needs, the corrupt regime continues to squander vast sums abroad." The US Treasury Secretary added: "Instead of funneling billions of dollars to its terrorist proxy groups, Iran's government should spend this money on its own people." These remarks come amid the intensification of the United States' financial sanctions campaign to restrict the Iranian government's access to foreign currency resources. @VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
