Asieh Ramezani, a 39-year-old woman and mother of two, was killed by direct fire from Islamic Republic forces during the suppression of the December 2025 protests in Tehran. Agents shot Ramezani from behind at close range in the early morning of Friday, January 9, 2026. The bullet passed through her back and chest, reaching her heart and killing her. Asieh Ramezani was the mother of a teenage daughter and an elementary school-aged son. Her family says that after the shooting, she was taken to a clinic but was left bleeding for about five hours without effective medical treatment. After losing track of her fate, Ramezani's family spent three days searching for her between the Kahrizak forensic medicine center and Behesht-e Zahra cemetery until they finally found her body. When the family found Ramezani's body, her cheek was bruised, and blood was still visible from under the cover in which the body had been placed. They said her body had been kept in "unworthy and painful" conditions. Ramezani's family also says her clothes, shoes, and other personal belongings were removed and not returned to them. After receiving the body, they realized that Ramezani's heart, which had been torn open by the bullet, had been stitched up without their knowledge. In their account, Asieh Ramezani's family wrote: "We will not forget those three days. We will not forget those five hours, that blood, that cover, that torn heart, and the belongings that should have been returned to her family." They emphasized that not all the facts surrounding her killing have yet been clarified, adding: "Even if a thousand years pass, the blood of our loved ones will not be washed away. We will repeat their names, keep their stories alive, and remain seekers of justice." @VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
