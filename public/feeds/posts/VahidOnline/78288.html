The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), in a statement addressed to the "people sent forth by Islamic Iran," announced that it has attacked Jordan's Al-Azraq base under the codename "Heidar Karrar." The statement said the attack targeted the location of fighter jets. Earlier, Sky News had reported the interception of missiles in Jordanian airspace. Syrian state television also reported that Syria's air defense intercepted some missiles fired from Iran over the Jordanian border city of Irbid. Some media outlets in Iran, including Hamshahri, have also said that the IRGC attacked Jordan with "cluster bombs." @VahidOOnLine 📡 @VahidOnline
