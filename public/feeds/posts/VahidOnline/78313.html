Following the escalation of tensions in the Middle East, the price of Brent crude oil surpassed $102 on Thursday, showing a rise of about one percent compared to the previous day and about eight percent compared to the beginning of the month. According to the U.S. Energy Information Administration's estimate, last month Iran's daily oil production fell by 800,000 barrels compared to July due to the reimposition of the U.S. naval blockade, but at the same time the intensification of the Islamic Republic's attacks on ships in the Strait of Hormuz and the start of Houthi attacks in the Red Sea and Bab al-Mandab on Saudi oil tankers also caused the average daily oil production of Arab countries in the region to decrease by 940,000 barrels last month compared to July. The total oil production of Iran and Arab countries in the region last month was 6.7 million barrels less than in the period before the Middle East war.@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
