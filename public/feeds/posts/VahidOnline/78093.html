Concurrent with Fars News Agency's report that American drone strikes on Lark Island were carried out "from Jordan with the support of that country's bases," the IRGC Public Relations Office announced in a statement early Monday: "In response to the attack on Lark Island, the Aerospace Force, in a combined missile-drone operation, targeted the technical and maintenance infrastructure and the deployment site of fighter jets at two airbases in Jordan, namely King Hussein and Al-Azraq, with ballistic missiles."
