Masoud Pezeshkian, the President of Iran, once again repeated his previous stance on the necessity of ending the war with the US, saying: "War is not always the solution. A knot that can be untied by hand should not be bitten open with teeth." On Wednesday, August 25, at a public ceremony, Pezeshkian once again called Iran the "victor of the field" while adding that this path can be traversed with "prudence and thought."
