Source: @VahidOnline · 2026-09-02 13:31 UTC

According to figures announced by Iran's National Iranian Oil Products Distribution Company, the average daily gasoline consumption in the first week of Shahrivar has exceeded 148 million liters. According to these figures, the highest daily demand in the first eight days of the last summer month was over 154 million liters, and in this period, more than 1.2 billion liters of gasoline were supplied in total. The sharp decline in production capacity in recent months due to U.S. attacks on Iran's oil facilities, on one hand, and the government's problems in importing gasoline from other countries, on the other, have led to higher gasoline prices and even the possibility of further price increases for this product, as well as a sharp rise in demand. President Masoud Pezeshkian and several other officials have stressed that the government does not have much capacity to import gasoline, especially to supply it at previous prices. However, the Iranian government also claims that the queues at some gasoline stations are due to excitement and psychological pressure, and there is no problem in supplying the country's needed gasoline.
