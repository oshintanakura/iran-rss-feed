The price of diesel in the US has reached a new record of more than five and a half dollars per gallon. The American Automobile Association announced on Friday, September 4, that the price of diesel in the country has now reached five dollars and 85 cents per gallon, while a year ago its price was three dollars and 71 cents. Each gallon is about 3.8 liters. The association attributed this price increase to disruptions in fuel transportation due to America's war with Iran. Diesel is a vital fuel used in road transportation, agriculture, and construction, and there are fears that its significant price increase will raise the inflation rate.
