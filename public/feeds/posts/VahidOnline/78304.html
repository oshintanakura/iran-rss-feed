The President of the United States said on Wednesday, September 9th, that in his view, the war with Iran will end "immediately" after the US midterm elections. Donald Trump told reporters before departing for Dallas to attend a Republican Party event: "I think the war will be over immediately after the elections. Because they (Iran) can no longer hold on." Trump added about Iran's situation: "They are desperate and trying to influence the elections." In response to a question about the widespread attacks by both sides around the Strait of Hormuz, Trump said: "The attacks were carried out by us. We hit nine of their oil tankers.
