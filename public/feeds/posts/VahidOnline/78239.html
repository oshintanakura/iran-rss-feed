The United States Department of State announced on Friday, September 4, its approval of the sale of five billion dollars worth of bombs, guidance kits, and other military equipment to Saudi Arabia. The department stated that this sale will strengthen Saudi Arabia's air defense capability to counter current and future regional threats and will increase the interoperability of the country's equipment with the systems of US forces and other Washington partners in the Persian Gulf. Saudi Arabia has been repeatedly targeted by missile and drone attacks by Iranian forces and Tehran-backed Houthis in Yemen since the start of the US-Israel war against Iran. The US State Department has informed Congress of this deal; this sale still requires approval from US lawmakers to be finalized.@VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
