While the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) announced in a statement early Monday morning, September 6, that it had targeted an unmanned vessel belonging to CENTCOM in the Strait of Hormuz, the US Army rejected this claim and called it a "blatant lie." Iranian state media had reported that this American unmanned vessel intended to enter an area of the Strait of Hormuz that Iran had declared off-limits. Captain Tim Hawkins, spokesperson for the US Central Command (CENTCOM), told the Associated Press that the IRGC's claim is a "blatant lie." @VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
