The price of Brent crude oil continued its downward trend on Thursday, reaching $86.75, following improved shipping traffic through the Strait of Hormuz and expectations of positive talks between Iran and Qatar. Oil prices fell by more than one dollar compared to Wednesday and about eight dollars compared to last week. Following the visits of Oman's Foreign Minister and Pakistan's army chief to Tehran in recent days, Esmail Baghaei, the spokesperson for Iran's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, announced on Wednesday that Qatar's Prime Minister would also visit Tehran soon. Meanwhile, Qatar's Foreign Minister, in a call with his Iranian counterpart, emphasized Doha's support for all diplomatic efforts and actions aimed at finding a solution to guarantee freedom of navigation and paving the way for a comprehensive agreement to establish lasting peace in the region. @VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
