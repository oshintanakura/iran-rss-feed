Iranian media have reported hearing the sound of several explosions near Kharg Island, Iran's most important oil export terminal, and the targeting of a small Iranian tanker. Tasnim News Agency reported that this tanker was "targeted" on Saturday morning, September 5, six miles from Kharg Island within the anchorage area. Tasnim says this targeting was carried out "with four projectiles by American forces." According to local sources, the incident caused no casualties, and crew members are in the process of evacuating the tanker. The Asr Iran website also reported four projectiles hitting the vessel. Fars News Agency had earlier announced that the sound of explosions was heard from the Persian Gulf area, but no sign of smoke was observed and the source of the sounds was unclear. Nour News also, citing local sources, published a report of "an American missile attack on an Iranian tanker," but called it unconfirmed.
