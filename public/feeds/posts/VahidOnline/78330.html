The Telecommunications Company of Iran, by publishing a notice in the Codal system (Comprehensive Information System of Listed Companies Accepted on the Stock Exchange), announced that the cost of landline-to-mobile phone calls will increase by 45 percent starting Friday, Shahrivar 20. According to Entekhab, based on this notice, the maximum cost of landline-to-mobile calls has increased from 625 rials to 906 rials. This change is being applied following the notification of the directive to increase the cost of landline-to-mobile calls, mobile-to-mobile calls, and SMS. The Telecommunications Company of Iran announced that the exact extent of the impact of this increase on the company's revenue is not yet clear, and its financial effects will be published in periodic reports. This company had also increased the monthly fixed cost of landlines by 45 percent in Khordad.
