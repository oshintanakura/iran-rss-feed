US Treasury Secretary: We are identifying and tracking the assets of the IRGC
Scott Bessent, the US Secretary of the Treasury, on Tuesday, September 1, on the sidelines of the G20 finance ministers and senior officials meeting, announced the intensification of Washington's economic pressure on Iran and said the US will likely sanction one bank this week and another bank next week.
Bessent said: "We will probably announce the sanctioning of one bank this week, and next week we will announce another one." He added that the US is in talks with its allies on this matter and has their support.
The US Treasury Secretary also said Washington is examining the sanctioning of aircraft leasing companies and other entities that do business with the IRGC.
He said: "These could be various entities. They could be aircraft leasing companies that we will examine. They could be anyone who does business with the IRGC. We are identifying and tracking the assets of the IRGC."
