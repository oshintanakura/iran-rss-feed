The executive deputy to the Iranian President confirmed that the Islamic Republic's government cannot import gasoline due to the American naval blockade. According to the IRNA news agency, Mohammad Jafar Qaempanah, on Wednesday evening, deemed changes in the prices of energy carriers, including gasoline and diesel, necessary, explaining: "Gasoline distribution is not fair, and continuing this path is impossible. Moreover, gasoline production is insufficient, and due to the American naval blockade, we cannot import gasoline." This Iranian government official also said it is unclear to what extent and when these changes will occur. In recent days, alongside increased statements by Islamic Republic officials about the need to raise gasoline prices, various reports have emerged of some fuel stations in Tehran closing and long queues forming in front of them. According to the latest announced figures, daily gasoline production in the country is about 115 million liters, while consumption is about 129 million liters. Thus, daily production is about 14 million liters less than consumption. @VahidHeadline📡 @VahidOnline
