Vance: I am skeptical about the possibility of the US role in the attack on a wedding ceremony in Sirik

🔸The Vice President of the United States says investigations into the "alleged attack on a wedding ceremony" in southern Iran are ongoing.

🔸JD Vance, who was answering reporters' questions at the White House on Thursday, September 3, said in response to a question on this matter: We still don't have enough information, but the US military, "unlike the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps," never targets civilians; but sometimes "mistakes" can happen.

🔸Donald Trump's deputy continued: The important point is that even if a mistake occurs, the US armed forces, "again unlike the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps," learn from their mistakes so that such mistakes are not repeated.

🔸Vance finally, emphasizing that the investigation is ongoing and information is not yet complete, said he personally is "skeptical" about the possibility of the US role in this incident.

🔸According to Iranian officials, in the attack on the evening of September 1 by the US on a wedding ceremony in Kohestak, Sirik, near the Strait of Hormuz, four people including a child were killed and dozens were wounded.

The US Department of Defense has also announced since February 28, 2026, regarding the attack on an elementary school for girls in Minab that it is investigating, but more than six months after the incident and despite pressure from Congress, it has still not been willing to release the results of the investigation.

🔸Officials of the Islamic Republic say that in the attack on the Shajareh Tayyebeh school, more than a hundred students, teachers, and family members of the students were killed.
