The Speaker of the Islamic Consultative Assembly said: Americans must fulfill their commitments so that we can take action to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, in a meeting with Hamas's Islamic relations official, said the Islamic Republic does not reject negotiations but views them as a "tool for struggle." He said abandoning the fight against America and Israel means defeat. He added that during negotiations, the Islamic Republic raised ending the war against Iran and its allies in the "Axis of Resistance" as the first article of the memorandum, while, according to him, the other side in the initial 15-article text had demanded a complete halt to missile, nuclear, and "Axis of Resistance" activities.
