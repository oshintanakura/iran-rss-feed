Hossein Shariatmadari, managing director of Kayhan newspaper, wrote in a note on Thursday, September 3 (12 Shahrivar), that the Army and the IRGC should use the 'lever' of international fiber-optic cables passing through the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz to 'confront America and its allies.' The managing director of Kayhan wrote: 'In the depths of the waters of the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz lies one of the international fiber-optic highways. A highway through which most internet communications, international calls, banking transactions, cloud services (iCloud), and even AI communications and data centers pass through these cables.' Hossein Shariatmadari, Khamenei's representative at Kayhan newspaper, emphasized: 'This is a word to the country's officials, especially the Army and the IRGC; look carefully! The global internet cables from under the waters of the Strait of Hormuz and the always-Persian Gulf are waving at us and saying in a thousand tongues, why have you forgotten our role in this war?'@VahidOOnLine📡 @VahidOnline
