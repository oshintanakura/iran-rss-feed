An official report by US government oversight bodies says the war with Iran has led to "strategic shortages" in the reserves of some advanced US weapons. The first official report by US government inspection bodies on "Operation Epic Fury," publicly released on Monday, 23 Shahrivar, says the extensive use of weapons in the war with Iran "has led to strategic shortages in inventories and exposed bottlenecks in the industrial base for resupplying munitions."
