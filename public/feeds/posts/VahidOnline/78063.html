The IRGC Intelligence Organization issued a statement on Thursday, warning of escalating social discontent. The statement referenced the failure of "Iran's enemies" in their "attempt to change Iran's government through military attacks," adding: "Opponents of the Islamic Republic are changing their strategy." This military and security body claimed that activating internal crises, psychological warfare, economic pressure, and security operations are key aspects of this shift in approach. In the statement, the IRGC Intelligence Organization warned of rising social discontent and the possibility of street protests, saying opponents of the Islamic Republic have focused on "disrupting stability and reducing national resilience" through "cognitive warfare and generating fear and ambiguity." The organization also announced the identification of what it called a "secret and dedicated structure" of Mossad, Israel's intelligence agency, for exerting pressure from within Iran, claiming this structure operates through contact with opposition groups, carrying out sabotage operations, and employing local agents. The statement claimed that the Islamic Republic has moved beyond a position of merely "responding" to attacks and seeks to increase its role in shaping the course of war and diplomacy. Part of the statement read: "Iran is no longer merely in a position of responding to the other side's attacks" and is moving toward "increasing strategic initiative and influencing the timing, location, and cost of war and diplomacy."
