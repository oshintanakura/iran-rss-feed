.Neemad: Mohammad Ghouchani, journalist and political figure, recently in a note titled "Masked Liberalism" claimed the end of the "religious intellectualism" movement, and through this, targeted Abdolkarim Soroush, the philosopher and one of the main figures of this intellectual current in Iran. In this note, he accused Mr. Soroush of "purging the university" and wrote that "he has succumbed to the wave of neo-Marxism" and that Soroush "speaks in the language of the left and explains the competitive free market system with the label of capitalism." Soroush Dabbagh, philosopher and psychologist, who himself is among the religious modernists, has responded to Mr. Ghouchani in this note published by Neemad. Neemad welcomes discussion on this matter. *** "Reference. To write honestly, given the turbulent and worrying situation of the country and in the midst of ceasefire, war, executions, runaway inflation, the fuel crisis... I preferred, as a member of the intellectual family, not to enter further into the dust that has recently appeared in the atmosphere, and to suffice with the two writings 'A Suitcase of Lies' and 'Shariati, Chandelle, and Being Muslim'. But the publication of the writing 'Masked Liberalism' led me to write the following lines. May it be of use to discerning audiences and those who monitor the intellectual developments of contemporary Iran. A few months after the controversial and contentious presidential election of June 2009, around the publication of the first issue of the new religious modernists, he has also severely criticized and attacked the leftist intellectual tradition from the perspective of liberal teachings and the free market economy.[1] Although I was not and am not in agreement with his attitude and method of analysis and arguments; nonetheless, I did not fault him for this, because theoretical-political housecleaning and revisionism is the right of any researcher, writer, and journalist. But, upon reading his recent writing, I realized that the story goes beyond this and has clear non-epistemic dimensions. It is of the nature of ethnic-tribal confrontation, mixing oil and water, labeling, and building files; that 'whoever is not killed is not of our tribe'. To justify my claim, I will address several clear errors and mistakes in the writing 'Masked Liberalism' and pass over the rest, as per the example: 'The rest of this poem, O noble minstrel, count in this manner, for this manner is my desire'..."
