An excerpt from the article "In the Spirit of Ludwig and Sohrab" by Soroush Dabbagh, in which he refers to a verse by Bidel Dehlavi: "Hope always pertains to the future. A person who lives in the present is neither hopeful nor hopeless. As Bidel Dehlavi, who had a significant influence on Sohrab Sepehri's poetry and thought, put it: In this gathering, hope and despair each have their own intoxication—I am glad that they made me drunk with the pain of being without quality. Whether one is hopeful or hopeless, each carries its own intoxication or trouble. The poet is content that he has fallen into a state of being without quality or without hope. Along the same lines, Wittgenstein emphasizes indifference toward the surrounding world. This attitude highlights not being dependent on external events and their misfortunes." https://t.me/Biddle_Academy
