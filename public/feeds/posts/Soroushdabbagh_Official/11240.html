Many friends and audience members have kindly been asking over the past week about the promised lecture series on Hafez's ghazals. For the benefit of these dear ones and other followers of my audio works and lectures, I am writing these lines. The first session of "In the Presence of Hafez" was held this Saturday at the "Open Society" Club. The audio file of this session is available for download to those interested through my Telegram channel and YouTube channel, as well as the Telegram channel of the "Open Society." The opening verse of the ghazal that I undertook to reread and recreate in this session is as follows: If the Sufi drinks wine in measure, may his drink be sweet; otherwise, may the thought of this act be forgotten! A continuation of the rereading of this ghazal remains, which, God willing, I will address in the next session. I truly benefited from the insightful observations and remarks of those present during the question and answer portion of the session. I intend, in these lecture series held every fifteen days, to undertake the rereading and narration of thirty ghazals from Hafez's exquisite ghazals, selected by myself, and to proceed together with the audience: "Let us see what move the pawn will make on the board."
