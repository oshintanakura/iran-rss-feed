Last autumn, my collection of forty Velvet Whispers, which I wrote over the course of one year between Aban 1403 and Aban 1404, was published along with an introduction and an afterword in the form of the book "Velvet Whispers: Existential Murmurs" and was made available to dear readers. After a six-month pause and silence, I resumed writing the whispers two months ago. During this time, I have written twelve whispers. If life permits, whenever the new whispers reach forty, they will be sent for publication in the form of the second volume of the book "Velvet Whispers."
