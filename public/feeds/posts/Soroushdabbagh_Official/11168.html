.Yesterday, the fortieth-day ceremony for my dear uncle, who has passed away, was held in Tehran. I am deeply grateful to all the friends and loved ones who, out of kindness, grace, and affection, offered their condolences over the past forty days. Despite my wish, unfortunately I was unable to respond to each and every one of the many condolence messages I received through Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp, Clubhouse, and email. I take this opportunity to sincerely thank all those who have remembered me and my family during this time. What strange times these are, dear ones! The hand of fate denied me the chance to attend my uncle's funeral, burial, memorial, and fortieth-day ceremony.... I pray from the bottom of my heart for greater resilience for my dear compatriots in facing all kinds of adversities, bitterness, and unfavorable winds that blow in these turbulent times in our beloved Iran. I would like to treat the audience to some profound verses from one of the exquisite ghazals of the wise, contemplative Shirazi poet: The outcome of the workshop of existence and time is nothing but this— Bring wine, for the means of the world are nothing but this. In the few days you have respite in this stage, Rest well, for time is nothing but this. O ascetic, do not feel secure from the play of jealousy, beware— For the path from the monastery to the tavern is nothing but this.
