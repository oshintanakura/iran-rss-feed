The next main risk is not a single retaliatory strike; it is the normalization of periodic attrition operations aimed at preventing Iran's capability rebuilding. The simultaneous message of "response to regional facilitators" and "return to understanding" is likely a combination of deterrence and keeping an exit route open; without a unified stance, it may be interpreted by Gulf states as a direct threat and by the US as a lack of negotiating authority. The fresh attack on the tanker shows that even without a second official US wave, the maritime insecurity cycle continues independently of diplomacy. The zeroing out of observable liquid tankers and the drop in US strategic reserves to about 286.6 million barrels further narrows the market's tolerance margin; however, no new and quantified supply disruption has yet been proven.
