The attached text is the complete translation of Document No. 116 from the official series Foreign Relations of the United States: Airgram A–56 from the U.S. Embassy in Tehran to the Department of State, dated February 22, 1971 / 3 Esfand 1349, on the subject "Youth in Iran: An Assessment of the Embassy Youth Committee." The document was originally classified Secret; Noforn. (Office of the Historian) This document reveals the depth of awareness/unawareness of the U.S. Embassy regarding dynamics related to the younger generation in Iran in the 1960s/1970s and is historically very important and valuable. Hossein-Ali Ashena
