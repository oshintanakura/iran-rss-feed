🔸️ "Narrative craft" (Ravayat-varzi) refers to the art of designing and orally presenting an idea, experience, or story in the form of a coherent narrative in the public sphere. Narrative craft is a form of rhetoric; an attempt to express one's world, connect with others, convey experience, persuade, and even entertain the audience. 🔹️Narrative craft is not just public speaking, and its main difference from public speaking lies in its reliance on "narrative"; that is, turning an experience, issue, or idea into a narrative, living, and impactful form. ✳️ This social skill is now used in various fields: from education, media, and content production to social action, entrepreneurship, management, policy-making, cultural activity, idea presentation, teaching, negotiation, personal branding, and even everyday life. 🔰 In the 12-hour "Narrative Craft" workshop with Dr. Milad Dokhanchi, we will learn the basics and main components of narrative craft; from narrative structure and tension to performance, rhythm, and audience connection. 🔆 Then each participant will work on their own idea and, with the help of the group, rewrite and refine their narrative.
