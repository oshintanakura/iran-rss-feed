Ghaf like Gharbzadegi (Weststruckness)! 🔹️Few books in Iran's contemporary history have created as many supporters and opponents as Jalal Al-e-Ahmad's "Weststruckness," but usually such texts have been read far less than the talk and rumors surrounding them; we want to read it once carefully, historically, and critically. 🔸️In the re-reading course of "Weststruckness," the text itself will be the main focus of discussion; but we will not separate the text from its historical, philosophical, and political context; and we are not going to make Jalal Al-e-Ahmad a sacred or outcast figure. We want to re-read one of the most influential books in the history of Iranian intellectualism, which happens to have a lot to say for us today.
