🧩 Design Thinking; From Idea to Execution
Every idea needs more than just creativity to become a successful product; you need to see the problem correctly, develop the idea, turn it into a product, and take concrete steps to execute it.
In the "Design Thinking; From Idea to Execution" workshop, you will work on real challenges and ideas using design thinking tools and techniques, and experience the journey of turning an idea into a product up close.
By the end of the workshop, you won't just be a listener; you'll leave with an Action Plan to continue your idea or project.
