◼️Condolence message from Dr. #Reza_Soleyman_Heshmat on the passing of Dr. Mohammad Reza Rikhtehgaran
The late Dr. Mohammad Reza Rikhtehgaran was a highly virtuous person with a remarkable inner calm and tranquility. He learned a great deal from the late Professor Seyyed Ahmad Fardid and Professor Davari Ardakani, especially in the fields of art and poetry. He also spent time in India researching ancient texts and the culture of the subcontinent, and upon his return, he took on the teaching of the course on wisdom in Asia and the East at the University of Tehran. He was deeply committed to contemplative thought and the philosophical and wisdom traditions in the history of Eastern and Western philosophy. If he leaned toward one side in matters of belief and politics, he never abandoned fairness and moderation.
One of my unforgettable memories with him—after which I never had the chance to see him again—was when, at the invitation of Dr. Ayatollahi, it was arranged for me to speak on "Paradoxy" in Hafez, Kierkegaard, and Nietzsche at the House of Humanities Thinkers, and he was to serve as the discussant. It was a good session. Afterward, he called and expressed his kindness and appreciation. What I remember is that I said the core point, of course in Derrida's terms, goes back to Heidegger's two-fold dialectic and the late Fardid's insights on opposites, but the detailed exposition was my own, and naturally any errors or shortcomings in it were mine.
Once again, I ask God Almighty for forgiveness, mercy, and elevation of rank for the deceased, and for patience and reward for his family, friends, and admirers. Undoubtedly, Iran's academic and scholarly community will always benefit from his written works, God willing.
🖋 Reza Soleyman Heshmat
