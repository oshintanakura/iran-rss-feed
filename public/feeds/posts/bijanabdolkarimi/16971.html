◾️ The passing of the esteemed professor and former member of the Department of Philosophy at the University of Tehran, Dr. Mohammad Reza Rikhtegaran, has caused deep sorrow and grief in the country's philosophical community. ◽️ Dr. Rikhtegaran spent his blessed life in the service of education and the promotion of wisdom and philosophy, nurturing many students along this path. ◽️ His works and writings were mainly in the fields of philosophy of art, phenomenology, existential philosophies, Eastern religions, and aesthetics. ◾️ We offer our condolences for this sorrowful loss to his respected family, the distinguished professors of the Department of Philosophy, students, and all lovers of philosophy, and we beseech God Almighty for divine mercy and forgiveness for his soul and patience and fortitude for the bereaved survivors. ⚫️ Undoubtedly, his name and memory will forever remain alive and eternal in the historical memory of the Department of Philosophy at the University of Tehran and the country's academic community. 🏛 The Scientific Association of Philosophy Students at the University of Tehran 🆔 @AnjomanPhUT
