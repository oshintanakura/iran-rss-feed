◇ When labeling takes the place of science and expert opinion

Dr. Farshad Momeni, criticizing the public sphere's descent into "left versus right" dichotomies and Heydari-Nemati-style squabbles, stresses that many of these labels bear no relation to science or national interests. Instead, he argues, attention must be paid to the "capture of the power structure" by usurers, brokers, and large-scale importers.

Citing studies on the effects of importing manufactured goods that could be produced domestically, he highlights the relationship between imports, employment, and national production, and also references Mohammad Naghizadeh's research on the job-destroying consequences of imports and the sale of raw materials.

In closing, Momeni calls on the government and the media to stop turning a blind eye to the main centers of threat to the national system, and instead — by raising the level of expertise and refraining from labeling — to pave the way for a scientific dialogue on national interests, national independence, and social justice.
