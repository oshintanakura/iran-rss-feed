On demagoguery: Demagogic politicians around the world use certain principles whose examples are not hard to find: 1. Questioning the speaker's motives: When someone presents a solid and documented argument, the demagogue does not say "this argument is wrong." Instead, he says: "Why is this person saying this? Who is behind him? What is his interest?" 2. Creating cynicism toward everything: The demagogue goes a step further and says: everyone lies, the media are corrupt, experts care about their own interests, politicians are all the same. When people do not trust any voice, the only voice that remains is his own. 3. Exploiting the natural ambiguity in speech: Language and expression have ambiguity, and every speaker has some interests. The demagogue exploits this half-truth to draw a completely false conclusion: because no argument is completely pure, no argument is trustworthy. Finding numerous examples is up to you!
