The golden rule of gold! Amirhossein Khaleqi, a political economy researcher, says in the program "Economy Politics" that to predict the trend of gold, before any analysis, one must answer a question: Is trust increasing or decreasing? According to him, whenever people's trust in the financial system, government, and economy weakens, the tendency toward assets that preserve their value in the long term increases, and gold is the most important safe haven for capital in such conditions. Khaleqi explains that humans are inevitably dependent on trust to preserve the value of their assets; either on human institutions like fiat money and governments, or on nature and limited assets like gold, whose supply has grown at a relatively constant rate throughout history.
