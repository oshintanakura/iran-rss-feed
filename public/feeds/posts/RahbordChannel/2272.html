On Trumpism: Trumpism is perhaps the most important challenge to the global economic order in the last decade. Its proponents believe that tariffs, support for domestic production, and bringing industries back to America can help improve employment, security, and America's industrial power. But critics warn that these policies may improve some economic indicators in the short term but will ultimately lead to a decline in consumer welfare, a drop in competitiveness, and a slowdown in the growth of the American and global economy.
