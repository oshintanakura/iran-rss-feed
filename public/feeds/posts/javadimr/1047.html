The person who posted this film tweeted: "Sir, it's impossible—we must uproot everything and build from scratch..." But I don't hold such a view. In my opinion, there's no need to uproot everything. In the absence of a government that is willing and able to deal with violators—and deal with them equally—our driving situation is acceptable; because the current order in driving, especially in Tehran, is the product of people interacting with each other in the absence of the government (in the sense of the state) and the police. We are left to our own devices, but we haven't gone out of control. People are good; the blame lies elsewhere. And of course, driving is a symbol and a sign of how the country is administered.
