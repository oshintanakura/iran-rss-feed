The negotiation-war dichotomy is not real. The general public does not want war at any cost or negotiation under any conditions. The main goal is to maximize national interests through a possible combination of war and negotiation, as decided by political-military officials. The street or television, without the necessary information, is not a decision-maker or evaluator; it supports the system's decision.
