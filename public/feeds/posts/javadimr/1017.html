Must political power change first so that social relations can be repaired, or is no lasting change in power possible without beginning to repair social relations? The reasonable answer is probably not in choosing one and eliminating the other. Freedom from domination and the ability to build, justice and care, exposing the source of harm and accepting individual responsibility, must be pursued simultaneously. A society that only engages in pathology will gradually become convinced of its own incapacity; a society that only speaks of kindness may become blind to the reality of power and injustice.
