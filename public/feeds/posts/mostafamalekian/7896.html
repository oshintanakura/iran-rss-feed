🔴 Four differences between morality and religious rituals and rites✍Mostafa Malekian🔹Why are religious rulings and rituals not among moral requirements? Because moral propositions and ritual religious propositions differ from one another, including:▪️ 1. Rational justificationMoral rulings are capable of rational justification; one can explain what effect observing or not observing them has on human life.For example, reason understands that lying destroys trust, misleads others in decision-making, and damages human relationships. But reason alone does not understand what problems human life in general would face if we did not perform wudu or tayammum.▪️ 2. The bond among human beingsMorality can serve as the basis of a shared system for the life of all human beings; a system that helps their coexistence and compatibility. But rituals and rites do not create such a universal commonality and can also mark the boundary between followers of different religions and sects.▪️ 3. Conscience versus instructionMorality is related to the inner conscience of the human being; a person feels within themselves that good is preferable to evil. But knowledge of rituals and rites depends on instruction and cultural transmission.Imagine a fourteen-year-old adolescent who has not received religious instruction: they can understand the meaning of honesty — that is, harmony among beliefs, feelings, desires, speech, and action — and grasp its value; but they do not know prayer, tayammum, or the Eucharist without instruction.▪️ 4. Stability versus historical dependenceMoral values are not specific to a particular time, geography, ethnicity, or nation; but the form of rituals and rites depends on historical and cultural contexts.For example, the form and limits of religious covering and what is considered hijab have not been the same in different periods and societies. Ways of worship also differ among religious traditions. But the value of honesty does not change with a change of clothing, land, or historical period; harmony of speech and action and not deceiving others still has moral value.➖An excerpt from a private conversation among friends - Dey 96@mostafamalekian
