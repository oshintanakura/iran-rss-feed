🔴Mostafa Malekian:🔹Allameh Tabatabai had a subtle point about friendship. He said friendship is like a bite of food; the first couple of times, a person chews it consciously, but from a certain point on, swallowing becomes unconscious. The moral characteristics of a friend are the same; initially, we only see their good or bad qualities, but after a while, those same qualities gradually permeate our being without us even noticing.@mostafamalekian
