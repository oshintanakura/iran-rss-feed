🔴The Effects of Meaningfulness in Life✍️Mostafa Malekian🔹According to many moral psychologists, those who consider life meaningful experience at least three profound effects in their lives.1️⃣ Inner CoherenceThe first effect of a meaningful life is the creation of a kind of inner coherence. A person is usually composed of different domains: beliefs, feelings, emotions, desires, and will. These domains are not always in harmony with each other and sometimes conflict.That's why sometimes we do something and immediately blame, reproach, or condemn ourselves. It's as if one part of our being says: "Do this," and another part says: "Why did you do that?"This struggle shows that a person is at war with themselves and lacks inner peace. Moral psychologists believe that when a person considers life meaningful, a kind of harmony and compatibility emerges among the different domains of their being. The person is at peace with themselves and moves away from inner turmoil.2️⃣ Increased Capacity to Endure SufferingThe second effect of a meaningful life is an increased capacity to endure suffering. Life is nothing but a collection of events and relationships, and many of these events and relationships are painful and distressing. Someone who considers life meaningful can better endure these sufferings. The greater the capacity to endure, the less intense the suffering is felt. Sometimes the level of tolerance increases so much that what was previously considered suffering no longer carries the same weight.Therefore, the meaningfulness of life causes a person to develop greater capacity for facing life's pains and hardships.3️⃣ Giving Purpose to LifeThe third effect of a meaningful life is giving purpose to life. If a person considers life meaningful, they can better determine:* what they should pursue in life;* and what they should refrain from pursuing.Meaningfulness clarifies the direction of a person's movement and gives purpose to their life.@mostafamalekian
