🔴The Effects of Believing Life Is Meaningless✍️Mostafa Malekian1️⃣ Boredom, Indifference, and a Vegetative LifeThe first effect of considering life meaningless is that life gradually takes on a vegetative state. A person no longer admires anything, has no deep interest in anything, and everything becomes monotonous and boring. Only when life is meaningful can a person praise something, become attached to something, and distinguish between different matters.In such a situation, life becomes the repetition of repetitions. Suppose someone is traveling from Tehran to Mashhad. Every time they stop at a gas station, they say: "I'm filling up the tank so I can reach Mashhad." This action has meaning because there is a destination.But if someone says: "I'm filling up the tank to reach the next gas station, and I'm going to the next gas station to fill up again and reach the next one," this cycle has no purpose and is mere repetition.Similarly, when life lacks meaning, a person's activities become aimless repetitions—just like a plant that bears fruit every year without knowing why.2️⃣ Nihilism, Anger, and FrustrationThe second consequence of considering life meaningless is nihilism. Nihilism means a person denies any meaning for life and even mocks those who attribute meaning to it.Moral psychologists believe such people typically suffer from:inner anger,a constant sense of frustration,and a kind of indifference toward the affairs of life.In their view, nothing truly matters; therefore, they don't feel much difference between various events and lose their sensitivity and attachment to life.3️⃣ Reduced Psychological Capacity to Cope with ProblemsThe third effect of considering life meaningless is the reduction of a person's psychological capacity. Meaning plays the same role for the mind and psyche that food plays for the body. Just as the body becomes weak if it doesn't receive food for several days, if life is meaningless for a person, their mind and psyche will also become weak and listless.As a result, a person finds less capacity to face life's problems and hardships. Enduring suffering means that after suffering befalls a person, they can tolerate and digest it within themselves. Coping with problems means having the strength to stand firm and resist before harm or injustice befalls them.According to moral psychologists, the meaningfulness of life strengthens both capacities in a person, but meaninglessness weakens both.@mostafamalekian
