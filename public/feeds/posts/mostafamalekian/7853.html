🔴What problems/questions exist regarding the statement "I believe"?✍Mostafa Malekian1. What necessity is there for believing?Why should one believe? Is life somehow deficient without faith? Many people live good and happy lives without faith. Do we have a moral obligation to believe? Do we have a prudential obligation to believe?2. What does believing mean?Is believing an action or a passion? In believing, are we active or passive? That is, do I perform an act whose name is faith, or do I undergo a reception of an act whose name is faith?3. Is this faith above knowledge or below knowledge?When I believe, does it mean I am less than someone who knows, or more? That is, when I believe that "A is B," is this higher than the awareness and knowledge of someone who knows "A is B," or lower?4. Who determines the object of faith?Assuming that believing is necessary, what should I believe in? In what things? Should I believe in propositions or in beings? Should I say "I believe in God" or "I believe in the proposition 'God exists'"?5. What function does faith have in my life that makes me different from unbelief?That is, what distinctions do those who have faith in their lives have compared to those whose lives are without faith?➖Excerpt from a lecture at the unveiling session of the book Questions of Faith by Peter Berger@mostafamalekian
