🔴Why do people turn to superstition?✍️Mostafa Malekian🔹The meaning of superstition, in the view of the Stoics, is that when there is a natural cause for something, we ignore that natural cause and consider an unnatural one instead.For example: Because I treated my wife so badly, we got divorced a year after our marriage.🔹Well, what is the natural cause? That you were ill-behaved; neither your words, nor your actions, nor your body language, nor your spending, nor your earning was right; well, you got divorced. That's called a natural cause.🔹But if you said: "On the night we were going to get married, the moon was in Scorpio, and that was the factor that caused our divorce," that becomes superstition.And here we must pay attention to the natural cause, not the unnatural ones (superstition).And we usually turn to superstition and unnatural causes because:1. We hide our own guilt.He says: "What could I do, man? It wasn't in my hands; the moon was in Scorpio!" I no longer say what behavior I engaged in, because I would become the guilty one.Or for example, he says: "I had left the broom upside down in the courtyard, and that was the reason my son and daughter-in-law had a fight!"Because we don't want to see what the real cause of our son and daughter-in-law's problems is, and to escape this responsibility and guilt, we turn to superstition.2. We don't seek to make amends.Because whenever we refer to natural causes, we must seek to make amends; but to avoid making amends and to escape responsibility for our mistakes, we turn to unnatural causes (superstition).➖Mostafa Malekian's lecture titled: Introducing the book "The Work of the Lighthearted"; Presenting 53 Teachings of the Stoic Epictetus@mostafamalekian
