🔴 Why is having an ideology so attractive? What are the harms of ideological thinking?✍️Mostafa Malekian🔹Ideology means believing that for every question, there is only one answer; for every issue, only one solution; and for every problem, only one way to fix it. As if the truth has already been discovered and there is no longer any need to think, ask, or search.🔹But why are people drawn to ideology? Because the world is far more complex than we would like it to be. Life is full of ambiguity, doubt, contradiction, and uncertainty, and humans naturally suffer from such a state. They want to see the world as clear, orderly, and predictable.🔹Ideology precisely targets this need. It promises to clarify everything; to answer all questions, to have solutions for all problems, and to save people from confusion and ambiguity. That's why it has always been attractive to many people.🔹But this peace comes at a heavy price. The harms of ideology: The first cost is setting aside personal reason. When the answers to all questions are prepared in advance, you no longer need to think for yourself; someone else has thought on your behalf.🔹The second cost is losing personal responsibility. When someone else makes the decisions, the responsibility for those decisions is also lifted from your shoulders.🔹The third cost is the loss of freedom and free will. An ideological person no longer chooses; rather, they obey. Obedience replaces questioning, and "as you command" replaces "why?"🔹And finally, the most dangerous situation is when ideology falls into the hands of political power. In this case, those in power also present themselves as the owners of truth. They no longer merely govern people's behavior, but they determine what truth is, how the world should be seen, and what is right or wrong. From this point on, power not only dominates people's lives; it also claims ownership over truth.➖Part of the lecture introducing the book The Power of the Powerless@mostafamalekian
