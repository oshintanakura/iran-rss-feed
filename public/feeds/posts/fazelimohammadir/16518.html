Solutions to the Iran-US conflict: from a national doctrine to redefining power. Sahand Iranmehr is an expert in international relations and foreign policy, although he is better known as a literary author. In this conversation, he examines the history of the Iran-US conflict from the Pahlavi era to the present day to reach the complexities of this relationship and show why this relationship can only be managed through a gradual process. Watch the conversation: https://youtu.be/G4p9D-CB28A. Sahand Iranmehr believes that the recent wars between the two countries have created grounds for different perceptions of the two countries' situations relative to each other, and despite all the mistrust, grounds have been created to move past tension and gradually move toward a state of conflict containment and competition management. He offers various solutions, including presenting a national doctrine to redefining power, as solutions for managing the Iran-US conflict.
