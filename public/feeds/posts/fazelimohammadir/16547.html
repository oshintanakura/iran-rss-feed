📚 Groundwater Governance: Developments and Advances

Iran's water crisis is no longer a matter of the future; we see its signs today in the drying up of wetlands, land subsidence, and the depletion of groundwater reserves. Years of unchecked extraction from aquifers have consumed resources that, for many, may take decades or even centuries to replenish.

The book "Groundwater Governance: Developments and Advances" takes an interdisciplinary look at one of the most important yet least visible aspects of the water crisis: groundwater — resources that remain unseen, yet the lives, agriculture, and food security of communities depend on them.

By examining the experiences of various countries, this book demonstrates that protecting groundwater is not achievable through isolated projects alone; it requires scientific management, sound policymaking, and cooperation among government, local communities, and consumers.

▪️Iran's water crisis, rather than being a crisis of water scarcity, may be a crisis of water management.
