Iran's Grand National Security Strategy: Forward Defense and the Axis of Resistance - Part Five
The fifth installment of the review of the book "On the Edge of Loneliness: An Account of Iran's Grand National Security Strategy" by Vali Nasr presents how the doctrine of "forward defense" and the formation of the "Axis of Resistance" came into being and evolved. Vali Nasr shows how "forward defense" and the "Axis of Resistance" emerged from Tehran's security needs and in response to actions taken by the United States in the region, especially in Iraq. Nasr continues to emphasize the geopolitical reality of Iran's actions, with the ideological veneer serving the geopolitical reality.
