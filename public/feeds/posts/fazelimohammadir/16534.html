Gradual Change or Revolution: The Importance of Parties in Iranian Society

Mohammadreza Jalaeipour, a sociology graduate of Oxford University and Chairman of the Central Council and Head of the Think Tank of the Ahd-e Iran Party, in this conversation emphasizes the necessity of gradual change in the system based on three principles: justice, solidarity, and democracy.
