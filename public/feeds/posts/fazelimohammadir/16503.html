To implement the plan to counter infiltration approved by the parliament, several new inquiry systems must be established:
The "SAKET" system
A system for evaluating classes and training
For every in-person and online course, and possibly in the future, cooking classes for family members.
The "JORM" system
Search and monitoring of demands
To review people's suggestions and determine which suggestions can be upgraded to a security case.
The "MABAD" system: A system for managing academic admissions and scholarships
Its name makes the applicant's situation clear from the start: "Don't you dare apply!"
Another version of it is the "BEMAN" system
Reviewing permits for applying and registering
To review requests for applying, admission, and scholarships from foreign universities.
The "BIGANEH" system: Integrated review of public conversations and unreliable communications
For any communication with a foreign person in cyberspace.
The "NARO" system
A system for monitoring entry to foreign representations
For issuing permits to visit embassies inside and outside the country.
The "EETERAF" system
Public declaration of cross-border interactions, relations, and communications
For voluntarily registering all the things you did before the system was established, when you didn't know that one day a system would be created for them.
