Give the gasoline to the people: fixing the imbalance with quota certificates. Sadegh Al-Hosseini argues that all the solutions proposed by the Ministry of Oil, the Plan and Budget Organization, and other solutions offered by others are inefficient and have more side effects compared to a plan based on giving an equal share of subsidized gasoline to all people. Watch the interview: https://youtu.be/4v6GxJolE9A. Al-Hosseini believes that by distributing an equal quota among all Iranians, based on a distribution of 90 million liters per day, and setting aside 30 million liters for working cars and motorcycles, the gasoline imbalance can be treated, and more importantly, an economic mechanism free from government intervention can be created to optimize equipment and energy consumers. In this detailed interview, he explains all aspects of his plan and finally addresses criticisms against the removal of the preferential exchange rate. This interview was recorded in response to Mohsen Yazdanpanah's critiques of the equal gasoline quota plan for everyone, which was previously published on IranTalk.
