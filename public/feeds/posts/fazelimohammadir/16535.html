Gradual Change or Revolution: The Importance of Parties in Iranian Society

Mohammadreza Jalaeipour, a sociology graduate and the head of the central council and head of the think tank of the Ahd-e Iran Party, in this conversation emphasizes the necessity of gradual change of the system based on three principles: justice, solidarity, and democracy.
