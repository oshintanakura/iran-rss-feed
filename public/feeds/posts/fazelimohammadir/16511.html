Gasoline Reforms: No Fireworks! Dr. Mohsen Yazdanpanah believes that Iran's economic situation and the government's constraints in supplying gasoline have reached a point where price and non-price reforms in gasoline have become necessary. However, the plan that some in the government are proposing and pursuing is dangerous and will have unpleasant consequences like those of Aban 98 (November 2019) or Dey 1404 (January 2026). Watch the interview: https://youtu.be/jcPunsWgbMc Mohsen Yazdanpanah advocates for several principles in designing gasoline reforms: being gradual and time-bound without price shocks; distinguishing between different types of gasoline and different gasoline consumption markets; and paying attention to the energy effects on Iran's overall economy, which have resulted from sanctions and may, due to shock therapy, lead to an economic disaster.
