Iran's Industrialization: So Far, So Hard

The Story of Failure in Industrialization

In the early 2000s, Masoud Nili drafted Iran's industrial development strategy, but he believes the governance of that era lacked the capacity to approve and implement it. Two decades later, he and his colleagues reexamined Iran's industrial trends and laid out the challenges facing Iran's industrialization.

In this conversation, Masoud Nili shows in detail what industrialization is, what its social effects are, what consequences de-industrialization has had for Iranian society, and what mechanisms in domestic politics and sanctions caused Iran's industry to decline from the mid-2000s onward. Ultimately, by comparing Iran's situation with four different categories of countries, he analyzes the effects of economic governance on industrialization.
