.👈 Personal Geography (Book Introduction)
Dr. Morteza Nemati, the resigned professor of Shahid Chamran University of Ahvaz, is one of my staunchest critics in cyberspace. He believes that the methods of people like me, which are based on dialogue and understanding, with the aim of gradual and low-cost transformation of the existing situation, are not only fruitless in today's Iran but also reinforce oppressive structures. See one of his critiques here:
https://t.me/MortezaNemati1/233
In December 2023, he tore up his doctoral degree, which he had received from the University of Tehran in 2010, in front of the camera in protest against the University of Tehran granting an honorary doctorate to Bashar al-Assad. Watch the video of this symbolic act of his at the following link:
https://t.me/MortezaNemati1/115
A year before that, after twelve years of teaching at Shahid Chamran University of Ahvaz, he had resigned from the faculty of that university in protest against the unscientific, non-transparent, and discriminatory processes in the selection and promotion of faculty members. Over these years, to earn an honorable living, he started a restaurant, painted, baked bread, and did many other things; and now he is setting up a small rock climbing school for teenagers in his hometown (Abdanan).
Now he has written his experience of these years of pressure, discrimination, loneliness, and hardship, in the university and in society, in a book titled 'Personal Geography.' His book has not been granted a publication permit, and he has now shared it with us digitally. His pen is biting, his language is blunt, his criticism is fearless, and sometimes borders on insult. But I believe in the words of John Maynard Keynes: 'Words ought to be a little wild, for they are the assaults of thoughts on the unthinking.'
