.👈 Effective Centers
(Introduction to the book 'Effectiveness in an Ineffective Institutional Environment')
Everyone is looking for big deeds; deeds of the revolutionary kind; hoping to fix the world quickly and all at once. The government is either pursuing a global revolution and overthrowing arrogance, through organizing resistance forces; or it is pursuing writing a vision plan that will become first in everything in the region within a short time. The government's opponents and critics are also seeking to overthrow the government by organizing widespread protests to fix the country overnight and with a revolution. But wars' main achievement has been blood, tyranny, and displacement, and revolutions' main achievement has been the destruction of the institutional and physical infrastructure of society and the postponement of democracy and development for several generations. The reality is this: the world has been improved and advanced by micro-level individual efforts and medium-scale collective actions.
