🔸 What initiates a society's movement towards the ladder of development, and what provides the energy needed to climb the difficult steps of this ladder, is nothing but the 'common understanding' and 'common demand' of the people for development.
🔸 Everything begins with 'wanting,' and it is impossible for people to want a common goal and not achieve it, and it is impossible for any government, no matter how powerful, to have enough energy to oppose the common will of the people.
🔸 In these days when the air pollution of many cities in the country takes our breath away, and the heaviness of lead tightens our chests, we want to open our minds and fly in the clear and beautiful sky of development. Let us try to reach a common understanding of development and know what to demand.
🔸 'Development Lectures' is a novel and structured video series that aims to explain the concept of development to all Iranian development-seekers.
🔸 This series intends to explain the development process and predict its future path, both by looking at the frontiers of knowledge in the field of development and by focusing on the indigenous characteristics of our country, Iran.
🔸 This series includes various chapters, the first of which is ready for release, and subsequent chapters are being prepared.
🔸 Development Lectures help us take a better and more efficient path for seeking development in Iran by understanding the concept, past, and future of development more precisely.
