.👈 Animation and Sexual Life
About five years ago, I participated in the 'Sexual Wisdom' series and spoke about 'Development and Sexual Life.' Because I believed that irrational taboos must be broken and the way must be opened for collective rationality. That series, without the support of official institutions, was a successful series and brought important topics to families. See my interview with that series here:
https://www.aparat.com/v/LV4U5
Now, after five years, a new season of that series titled 'Animation and Sexual Life' has been produced, which can provide important awareness to families who have children and where animation takes up a large part of their children's time. Caring for the data that unconsciously enters our children's minds in the process of daily life is a great responsibility on the shoulders of parents. The images that are imprinted on a child's mind in childhood through watching films and animations gradually create archetypes that cast a shadow over the person's mind and language for the rest of their life. A large part of our children's time passes in front of screens without any screening, and this is the most important gateway for harm to children. Watching and listening to the 'Animation and Sexual Life' series can make parents sensitive to the choice of films and animations that children watch.
This series deserves promotion because it has not only not been supported by official cultural institutions, but has even been denied an official publication permit due to the narrow-mindedness resulting from patriarchal cultural sediments. Therefore, it is fitting that affluent people attached to development support such productions.
