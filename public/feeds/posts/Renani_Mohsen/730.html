🔴Judgment Beyond Politics🔺
Trust, January 5, 2025
✍️Abbas Abdi
🔘Justice is established when the law is both fair and implemented impartially and completely. Since justice is an important pillar of any political system, and if it is violated, one must react, during the tenure of previous heads of the judiciary, there were many cases where I would write critiques of them. Fortunately, in the new administration, such cases have noticeably decreased. However, recently we are witnessing cases that require sensitivity, lest the path of judicial proceedings deviate as in the past. It is expected that the esteemed head of the judiciary will both pay attention to the general logic of this article and issue orders to investigate the mentioned cases.
🔘These cases concern Dr. Mohsen Renani, Dr. Mohsen Borhani, and Mohammad Mohajeri, and previously Dr. Ahmad Zeidabadi. All of them are among the best in their fields, and at least I have seen nothing but goodwill in their writings. In my opinion, these gentlemen have been subjected to biased proceedings because of their scientific, legal, or political writings.
🔘First, I will present here part of my defense statement from my last court session, which the esteemed judge did not allow me to read, arguing that it was outside the scope of the charge, while in my opinion it was completely relevant. This part of my defense shows why these types of proceedings are not fair.
