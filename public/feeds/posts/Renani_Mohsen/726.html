.👈 How Can a Country Be Stolen? (Book Introduction 👆👆👆)
This book, after remaining in the publication queue for two years without receiving a permit, was finally published digitally by the author. The book provides good information, history, and analysis of the processes and consequences of privatization throughout the entire post-revolutionary period. By reading this book, we understand why privatization in Iran did not lead to the formation of a competitive and dynamic economy and only resulted in the formation of a 'new class' of rentier capitalists. In my opinion, the Islamic Republic, through crony privatization, first became an economic fiefdom, and then the new class that emerged from economic fiefdom also moved the political structure of governance towards political fiefdom. Now Iran's economy and politics have become various fiefdoms in the hands of specific political-economic groups. The experience of Russia and some other countries also shows that these fiefdoms turn into economic and political gangsterism during long-term political-economic instabilities and crises.
Reading this book can be useful for those interested in understanding the causes of today's stagnation of Iran's economy.
