.👈 The Revolutionary City (Book Introduction)
We still have an old and traditional understanding of revolution. The nature of 21st-century revolutions is completely different from those of previous centuries. Urbanization, information technology, and globalization have changed the nature and dynamics of revolutionary movements. In the book 'The Revolutionary City,' Mark Baisinger, by analyzing historical developments and examining recent examples of revolutions, provides a theoretical framework for analyzing urban civil revolutions in the new era. The content and main theory of the book can be summarized in two points. One is the 'spatial theory of revolution' and the other is 'the power of numbers' instead of the power of weapons.
