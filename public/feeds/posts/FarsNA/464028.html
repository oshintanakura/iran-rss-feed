The Helpless Human

🔹At night, a king was very tired and wanted to sleep, but a persistent mosquito kept landing on his face and preventing him from sleeping.

🔹Each time the king raised his hand to swat the mosquito, it would fly away and his hand would strike his own face hard.

🔹This happened several times, and a king who ruled over a vast country and whom everyone held in awe could not overcome a tiny mosquito and was slapping himself.

🔹Meanwhile, one of the nobles who was watching this scene from afar turned to one of the mystics and sages present and asked: "What is the wisdom behind the creation of a mosquito with such a small and troublesome body?"

🔹That wise man answered: "God created it to show tyrants and powerful kings their own helplessness and weakness; so that they know that with all their claims and power, they are no match even for the smallest of God's creatures."
