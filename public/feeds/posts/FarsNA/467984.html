New foreign exchange directive issued for banks
🔹Under a circular from the General Directorate of Foreign Exchange Operations, instructions have been issued for granting $60 million in foreign exchange facilities from banks' own resources for the import of essential goods.
🔹This measure follows a Central Bank resolution and is being implemented to ensure food security and sustain the supply chain through the end of the current year.
🔹Per this directive, the allocation of these facilities comes directly from the internal resources of the operating banks.
