The prerequisite for theft

🔹A man took a fancy to becoming a thief, and to learn the trade professionally, he went to Nishapur, for he had heard that a skilled and seasoned thief lived there.

🔹When he entered the thief's house and stated his purpose, the master thief accepted him and took him on.

🔹The thief told him, "My son! On the path you've set out on, the very first step is that they cut off your right hand, because that is the religious law's ruling for a thief. Once they cut off your right hand, you must be able to eat with your left hand so you don't suffer."

🔹Hearing this, the man came to his senses and took heed. He said to himself, "For the sake of gaining a little silver and wire, it isn't wise to lose a hand this precious!" So right then and there, he put the whole idea of theft out of his mind and gave it up entirely.
