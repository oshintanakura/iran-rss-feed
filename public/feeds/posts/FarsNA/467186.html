Execution of Former US Army Officer by Firing Squad to Be Broadcast Live
🔹US Secretary of War Pete Hegseth said on Thursday that he intends to make it possible for the public to watch the execution of a former US Army officer.
🔹This officer has been sentenced to death for killing 13 people and wounding more than 30 others at Fort Hood base in the state of Texas.
🔹In an interview with US media, Hegseth said: "We will make sure that people can watch this execution; this punishment must be public."
🔹A US Department of Defense official also announced: "This execution will be broadcast live, and details will be announced later."
🔹Nidal Hasan, a US Army major and psychiatrist, opened fire on the afternoon of November 5, 2009, at the Soldier Readiness Processing Center at Fort Hood base.
🔹He was found guilty in a military court in 2013 and sentenced to death. He is now 56 years old and paralyzed from the waist down.
🔹According to a memo from Adam Tell, Acting Secretary of the US Army, he is scheduled to be executed by firing squad at 1 PM on December 3 at Fort Hood base.
