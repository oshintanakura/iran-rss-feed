Central Bank's final blow to the phantom deposits of trust companies

What is phantom depositing?

🔹Phantom depositing by trust companies referred to a phenomenon where the deposit of export earnings was reported to the bank, but upon attempting to use the funds, it turned out that no financial transfer had actually taken place.

🔹Last winter, the scale of phantom depositing by trust companies reached such levels that the government faced a dollar shortage. This shortage is considered one of the factors behind the government's decision to eliminate the preferential exchange rate.

What is the Central Bank's new mechanism?

🔹Under the Central Bank's new framework, trust companies must first transfer a specified amount, and only after full verification by the Central Bank do they receive authorization to move larger sums.
