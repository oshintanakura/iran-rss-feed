Joint statement by the US and its allies against Iran on the anniversary of the snapback mechanism

🔸The US, along with 50 allied countries, issued a joint statement on the occasion of the anniversary of the implementation of the so-called snapback mechanism.

🔹In this statement, without any reference to the violation of commitments by Western countries under the JCPOA, responsibility for the return of sanctions is attributed mainly to Iran, and countries around the world are called upon to enforce the restrictions imposed against Tehran.

🔹The statement was released on Thursday, October 2, by a group of member countries of the "Proliferation Security Initiative," with the US, UK, France, and Germany among its signatories.

🔹In the new statement, the US and its partner countries announced that they will continue to enforce the reinstated restrictions.

🔹They placed particular emphasis on preventing the transfer of equipment, technology, and materials that they claim could be used in sensitive nuclear activities, the development of weapons of mass destruction, or their delivery systems.

🔹The statement also endorsed US sanctions against Iran under the label "economic exclusion operation." @FarsNewsInt
