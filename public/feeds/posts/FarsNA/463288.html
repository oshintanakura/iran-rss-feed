Prosecutor's Office Steps In Over Araz Cup Fallout in Nowshahr

🔹Nowshahr Public Prosecutor: After judicial officers learned of certain conduct contrary to moral and religious standards on the beach next to the Araz International Hotel in Nowshahr, the matter was investigated on an urgent basis.

🔹After taking witness statements and reviewing several hours of CCTV footage from the Araz Hotel complex, it was established that the incident occurred after the conclusion of the three-on-three basketball and beach tennis competitions, on the beach adjacent to the complex.

🔹Preliminary investigations indicate that the oversight and coordination by the responsible agencies and the competition organizers require close scrutiny.

🔹If negligence or wrongdoing is established, the responsible individuals and legal entities will be dealt with in accordance with the law, and no leniency will be shown in enforcing the law.
