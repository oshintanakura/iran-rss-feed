The wound of loss and the salt of mockery

🔹A merchant lost a thousand dinars in trade, and his capital was wiped out. He turned to his son and said firmly, "Never breathe a word of this to anyone, and do not let friend or stranger find out about this loss."

🔹The son replied, "Father, I will carry out your command and keep my lips sealed; but I want to know what good comes of hiding this matter and what the wisdom behind it is."

🔹The merchant said, "So that our grief is not doubled: one is the loss of our capital and wealth, and the other is the mockery and taunting of neighbors and ill-wishers."
