The consequence of befriending deceivers

🔹A crow, a wolf, and a jackal lived in a thicket alongside a lion, and they got by on the leftovers of his kills.

🔹One day, a camel that had fallen behind from a caravan came to that thicket to graze. When the camel came upon the lion, it bowed, and the lion granted it safety, saying it could live in peace under his protection.

🔹Some time later, the lion was badly wounded in a fight with an elephant, collapsed in his cave, and lost the strength to hunt. The animals around him were left helpless with hunger. The crow, looking for a way out, proposed to his companions that they find a way to get the lion to eat the camel.

🔹The jackal said, "The lion has granted him safety, and breaking one's word is shameful." The crow replied, "Leave it to me; I'll devise a scheme so the camel himself walks into being sacrificed."

🔹The crow went to the lion and, with smooth talk, won him over and promised to arrange things so that there would be no need to break the covenant.

🔹Then he went to the wolf, the jackal, and the camel and said, "The Sultan's life is in danger; come, let us show our loyalty and offer ourselves. The lion does not eat our flesh, but the custom of service is fulfilled."

🔹They all went to the lion. First the crow stepped forward and offered himself, but the wolf and the jackal cried out, "You're scrawny and your flesh tastes foul!" Then the jackal volunteered, and the others called his flesh stinking. It was the wolf's turn, and they deemed his flesh harmful.

🔹The simple-hearted camel, seeing this staging, thought to himself that if he too made an offer, the companions would, following the same custom as before, come up with some excuse and the lion's protection would hold. So he came forward and said, "My body is a sacrifice for the Sultan; my flesh is pure and will fill everyone." No sooner had he finished speaking than all three animals said with one voice, "He speaks the truth!" and immediately fell upon him and made him their prey.
