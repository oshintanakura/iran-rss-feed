Eurasia agreement to serve the oil market
🔹Iran Customs transferred part of Golestan's sunflower oil import quota to Gilan so that oil shipments would not sit idle at Gilan Customs.
🔹Under this transfer, Golestan's quota dropped from 10,000 tons to 2,000 tons, and 8,000 tons of the remaining quota was reassigned to Gilan. With this decision, Gilan's oil import capacity rose from 170,000 tons to 178,000 tons.
🔹The aim is to enable importers to clear their shipments under the preferential tariff of the Iran-Eurasia free trade agreement, so that oil imports do not run into trouble.
