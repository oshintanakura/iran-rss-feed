The Price of Greed

🔹A fox happened to fall into a well bucket and, as the rope turned, went down to the bottom of the well. Struggle as it might, it found no way out—until a hungry wolf came to the well's edge and lowered its head.

🔹The clever fox put on an act and cried out: "Down here a feast of food and comfort awaits, but I can't manage it alone; if you'd like to share the meal, get into the upper bucket so you can come down to me!"

🔹The greedy wolf leapt without a moment's hesitation and sat in the upper bucket. Since the wolf was heavier, its bucket hurtled downward, and at the same time the rope turned and drew the fox's bucket upward.

🔹Midway up the well, the two buckets passed each other. The wolf cried out in astonishment: "Where are you off to, friend?!"

🔹The fox laughed and said: "That's the way of this wheel of fortune—unless one goes down, the other never comes up!" The fox reached the bright mouth of the well and sprang out, while the wolf was left behind in the dark depths.
