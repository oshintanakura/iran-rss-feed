Google cracks down on fake journalists
🔹Google has updated its content quality evaluation guidelines, classifying the creation of fake author identities — including the use of names, professional backgrounds, and AI-generated images — as deception.
🔹Under these guidelines, websites that fabricate authors' identities to pass off their content as written by real experts may lose their credibility and ranking in Google search results.
🔹The decision comes amid a surge in media outlets seeking to attract visitors by mass-producing AI content and inventing nonexistent journalists.
🔹Google stresses that the issue is not the use of AI, but rather the fabrication of human identities to deceive audiences and lend false credibility to content.@Farsna - Link
