US's insulting conditions for resuming Najaf flights
🔹The US Treasury Department, by issuing a temporary license, announced bizarre conditions for resuming flights to Najaf Ashraf.
🔹Under this license, only Iraqi airlines are permitted to transport passengers, and Iranian companies will still have no right to enter Iraqi airspace.

🔹The US Treasury Department has banned the transfer of large amounts of cash, shipments belonging to Iran's intelligence, military, and law enforcement agencies, as well as the transfer of sanctioned individuals. This means that under this license, Iranian officials and figures who are under US sanctions will have no right to air travel for pilgrimage to the holy sites.
🔹Iraq must also prevent the entry or passage of Iranian flights through its airspace beyond permitted cases, otherwise the issued license will be canceled or amended.
