Pentagon Admits War with Iran Has Cost $43.6 Billion
🔹In its latest estimate, the Pentagon has admitted that the war with Iran has cost the United States $43.6 billion so far — a figure that does not yet include possible damage to American military facilities in 8 countries across West Asia.
According to a document the Pentagon submitted to Congress, this figure breaks down into two parts:
🔸$11.2 billion in expenses such as fuel, salaries and benefits for personnel involved in operations, medical services, equipment parts and maintenance, and other operational costs
🔸$32.4 billion in compensatory costs, including the cost of replacing expended munitions, damaged aircraft, and other military equipment and assets
🔹Accordingly, a significant portion of the estimated cost of the war stems not merely from day-to-day operational expenses, but from compensating for lost or damaged munitions and military equipment.
