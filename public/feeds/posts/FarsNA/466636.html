Saudi oil strikes out for the desert instead of the sea

🔹The insecurity of maritime routes and attacks on oil infrastructure have pushed Aramco to explore transferring Saudi oil overland to Oman and exporting it from the Arabian Sea coast.

🔹Saudi Arabia currently relies on two main routes to export its oil: the Strait of Hormuz and the Red Sea.

🔹Both routes have faced escalating security tensions in recent years, and attacks by Yemeni forces have driven up the cost and risk of oil tanker transit for Saudi Arabia.

🔹For this reason, Riyadh is seeking options to move oil overland through Saudi territory to a safer point before it reaches high-risk maritime routes.

🔹Saudi Arabia wants to transport part of its oil overland from the east of the country to a new outlet on the Arabian Sea coast, and from there ship it to Asian markets.
