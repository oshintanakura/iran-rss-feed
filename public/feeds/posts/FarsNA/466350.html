Trouble with second teams for Esteghlal and Persepolis

🔹Under the Asian Football Confederation's new rules, clubs that own two teams are not permitted to take part in certain competitions (such as the Hazfi Cup), and if they do so, they will lose even the right to compete in the AFC Champions League Elite and the AFC Champions League Two.

🔹In other words, Esteghlal Club, which owns Esteghlal B of Sistan as its second team, will not be allowed to compete in Asia if it wants to enter the Hazfi Cup alongside that team — unless Esteghlal Club prevents the Esteghlal B of Sistan team from participating in the Hazfi Cup.

🔹Persepolis, which is looking to buy a second team from one of the clubs in the lower leagues, will face the same situation — unless the second team purchased by clubs across Asia has separate statutes and is entirely separate from the first team.
