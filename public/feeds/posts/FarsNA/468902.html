🎥 Fire at a tourist resort in Bangladesh leaves at least 10 dead

🔹A fire at a tourist resort on the beach in the Cox's Bazar area of southeastern Bangladesh left at least 10 people dead and 9 injured.

🔹Bangladesh's fire service said in a statement that the incident occurred this morning at a wooden resort and restaurant on Inani beach. According to the agency, the fire was fully contained after two fire service teams arrived at the scene and with the cooperation of local authorities and police.

🔹According to the statement, the fire may have started from the resort's kitchen stove or from an electrical short circuit; however, the exact cause of the incident has not yet been confirmed.
