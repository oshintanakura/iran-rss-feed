The story of the martyrdom of a member of the Leader's special office, the martyr🔹 Martyr Mohammad Reza Saghafi-Far was one of the members of the special office of the martyred Leader of the Revolution. From 1368, upon joining the Corps of the Wali al-Amr, he served the martyred Leader of the Revolution with love and sincerity, and after retirement, by order of His Eminence, he remained by his side. On the ninth of Esfand 1404, though that day's shift was not his and he had gone to the workplace in place of his colleague, he was martyred in an American-Zionist attack.🔹The martyr's family say that Martyr Saghafi-Far's devotion was not limited to physical security alone; he was also steeped in ideals. His wife shares a moving memory: "My husband served the family of the martyred Leader of the Revolution for thirty years as a bodyguard, with love and devotion. After he retired, His Eminence the Leader had told one of his sons to call him so that he could speak with him. Our martyred Leader had told my husband: 'Mr. Saghafi! Where do you want to go?' My husband had said: 'Dear Leader! I have retired; I only had this much worthiness to be with you until now.' His Eminence had said: 'You are my fellow townsman and you are a Khorasani. Where do you want to go? From now on, come and be my assistant.' Upon hearing these words of our martyred Leader, my husband became very happy and would tell his daughter, 'I must remain by the Leader's side until the reappearance of Imam Zaman (may God Almighty hasten his blessed relief),' and he kept this pledge until his last breath."🔹His wife says, "We wished to meet with the Leader, but he would not get us a meeting card and would say, go and stand in line like ordinary people."🔹On the ninth of Esfand, the day of the incident, it was as if the lot of martyrdom had fallen to his name. His colleagues say that day was not his shift, but he stepped into the field in place of his colleague. His wife has a strange account of the morning of that day: "He did not take his bag of documents, his watch, or his ring with him; it was as if he knew he would not need these on this shift." He went with a light spirit, carrying none of his worldly attachments with him.@Farsna
