Photos and memories of the martyred driver of the martyred Leader of the Revolution

🔹 Martyr Haj Abbas Saadabadi was a veteran of the Sacred Defense era who joined Ayatollah Khamenei's protection team in 1984, during his presidency. After some time, he became his driver and also took charge of the transportation unit.

🔹 After years of loyal service, he was martyred on the 11th of Esfand, 1404, during a renewed bombardment of the area around the Leader's residence.

🔹 According to the martyr's family: The martyred Leader of the Revolution always insisted that the movement of protection vehicles should never inconvenience the people, and he had told Haj Abbas that if they were to reach their destination on time, the necessary coordination must be arranged in advance so there would be no need to disturb the public. This sensitivity was evident during provincial trips as well.

🔹 One day, on a certain occasion, the martyred Leader told Haj Abbas: "A grandchild is not only a son's child; we must also be attentive to the daughter's child and their upbringing." This same outlook had made Haj Abbas equally attentive and responsible toward his family, children, grandchildren, and the families of martyrs.

🔹 After years of service, when Haj Abbas decided to retire, on the last day of his service he went to the martyred Leader and said, "If you permit, I would like to leave." The martyred Leader asked in surprise: "Why so soon?" He then told Sardar Jabbari, the commander of the Vali-e-Amr Protection Corps at the time: "Keep Abbas Agha."
