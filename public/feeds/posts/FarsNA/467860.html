Saudi Arabia's Deadlock in the War with Yemen

🔹A British magazine has written about the worsening of Saudi Arabia's security crisis following missile strikes by the Yemeni army on Riyadh airport and the failure of the counterattack by the country's allied forces in Yemen — a situation that, beyond casting doubt on Saudi Arabia's military capability, also threatens Bin Salman's economic ambitions.

Saudi Arabia's Defenseless Skies

🔸The Economist writes that the reasons behind the failure of Saudi Arabia's air defense systems to intercept the missiles that struck Riyadh airport remain unclear. This comes even as Saudi Arabia is equipped with advanced American "THAAD" and "Patriot" systems. Saudi Arabia is also worried about the depletion of its interceptor missile stockpiles.

The Counterattack Has Hit a Dead End

🔹The halt of the Saudis' advance 40 kilometers south of the port of Al-Mokha shows that stagnation has once again returned to the battlefield. At the same time, Yemeni army forces are trying to besiege the city of Taiz, Yemen's third-largest city and one of the most important strongholds of Saudi-affiliated forces.

Saudi Arabia's Economic Losses from the Ongoing War

Negotiation: A Costly Option for Riyadh

Saudi Arabia, by contrast, faces direct threats against its investments, airports, and energy sector — threats that also jeopardize the country's plans to become a regional hub for tourism and business.
