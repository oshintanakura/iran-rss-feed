Bronze medal for Iran's female weightlifter
🔹Mahsa Beheshti, Iran's second female weightlifter in these competitions, won the bronze medal.
🔹Her opponents were from Bahrain, Turkmenistan, Mongolia, Japan, China, Kazakhstan, and South Korea.
🔹Beheshti took fourth place in the snatch with 113 kilograms; in the clean and jerk, her first attempt at 140 kilograms was unsuccessful, but she succeeded on her second attempt with 146 kilograms. Her Turkmen opponent failed to lift the final weight, and the bronze went to Beheshti.
