Temporary relocation of the "Clenched Fist" element in Enqelab Square for reinforcement

🔹The "Clenched Fist" structure, which had been temporarily installed in Enqelab-e Islami Square in Tehran on the eve of the funeral ceremony for the martyred leader of the revolution, was removed for repairs and improvements.

🔹After repairs, the structure will be reinstalled in Enqelab Square.
