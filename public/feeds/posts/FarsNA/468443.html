Smugglers of 200 historical objects caught in security forces' net
🔹More than 200 historical and cultural pieces that members of a 10-person gang planned to smuggle out of the country by exploiting wartime conditions were uncovered through the intelligence prowess of security forces.
🔹The collection includes artifacts ranging from pre-Islamic pottery to objects from the medieval Islamic period, and cultural heritage experts are examining and appraising them.
🔹Some of the artifacts, including objects from the Qalachi Bukan archaeological site, will be returned to their provinces of origin in coordination with the Ministry of Cultural Heritage. Elamite inscriptions and inscribed bricks belonging to Khuzestan are also among the discovered artifacts.
