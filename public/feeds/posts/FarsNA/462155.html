Europe's plan for teenagers' online space
🔹The European Union plans to restrict access for under-15s to social networks, video-sharing platforms, AI chatbots, and online games.
🔹Under this plan, children aged 3 to 12 will only be able to use age-appropriate services with parental controls, while teenagers aged 13 and 14 will have access to restricted accounts under parental supervision.
🔹Tech companies will also be required to strengthen age verification for users, parental control tools, and mechanisms for reporting harmful content.
🔹This proposal has not yet been finalized, and to become law it must be reviewed and approved by the European Parliament and among member states.
