Misplaced foresight

🔹One day a hunter went out to hunt and caught a deer. He picked up the deer's carcass and headed home. On the way, he came across a boar that attacked him.

🔹The hunter let an arrow fly and struck the boar on the temple; but as he was drawing his second arrow, the boar at that very moment dealt him a fatal wound and brought him down, and both died on the spot.

🔹A hungry wolf arrived at that plain and, seeing the bodies of the hunter, the deer, and the boar, was overcome with joy and said to himself: "Abundant provision has fallen to my lot, but the dictate of reason is to think of the future and not be greedy; I will hide and store the fresh meats so that I may live carefree for a long time, and today I will content myself with chewing only the leather and the bowstring."

🔹No sooner had the wolf set its teeth on the drawn bowstring and begun to chew it than the bent, compressed corner of the bow's wood snapped back with force, struck the wolf on the throat and neck, and killed it on the spot; so that all 4 creatures became victims of death in one place.
