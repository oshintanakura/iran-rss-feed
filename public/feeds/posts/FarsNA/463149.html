Grossi's rival withdraws from UN Secretary-General race
🔹Michelle Bachelet, Chile's first female president and former UN Human Rights Commissioner, has announced her withdrawal from the UN Secretary-General election.
🔹Chile's first female president said in a video message: "Some may believe the time was not right for a candidacy with the characteristics I represented. However, I do not regret accepting this great challenge."
🔹With her withdrawal, seven candidates (four women and three men) now remain in the running for UN Secretary-General. Rafael Grossi, Director General of the International Atomic Energy Agency, is one of them.
