Traffic Police: Skateboarding and scooter riding are prohibited on public roads
🔹 Rahvar Traffic Police of Urban Traffic: Using skateboards and scooters on public roads is prohibited, except on routes where the police have permitted their use by installing signs.
🔹 In the event of an accident, a scooter is also considered a vehicle, and the type of vehicle, how it was being operated, and its traffic conditions will be examined in the expert assessment process and in determining the contributing factors to the incident.
🔹 The police's aim is not to prevent the use of skateboards and scooters, but rather to prevent accidents and potential injuries, especially for children and adolescents.
