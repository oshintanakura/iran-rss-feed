Fuel card storage has not changed
🔹Despite some reports about changes to fuel card storage, the manager of the smart fuel system at the National Iranian Oil Products Distribution Company says no such change has been made.
🔸On this basis, if a vehicle has three months' worth (180 liters) of 1,500-toman gasoline, it will not receive a new quota in the fourth month, and the card balance will remain at 180 liters.
🔸The second-tier quota — that is, 3,000-toman gasoline — likewise cannot be stored or carried over to subsequent months.
