Attack on a supertanker off the US coast
🔸The FBI and the US Coast Guard are investigating a cyberattack on a supertanker near the coast of Texas.
🔹According to this report, the ship's propulsion system was breached. It remains unclear how long the hackers had access to this system and which parts of the ship they were able to control through it.
🔹Supplementary reports name the tanker as VL Prosperity and place the time of the attack on August 7, 2026, as it was passing through the Strait of Gibraltar.
🔹Some media outlets have also claimed that the hackers disrupted the ship's engine, cooling system, and navigation; however, US officials have not officially confirmed these details.
