Think good, so that good may come your way. In the time of Anoushirvan's reign, 2 men came to the court and stood before the palace. One cried out loudly: "Do no evil and think no evil!" And the other said: "Do good and think good, so that good may come to you!" Anoushirvan ordered that the first man be given a reward of one thousand dinars and the second man two thousand dinars. Those close to him and the courtiers asked the king in surprise: "Both statements meant the same thing; so why did you give one a greater reward?" Anoushirvan answered: "The second man spoke only of good, but the first man also mentioned evil. There is no good higher than friendship with the good and speaking of good, just as no evil is any different from keeping company with evil." #Hekayat @Farsna
