Sensitive F-35 fighter jet components ended up in China

🔹Bloomberg: A mistake in the shipping process for components of the advanced F-35 fighter jet caused a shipment that was supposed to be transferred from South Korea and Taiwan to the United States to be rerouted to Hong Kong and ultimately end up in China.

🔹The shipment in question contained two F-35 components: the cockpit canopy and the weapons bay door. Both parts were coated with radar-absorbent materials — materials that help reduce radar wave reflection and enhance the aircraft's stealth capabilities.

🔹An official from the U.S. Government Accountability Office said China likely tried, after obtaining the components, to reverse-engineer them to examine the structure and technology used, and to find ways to counter the aircraft's technical capabilities.
