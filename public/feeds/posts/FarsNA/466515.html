A mouth that opened at the wrong time

🔹In a clear pond, two ducks and a turtle were neighbors, and a deep friendship and bond had formed between them. Time passed, and due to drought, the water in the pond began to dry up.

🔹The ducks, seeing that it was no longer possible to stay, came to the turtle to say goodbye and migrate to another body of water.

🔹The turtle lamented with sorrow and tears and said: "Waterlessness is far more dangerous for me, a slow-moving animal, than for you, and my life is impossible without any water. It is not the way of chivalry that you abandon me in this waterlessness; think of me and take me along with you."

🔹The ducks said: "Being apart from you is hard for us too, but you have a flaw: you take the advice of friends lightly and you don't shut your mouth at the right time!

🔹If you want us to take you too, there is one condition: we will bring a stick, we two will hold its two ends in our beaks, and you hold the middle of the stick firmly with your mouth so we can take flight. In the sky, no matter what people say and whatever shouts they raise, do not open your mouth!"

🔹The turtle promised and said: "I will obey and will not open my lips until the destination."

🔹The ducks brought the stick, the turtle gripped its middle firmly with its teeth, and they leapt into the air. As they were passing over a village, people's eyes fell on this amazing scene. An uproar rose among the people and they shouted: "Look! How the ducks are carrying the turtle through the air with a stick!"

🔹Hearing the clamor, the turtle could not endure it and became angry. Thinking it would say something to them, it opened its mouth and said: "May your eyes be blinded!"

🔹But as soon as it opened its lips, the stick was released from its mouth, and it fell from the height of the sky to the ground.
