"Forgotten" for Journalists
The documentary "Forgotten," directed by Mehdi Afsharnik, will be screened on Wednesday, September 11, at the Department of Environment, with the presence of department officials, journalists, environmental experts, and activists. This screening, coinciding with the escalating debates over the development of the Sohrab oil field and the relationship between the oil industry and Hor al-Azim, could pave the way for a serious dialogue among officials, critics, and the filmmakers.
The Wednesday screening at the Department of Environment gains even more significance because department officials, experts, and environmental journalists are set to watch together a film that narrates part of the very controversy that has returned to the forefront of discussions today with the Sohrab field case. A film that, on one hand, critiques oil and, on the other, does not align with part of the prevailing environmental narrative; a combination that will likely make the post-screening session as compelling as the film itself.
