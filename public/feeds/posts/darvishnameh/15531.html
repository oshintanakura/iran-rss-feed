🏗 Moving Steel Industries to the Coast, Our Resilience StrategyMohammad Darvish, an environmental activist, emphasized in an interview with Sama the necessity of relocating steel industries to coastal lines to reduce vulnerability in critical conditions. According to him, this relocation, if done with due considerations, not only causes no harm but also brings economic benefits to residents of southern cities.
