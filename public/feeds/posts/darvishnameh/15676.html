🔴 Shared bikes still at a crossroads: good or bad?✍️ Dr. Mehdi Hassanzadeh40 percent of trips across the Tehran metropolis are under five kilometers, equivalent to eight million out of 20 million daily trips, some of which have the potential to be made by bicycle. Of this share, a significant portion lies in the central core of Tehran, including Districts 6, 7, 11, and 12, where people currently make these short trips by motorcycle or taxi, though they could potentially be made by shared bicycles. Moreover, under current conditions, the central area of Tehran sees a significant volume of motorcycles moving around for daily trips, and as a result of this heavy traffic, we are witnessing chaos, reduced safety, collisions with pedestrians, disruption of safe movement, and so on.Given that Tehran's public transportation system does not operate in an integrated manner and the first and last kilometers of trips often require citizens to cover considerable distances to reach public transit stations, establishing a shared bicycle system in the central core of cities can alleviate part of this problem and facilitate getting to public transportation or from public transportation to one's final destination.Given the country's current circumstances and the two recent wars that have made the issue of fuel imbalance more serious, as well as the importance of infrastructure and the likelihood that infrastructure may become unavailable, and on the other hand, the issue of passive defense, which has not received sufficient attention in the transportation sector, the importance of establishing a shared bicycle system in Tehran becomes far greater. This is because the shared bicycle system, with no dependence on fuel, at low cost, and with far less dependence on transportation infrastructure, can make short trips possible under critical conditions and also enable trips to shelters (in critical conditions).📰 This note, titled "Shared bikes still at a crossroads: good or bad," has been published in the Shargh newspaper:https://www.sharghdaily.com/fa/tiny/news-1130470
