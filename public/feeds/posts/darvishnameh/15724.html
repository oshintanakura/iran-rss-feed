🟢 The 90th Night of Iran has arrived at Iranian Sustainability! 🟢

✍️ According to a Miras-e Arya reporter, the 90th gathering in the "Night of Iran" series, titled "Night of Iranian Sustainability" and centered on "Environment as Life," will be held at the Andisheh Cultural Center.

Mohammadreza Vahdati, secretary of the "Night of Iran" gatherings, referring to the topic of this gathering, said: "The Night of Iranian Sustainability is an opportunity to talk about the environment — not just as the nature around us, but as an inseparable part of Iran's life, culture, and future."

He added: "At this gathering, Mohammad Darvish, writer and environmental activist, will raise important environmental concerns facing Iran and speak to the question of why the environment is no longer merely a specialized topic, but is directly tied to quality of life, the future of society, and the fate of future generations."

Vahdati continued: "At this gathering, an effort will be made for the concept of 'sustainability' to move beyond a scientific and specialized term and be examined as part of Iranian culture and way of life — from the way we deal with water, soil, and nature to our social responsibility toward the land in which we live."

According to the secretary of the "Night of Iran" gatherings, this program is intended to address the relationship between Iranians and nature, the concept of sustainability, and the future of the land from a different perspective, and to provide a basis for dialogue about the role of the environment in the quality of life and Iranian culture.

The 90th gathering of "Night of Iran," titled "Night of Iranian Sustainability," will be held on Tuesday, 14 Mehr 1405, at 17:00 at the Andisheh Cultural Center.

This cultural center is located in Tehran, on Shariati Street, before Seyyed Khandan Bridge, in Andisheh Park.
