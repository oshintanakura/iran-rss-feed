⭕️ Paralysis of Desalination Plants🧵According to CNN, in early September 2026, an unprecedented growth of microalgae on the Mediterranean coast led to the temporary shutdown of five out of six desalination plants of the Israeli regime. Since these facilities supply more than 65% of the drinking water in the region, this has created serious challenges for the regime's water security. The news of the paralysis of Israel's desalination plants due to the phenomenon of marine algae goes beyond an environmental crisis and is a tangible manifestation of the concept of "Desalination Deterrence." Contrary to unscientific claims about the regime being a pioneer in water security with desalination!!! The regime's excessive dependence on centralized, technocratic coastal infrastructure has created a strategic "Single Point of Failure" in which even a non-military natural phenomenon can cut off the country's vital artery and expose its water security to deep and humiliating disruption. The narrative of Israel as a "water superpower" collapses in the face of the inherent vulnerability of desalination technology. The transformation of water from a natural resource into a centralized security commodity has taken the Zionist regime hostage to any disruption (natural, cyber, or military). This #paralysis is not a rare occurrence but a symbol of the structural humiliation of a regime that has built its vital security and survival on a weak, costly, and highly fragile foundation. This issue holds many lessons for policymakers not to tie the country's water security to the interests of managers of #Tusi_Companies.
