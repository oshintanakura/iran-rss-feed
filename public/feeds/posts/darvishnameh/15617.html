Unveiling of the book "Iranian Sustainability: Environment as Life"
A look at the link between sustainable development, the responsible citizen, and public education
Beh-Mehr has for years pursued the approach of "human development, from children's education to good citizens," and regards education as one of the main grounds for shaping responsible behavior toward society and the environment. In this vein, and coinciding with the start of the new school year, the book "Iranian Sustainability: Environment as Life" will be unveiled in the presence of Mohammad Darvish, researcher and environmental activist — a session dedicated to examining the relationship between education, the environment, and sustainable development.
In this session, two central questions will be discussed:
1. If environmental education is part of the path toward educating a responsible citizen, what revisions do educational policymaking and the role of the school, teacher, and assessment methods require?
2. If the environmental crisis is tied to our way of living and decision-making, how can the school help shape a generation with more responsible choices and lifestyles?
We invite those interested to join us in this scientific session.
