🟢 A way to reduce bird casualties on the power grid in Markazi Province 🟢📚 @darvishnameh✍️ On the second of Shahrivar 1405, while visiting several fish farms that used solar panels—and whose reports I will publish soon—I came across this valuable initiative carried out by the staff of the Electric Power Distribution Company of Markazi Province. So far, they have installed more than 307,000 insulating covers with polyka pipes, stretching six thousand kilometers, on 20-kilovolt power lines beneath needle-shaped perches—as you can see in this video—to reduce bird casualties, as well as to lessen damage to their equipment and reduce voltage drops and power outages on the grid.
