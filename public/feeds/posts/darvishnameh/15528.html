🔹 🔹 Warnings about tower construction on the shores of Mazandaran went unheard / The retreat of the Caspian Sea and the intensifying risk of land subsidence in Sorkhroud🔖 @BABOLIHAA Dr. #Ali_Beytollahi:🔴 A case-by-case assessment on the coast of #Sorkhroud showed the distance from the intersection of Darya Boulevard and Boostan Street (the square between East and West Boostan) to the shoreline was about 200 meters in 2001, and this distance reached 325 meters in the summer of 2026, 25 years later.🔴 This difference indicates a 124-meter retreat of the water's edge in the Sorkhroud area. It has been announced that the sea level has dropped by 2 to 2.5 meters over the past decade. The drop in sea level and the retreat of the water's edge by up to 125 meters in the Sorkhroud area will intensify the trend of #land_subsidence in this area and generally along the Caspian Sea coast. 🔴This danger becomes seriously concerning when the extensive tower constructions in these areas have expanded dramatically in recent years. The image shows the numerous shadows of the towers built on the Sorkhroud coast.Friday, August 14, 2026. #Baboli-ha Information Channel, Media for Sustainable Development of #Babol County
