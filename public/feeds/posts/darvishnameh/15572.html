✅️ Fatemeh Mohajerani: Films like "Forgotten" bring environmental issues to the public's attention 1️⃣ On September 2, 2026 (11 Shahrivar 1405), the documentary "Forgotten," directed by Mehdi Afsharnik and narrated by Mohammad Darvish, was screened at the Department of Environment. The documentary was shown in the presence of the head of the Department of Environment, the government spokesperson, a number of department managers, and experts and activists in the fields of environment and oil. 2️⃣ Fatemeh Mohajerani, the government spokesperson, said after watching "Forgotten," highlighting the importance of environmental documentaries: "Films like this can turn environmental issues into a public concern." Mohammad Darvish, the documentary's narrator, also spoke about how the film's narrative came together: "In this film, we tried to reach out to every stakeholder and hear the various accounts and perspectives on Hour al-Azim." 3️⃣ Mehdi Afsharnik, the director of "Forgotten," also noted the Ministry of Oil's support for making the documentary and said: "The government should support this move by the Ministry of Oil; the fact that the Ministry of Oil has invested in making a film that ultimately criticizes the ministry itself and the oil industry's performance is a significant development. Government support for such an approach could dispel oil managers' doubts about investing in and participating in the production of independent, critical works." 4️⃣ Later in the ceremony, the experts and managers present also spoke about the state of Hour al-Azim, the environmental challenges facing this wetland, and its recent developments.
