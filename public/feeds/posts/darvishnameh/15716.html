✅ Documentary "Forgotten" Selected for the British Columbia Environmental Film Festival

🔹 The documentary "Forgotten," directed by Mehdi Afsharnik, has been selected for the official section of the British Columbia Environmental Film Festival (BCEFF) in Canada.

🔹 The festival, which focuses on environmental issues and the intersection of science, society, and documentary storytelling, will this year host works by environmental filmmakers and storytellers from around the world.

🔹 "Forgotten" tells the story of the state of Hawizeh Marsh and the crises that have affected this wetland on the Iran-Iraq border in recent years. Filmed in Iran, Iraq, and Turkey, the documentary explores the issues of water, dam construction, oil development, and their environmental and human consequences.

🔹 Mohammad Darvish, environmental activist and researcher, narrates the documentary, which seeks to transcend political borders and paint a picture of the many factors that have shaped the fate of one of the region's most important wetlands.

🔹 The online section of the British Columbia Environmental Film Festival will run from November 1 to 30, 2026, and its in-person program will take place on November 22 at the University of British Columbia (UBC).
