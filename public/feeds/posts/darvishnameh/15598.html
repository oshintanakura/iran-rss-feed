The Iranology Foundation is holding: A specialized session introducing and reviewing the book "Iranian Sustainability: Environment as Life"
⏰️ Sunday, 22 Shahrivar 1405, at 14:30
🏢 Iranology Foundation, Mohtasham Kashani Hall
🎤Speakers:
Dr. Ghasem Azizi, Professor at the University of Tehran
Dr. Mohammad Fazeli, researcher and social activist
Dr. Hayedeh Shirzadi, environmental specialist and activist
Mohammad Darvish, editor of the book's collection of articles and environmental activist
🖋Secretary of the session: Dr. Hamidreza Shibani, faculty member of the Iranology Foundation,
Book description:
The book "Iranian Sustainability: Environment as Life" comprises 16 articles by prominent experts and thinkers in the field of the environment, in three sections, compiled with an effort and foreword by Mohammad Darvish.
It should be noted that the purchase and signing ceremony of the book will be held on the sidelines of this session.

✅️The Iranology Foundation will welcome the general public and interested parties at this session.
