Waste Crisis: Local, Small, and Thoughtful Solutions
This conversation is for those who want to do something effective to improve Iran's situation. Waste management in Iran is not in good shape. The northern provinces of Gilan and Mazandaran are in a far worse state, and mountains of waste show that the crisis has become chronic. Waste causes pollution of water, soil, and air, and has adverse effects on human health.

Narges Azari has researched and worked on #waste_management for ten years, examined waste management policy-making, and ultimately established a think tank to think and act on waste management. In this conversation, she recounts the problems of waste management, policy shortcomings, stories of failures, and instances of successes in this field.
