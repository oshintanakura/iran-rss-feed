🎥🎄🌲🪓🪓Why were 30,000 trees cut down in Chitgar?#IRNA_NewsAgency❌Tehran-IRNA- The IRNA correspondent's observations from Chitgar Forest Park on the first day of Shahrivar show that the widespread cutting and removal of trees in this park is underway. According to the municipality's announcement, this operation is being carried out under a plan called the "Third Health and Cultivation Operation to Dispose of Infected Trees and Plant Compatible Saplings in Chitgar Forest Park." According to an environmental expert, the lack of irrigation in this park in recent years has caused these trees to dry out and become infested with pests, and the priority should be preserving and irrigating the old trees. #Chitgar#TreeCutting🔴 Iran Water House News and Information Channel 📱 @WaterOnline
