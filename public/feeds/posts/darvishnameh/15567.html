✳️ A cordial meeting between the President and environmental activists; the country's environmental concerns were raised🔸 A cordial meeting between environmental activists and the President, coordinated and followed up by the Department of Environment, was held on Thursday, September 3, 2026, with the presence of Shina Ansari, the Vice President and Head of the Department of Environment.🔹 In this meeting, environmental activists and experts shared their views and suggestions with the President regarding the most important environmental challenges facing the country.🔹 According to the meeting's agenda, Shina Ansari also delivered a speech at this gathering, followed by Reza Maknoon speaking on "Designing a Transition to Desirable Conditions for Environment and Water in Iran," Mohammad Darvish on "Environment, an Opportunity for Iran's Development," Afshin Danehkar on "A Paradigmatic Rethink of Land Management in the Country," Mohammad Nasrollahi Al-Mouti on participatory governance in the Department of Environment and the formation of a participation council for the organization and civil institutions, and Naghmeh Mobarghaei Dinan on "The Relationship Between Environment and Economy; Hidden Values and Damages in the Environment."News link🆔 @doenews
