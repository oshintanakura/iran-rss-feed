🟢 Introducing a good book by Hanif Reza Golzar 🟢✍️ The book "Human and Its Consequences," the second work of Hanif Reza Golzar, an environmental and natural resources researcher, was published in September 2026 by Nashr-e Resanesh Novin. This book is nearly six hundred pages long and, in sixteen chapters, examines the relationship between humans and nature, civilization, power, ethics, ideology, and technology. By distinguishing between the concepts of "intelligence" and "wisdom," the author raises the question of whether humans are truly a wise species or whether their greed has had far-reaching consequences for other living beings and the Earth's ecosystems. The role of the environment in the formation of the first civilizations and religions, the relationship between natural resources and the emergence of autocracy and tyranny, and the critique of concepts such as freedom, equality, property, democracy, and human rights are among the other topics of the book. "Human and Its Consequences" also examines the environmental record of movements such as socialism, capitalism, and feminism, and, relying on scientific studies, discusses the issue of the production and consumption of genetically modified agricultural products and its relation to food security and environmental protection.
