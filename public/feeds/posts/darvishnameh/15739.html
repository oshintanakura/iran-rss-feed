📌Immediate solution to overcoming the water crisis: implementing the National Food Security Document
🔹Environmental activist Mohammad Darvish sees the immediate solution to the water crisis as lying in the implementation of the National Food Security Document. This document calls for a 10 percent annual reduction in water consumption, and by implementing it, surplus water could be used to restore wetlands and rivers. There would be no need for dam construction and water transfer at great expense.
🔹Darvish believes that Iran has more water resources than its neighbors, yet its water shortage crisis is more severe. He sees mismanagement and improper water use as the main cause of this crisis, and calls for following the example of countries that have tackled drought.
