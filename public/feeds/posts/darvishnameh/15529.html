🔹 The slaughter of Hor al-Azim, at the oil wells, the only living part of the Hor al-Azim wetland, will become lifeless with the issuance of an environmental permit for the development of the Sohrab oil field. According to officials of the Environmental Organization, between 18 and 20 hectares of the northernmost part of the Hor al-Azim wetland will be dried up to increase oil extraction in this oil field. The inflow of Karkheh water into the Hor al-Azim wetland had made the location of the Sohrab oil field the only part of this ecosystem that has preserved its ecological functions. The fragmentation of the wetland bed reduces hopes for its restoration. The lack of water reaching the wetland bed also increases the likelihood of fires. / Haft Sobh🔹 @haftsobh_news🌿 @asoondar
