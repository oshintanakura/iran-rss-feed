Book Launch and Review Session for "Iranian Sustainability: Environment as Life" Held at the Be-Mehrbani Foundation (Darolekram) Mashhad

On Wednesday, 8 Mehr, the Be-Mehrbani Foundation (Darolekram) Mashhad branch hosted an inspiring event in the field of education and the environment.

The session was attended by 40 enthusiasts, concerned individuals, and activists in this field, along with the honored presence of the book's author, Mr. Mohammad Darvish.

The program, organized with the aim of explaining the role of public education in shaping responsible behavior toward the ecosystem, gave attendees the opportunity to hear the author's insights about the book, as well as to ask questions and exchange views on the challenges of sustainable development and the educational system.

The final part of this warm gathering was dedicated to book signings by Mr. Mohammad Darvish and the presentation of a number of copies to those in attendance.

The Be-Mehrbani Foundation, while expressing its gratitude for the valuable presence of Professor Mohammad Darvish and all the dear participants, hopes that the continuation of such programs will be an effective step toward raising environmental awareness and fostering responsible citizens.
