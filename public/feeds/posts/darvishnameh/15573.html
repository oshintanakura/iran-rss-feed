✅️ Good news for nature lovers around the world! 📚 @darvishnameh ✍️ An energy company plans to upgrade a refinery using technology from the Chinese firm Niutech to process waste plastics on an industrial scale. Waste plastics are converted into hydrocarbon materials through pyrolysis—thermal decomposition in an oxygen-free environment. The resulting product can be used as refinery feedstock to produce jet fuel. Niutech's technology essentially aims to solve a major problem in the plastics recycling industry: turning low-value, contaminated plastics into a hydrocarbon material with higher economic value. If this technology proves economically and technically successful on a large scale, it could tackle two issues at once: reducing plastic waste and supplying part of the raw materials needed by the jet fuel industry. https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/refinery-upgrade-turn-plastic-waste-080700948.html
