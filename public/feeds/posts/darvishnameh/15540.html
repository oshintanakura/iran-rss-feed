✅️ When the sun comes to the aid of epilepsy patients! 📚 @darvishnameh
1️⃣ At least one percent of our dear compatriots suffer from different forms of epilepsy. Recent power outages can be very harmful to them, especially if they occur during dental procedures, as they may, God forbid, trigger seizures.
2️⃣ Therefore, after consulting with the noble people responsible for the Iranian Epilepsy Association, we want to build a ten-kilowatt solar panel equipped with a hybrid inverter and lithium LFP (iron phosphate) battery for them.
3️⃣ The technical work for this structure will be carried out by Dastan Sanat Azarhoush company without charging any fees. The estimated cost is about two and a half billion tomans. I hope the exchange rate helps and it doesn't get more expensive!
4️⃣ The association's account number, IBAN, and card details are provided at the end of the video. Even contributions as small as a thousand tomans are valuable to us, because we want to show our dear compatriots struggling with this disease how many supporters they have...
