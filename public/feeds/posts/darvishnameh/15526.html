🟢 When the sun in Shazand, Arak, comes to the aid of fish! 🟢📚 @darvishnameh1️⃣ Using solar panels in fish farming complexes can have significant benefits; because aquaculture inherently requires relatively large amounts of electricity for water pumping, aeration, treatment, and water circulation.2️⃣ An important point is that in many fish farming complexes, part of the consumption load occurs during the day, coinciding with sunlight; therefore, solar power can be used directly for pumps and aerators, reducing the need for batteries. Also, as explained in the video: the damage caused by power outages at a fish farm can be very dangerous; because stopping aeration, especially in dense ponds, can reduce dissolved oxygen within a relatively short time and cause fish mortality. A solar system with a hybrid inverter and battery increases production security.3️⃣ But what can make this plan more attractive in Iran is designing a low-carbon aquaculture complex with intelligent water and energy management, leading to fish production with a lower water and carbon footprint. In recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS), this becomes even more important, as electricity consumption for pumping, filtration, and oxygenation is high.4️⃣ Bravo to those who are pioneers in greening the fish farming industry in Iran.
