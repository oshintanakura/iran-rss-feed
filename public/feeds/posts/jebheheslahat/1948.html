❇️ Spokesman of the Political Office of the National Development Party: The positions of the Iran Reform Front stem from the national sense of responsibility of the members of this organization

🔺Abbas Mousaei, spokesman of the Political Office of the National Development Party, reacting to the filing of a judicial case against the members of the Steering Board of the Iran Reform Front for announcing the positions of this political front regarding the unfortunate events of last Dey, said: The positions of the Iran Reform Front at various junctures, particularly the critical conditions of recent years, have stemmed from the national sense of responsibility of the members of this legal organization.

◾️Stating that the Reform Front, based on the strategy of "reform from within," considers itself committed to defending and pursuing the legal and legitimate rights and demands of society, he added: The fact that the Iran Reform Front has objected to certain procedures and approaches and their harmful consequences and has frankly shared this with the nation and the governance, stems from the nature of reformist and national politicking and the effort to prevent the deepening of problems and to set things right. From this perspective, it is questionable why, instead of hearing the voice of reform-seeking and accountability, this responsible approach of the Reform Front is met by security institutions with baseless accusations such as alignment with foreigners, propaganda against the system, and disturbing public opinion.

◾️The spokesman of the Political Office emphasized: Not recognizing peaceful mechanisms for criticizing performance and raising society's demands, and criminalizing reformist political activity under the title of propaganda against the system, means nothing other than restricting and threatening legal political freedoms and securitizing the political atmosphere. On the other hand, issuing heavy sentences including execution or confiscation of individuals' property merely for the crime of protest and expression of opinion not only solves no problem but adds to the hidden anger and protest of society and will lead to dangerous consequences such as what we witnessed in the Dey catastrophe of last year.

◾️Abbas Mousaei added: The Political Office of the National Development Party asks the officials of the Judiciary to, in accordance with the explicit principles of the Constitution regarding the recognition of the right to political activity, freedom of expression, and the right to criticize and its requirements and exigencies, especially in the current critical conditions, while immediately halting the death sentences and other heavy sentences issued in connection with the protests, review the aforementioned cases.
