❇️ Summoning of the Secretary of the Iran Reform Front to the Security Prosecutor's Office

🔺 Badr al-Sadat Mofidi, Secretary of the Iran Reform Front, appeared today at Branch 2 of the Security Prosecutor's Office of District 33, and after interrogation and presentation of final defenses, was released on bail.

◾️ The summoning of the Secretary of the Iran Reform Front comes as part of the ongoing process of summoning and filing cases against members of the Front's Board of Directors. This case concerns the Iran Reform Front's statement about the catastrophe of 18 and 19 Dey, which was published in Bahman of last year.

◾️ In that statement, the Iran Reform Front, while expressing sympathy and solidarity with the grieving people of Iran, emphasized the need for fundamental, urgent, and serious changes and a reconsideration of past approaches, and declared that reformist efforts to have reforms recognized as part of the solution have reached a dead end.

◾️ The formation of an independent fact-finding committee to investigate the various dimensions of this catastrophe, as well as the formation of a national assembly bringing together all elites and concerned citizens of the country to find ways out of the existing crises, were among the proposals put forward in this statement.

◾️ The issuance of this statement was met with a reaction from security and judicial bodies and the summoning of a number of members of the Board of Directors of the Iran Reform Front — a process that continues with the summoning of the Front's Secretary.
