◾️ The head of the political committee of the Iran Reform Front also emphasized that the Front's statement should be examined and judged not as an individual act, but as a collective act, in connection with the chain of activities of the Iran Reform Front.

◾️ The statement in question was published last Bahman in reaction to the events of Dey 18 and 19, and a judicial case has been opened over it for a number of members of the board of directors of the Iran Reform Front.
