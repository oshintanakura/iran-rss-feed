❇️ Examining ways to support the Islamabad Agreement at a joint meeting of the Committee for Provincial Affairs of the Iran Reform Front with the presiding boards of the provincial reform fronts

🔺 A virtual meeting was held between members of the Committee for Provincial Affairs of the Iran Reform Front and the heads and members of the presiding boards of the provincial reform fronts, focused on examining ways for the provinces to participate in the national campaign to support the Islamabad Agreement, promoting peace-seeking, and efforts to end the war.

▪️ The guest of the meeting was Ali Shakouri-Rad, who is in charge of launching the national campaign to support the Islamabad Agreement. Outlining the goals and plans of the campaign, he described support for the agreement as a national and cross-factional endeavor and emphasized that this document was built on strong foundations—including its approval by the Supreme National Security Council, confirmation by the Leadership, and the signing of the presidents of the two countries—and should not be easily cast aside.

▪️ Pointing to the need to strengthen the voice of supporters of peace and understanding in the country's public sphere, he said: "The goal of the campaign is to create social momentum and pave the way for a more active presence of moderate forces and supporters of the agreement in the public arena—forces that, in his view, despite backing this path, have so far lacked a prominent presence and voice."

▪️ Ali Shakouri-Rad, emphasizing the importance of the Islamabad Agreement in the process of ending the war, described it as a starting point for subsequent Iran-US talks and stated that even amid disagreement and conflict, the parties cite the provisions of this agreement to advance their positions. Preserving this document and pursuing its provisions can therefore serve as a basis for continuing negotiations and reaching further understandings.

▪️ The official in charge of launching the campaign also announced meetings with foreign policy experts and the formation of specialized committees to advance the plans, stressing the need to expand this movement to the provinces. According to him, holding brainstorming sessions with experts and influential provincial figures can help clarify the dimensions of the agreement and strengthen social participation in support of peace.

▪️ The meeting continued with discussions and an exchange of views on how the provincial reform fronts can join the campaign, the holding of provincial meetings, and the mechanism for coordinating activities at the national and provincial levels.
