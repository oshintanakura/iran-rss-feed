❇️ Head of the Reform Front of Hamadan Province: Supporting the Islamabad Agreement Requires Serious Action by the Government

🔺Ali Mehrabadi, Head of the Reform Front of Hamadan Province, speaking at a joint meeting of the Committee for Provincial Affairs of the Reform Front of Iran with the board of directors of the provincial reform fronts, held to examine ways of supporting the Islamabad Agreement, noted several positive steps taken after the agreement was signed — including the lifting of the naval blockade, the resumption of Iran's oil sales, and negotiations for the return of the country's assets — and stressed the need to clarify what has caused the agreement to stall.

▪️Stating that unless it is determined how much responsibility each party bears for derailing the agreement, and as long as leniency is shown in this regard, support campaigns will not achieve the desired outcome, he said: Supporting the agreement requires, beyond civil actions and popular campaigns, serious and responsible action by official authorities, especially the government.

▪️Ali Mehrabadi noted that despite the follow-ups, no permit has yet been issued for a gathering in support of the agreement, which the Reform Front of Iran had previously requested, and added: As long as marginal confrontations and the chanting of harsh slogans continue at nighttime gatherings, and no appropriate action is taken by official authorities — particularly the government — to manage these conditions, such campaigns in support of the agreement will yield little and will not be widely embraced by the public.

▪️In conclusion, the Head of the Reform Front of Hamadan Province, while commending the intentions of the organizers of these campaigns, expressed hope that these efforts will bear fruit, and emphasized: Alongside these actions, it is essential that Mr. Pezeshkian and the government take serious and effective measures to support those who find themselves in these difficult conditions.
