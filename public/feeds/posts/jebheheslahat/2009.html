❇️ Head of the Reformists Front of Gilan Province: Reviving the Islamabad Agreement must become the organizational priority of the Reformists Front

🔺 Saharkhiz, Head of the Reformists Front of Gilan Province, stressing the need for the Reformists Front to play its organizational role under the country's sensitive conditions, proposed that reviving the Islamabad Agreement and preventing the country from sliding back into a state of war be placed on the agenda as the main focus of this organization's activities until further notice.

▪️ At the joint meeting of the Committee on Provincial Affairs of the Reformists Front of Iran with the presiding boards of the provincial reformist fronts, he proposed that speeches and meetings related to the issue of peace and the Islamabad Agreement be held across the country with greater speed, with the participation of all reformists—both members active in organizational meetings and other forces of this movement.

▪️ Saharkhiz also identified the launch of an online campaign in support of the Islamabad Agreement as another necessary step, expressing hope that it would be widely welcomed by a people weary of war.

▪️ The Head of the Reformists Front of Gilan Province, referring to the media capacity of reformists in the provinces, stressed the need to centrally activate news outlets and media close to this movement, and called on the public relations and media committee of the Reformists Front of Iran to play a more effective role in coordinating these capacities.

▪️ In conclusion, he stressed the need to plan for utilizing the organizational and media capacities of reformists in support of peace and agreement.
