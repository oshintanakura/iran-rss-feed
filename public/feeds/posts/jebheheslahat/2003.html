❇️ Head of the Reform Front of Hormozgan Province: Support for the agreement must be carried out with the participation of all pillars of the government and political currents

🔺 Mohammad Zakeri, Head of the Reform Front of Hormozgan Province, emphasizing the importance of the Islamabad Agreement, said: Without a doubt, this agreement is a great and unprecedented step that is the result of sacrifices, courage, and political and social efforts made in the country.

▪️ Referring to the need to avoid politicizing this movement, he stated: In order for support for the agreement to bear fruit, the pillars of the government must first clarify their position, and governance institutions and principled figures who agree with this path must also step forward. Governors must also play a role in this process so that the ground is prepared for synergy among different sectors.

▪️ Zakeri added: If only the Reform Front is to take the lead and the main burden of this movement falls on the shoulders of the reformists, the work will not reach the desired result and the movement will remain incomplete. Given the unfavorable conditions of many provinces and the costs that gathering forces entails, it is necessary that this action begin at the level of the government and the pillars of governance and then expand in the provinces.

▪️ The Head of the Reform Front of Hormozgan Province proposed that before entering into field actions, the capacity for support of the agreement in cyberspace should be measured and the level of public reception should be evaluated, so that if the ground is prepared, executive and field plans can be pursued with stronger backing.
