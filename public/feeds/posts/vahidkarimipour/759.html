Apparently, the Mokhbareh site has removed all the courses provided by our colleagues that were freely available to all students of Iranian universities from its site. The site claims this was done at the request of the university. The university should presumably have a replacement method in mind to compensate for this incomprehensible action. I personally am surprised why the Mokhbareh site accepted such a thing, because the instructors of these courses have a share in their intellectual and moral ownership, and first and foremost, the instructors' consent should have been obtained.
