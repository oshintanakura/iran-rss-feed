Greetings and respect to each and every dear representative of the units of the Iranian University Faculty Union. I, Alireza Aghaei, a member of the central council of the Iranian University Faculty Union, in protest against the election of the First Vice President as the head of the union! which took place tonight, January 4, 2026, resign from membership in the central council. It is my honor that in the previous term of the council, I received the first vote and in the current term, the second vote of the elected members of the twelve-member central council from you, the representatives of the union units in universities across the country, and I have been and am grateful for your trust. Nevertheless, while apologizing profusely to you, dear representatives of the union units, I consider the presence of the First Vice President in the position of head of the faculty union as a clear example of conflict of interest and an insult to the union, and I do not deem my membership in this council permissible. With renewed respect 🌹 Alireza Aghaei
