The story of Ashraf Marwan, son-in-law of Gamal Abdel Nasser and Mossad's intelligence source, is a complex example of this hidden war; where intelligence, trust, deception, and misanalysis were intertwined. But more important than the existence of a human agent is the lesson that can be learned from this case, and that is how the closest individuals can endorse a misanalysis of the situation under the guise of patriotic strategy and become part of the cycle of miscalculation. Therefore, if today within a country some create an atmosphere where decision-makers think that any tactical restraint or thought of agreement is a sign of weakness, or that any costly action is necessarily a sign of authority, in practice—even if their intentions are good—they may reproduce the same miscalculation that the enemy has invested years in creating. No wise commander spends all of a country's national capabilities in battle. No professional chess player sacrifices his queen merely to show boldness. Power is not in consuming tools; it is in preserving and using them at the right time. From this perspective, defending the country is not just defending borders; it is also defending strategic reason, even if they set up a faction or group or the rent of television and the country's platforms is in their name. Just as infiltration into a military center can threaten security, the infiltration of a wrong logic into the decision-making process can lead the country toward choices that the enemy did not even expect in its most optimistic scenarios. For this reason, a clear line must be drawn between resistance and extremism. Resistance is the art of increasing the enemy's cost with minimal national cost. Extremism is the readiness to pay any cost without calculating the cost-benefit ratio. The former creates deterrence; the latter may erode that very deterrence. A country that considers these two as one not only weakens the concept of resistance but also unintentionally exposes one of its most important defensive lines, namely the health of strategic calculations, to threat.
