Sahand Iranmehr - Although the division between Bedouin and city-dweller in the case of Ansarullah - Saudi Arabia (and its satellites) is not very precise, in terms of unequal welfare and resources, my angle of view unconsciously ties to Ibn Khaldun's "Muqaddimah":
"City-dwellers have grown accustomed to comfort and prosperity. They have entrusted the defense of their lives and property to rulers and soldiers and rely on the walls and fortifications of cities… Generation after generation is raised in such a condition until they come to resemble women and children, who are dependent on the head of the household.
But the Bedouins are their own guardians, always carrying weapons, relying on their own strength, and courage has become for them a trait and a nature."
