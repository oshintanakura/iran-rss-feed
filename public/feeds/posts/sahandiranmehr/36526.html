The World of Retribution: Does the world have a reckoning? Do people eventually see the consequences of what they do to others? Is the world truly a place of retribution, and should one never be heedless of the consequences of their actions? In this episode, we turn to a tale from the book "Al-Faraj Ba'd al-Shiddah" by "Tanukhi": the story of a man who, at the height of his power, imprisons another and threatens him with the whip, only to later fall into the hands of the same structure of power himself. This episode examines the difference between a just world and a world where human actions have consequences, yet there is no guarantee that justice will be served.
