🔶According to data published by the Institute for the Study of War (ISW) and the Critical Threats Project (CTP), which is affiliated with AEI, a total of 1,530 locations across Iran were recorded in open sources as sites of airstrikes or attack-related explosions between February 28 and September 8, 2026 — 420 of them in Tehran. These figures represent a count of "reported locations," not the number of attacks or human casualties; that is, multiple reports may correspond to a single attack, and a single location may have been struck more than once. According to this map, 1,287 of the 1,530 recorded locations were reported during the first 40 days of the war, while only 13 were recorded between April 9 and June 7; then, in June and July, another wave of attacks emerged with 226 reported locations, 138 of which were in southern Iran, in areas below the 30th parallel. Meanwhile, areas such as Bushehr, Bandar Abbas, and Qeshm, which had been targeted before, once again stood out in the data from the second period. Of the total 1,530 cases, 544 are classified as confirmed airstrikes, 550 as reported attacks, and 436 as explosions captured on video; however, in 1,142 cases, the perpetrator of the attack is not identified in the published data. This dataset merely offers a picture of the geographic and temporal distribution of reported cases and, on its own, cannot show the extent of human and civilian damage from any given attack.@sahandiranmehr
