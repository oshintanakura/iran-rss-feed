Solutions to the Iran-US Conflict: From a National Doctrine to Redefining Power
Sahand Iranmehr is an expert in international relations and foreign policy, though he is better known as a literary author. In this conversation, he examines the history of the Iran-US conflict from the Pahlavi era to the present day to reach the complexities of this relationship and show why it can only be managed through a gradual process. Watch the interview: https://youtu.be/G4p9D-CB28A
Sahand Iranmehr believes that the recent wars between the two countries have created grounds for different understandings of each other's situations, and despite all the mistrust, they have paved the way for moving past tensions and gradually toward a state of conflict containment and competition management. He offers various solutions, including presenting a #National_Doctrine and redefining power, as ways to manage the Iran-US conflict.
