🔹 We are no less than the soil
"How does the soil breathe? Let us think...
Birds of the air died in flocks
The flower-bringers of the eternal meadow withered
In the sky and on the earth, ambush had panicked
In the narrowness of time, death had lingered!
Has the world come to an end?
The sky had no answer
Will the garden laugh again?
No one was certain
What a strange bitter cold...
How does the soil breathe? Let us learn: the glory of sprouting, now:
The dawn of Farvardin!
All that snow melted
All these flowers bloomed
All these colors blossomed!
The earth taught us
Not to step back before the event
Are we less than the soil?
The earth breathed
Why should we not breathe?"
Excerpt from the poem "How Does the Soil Breathe" by the late Fereydoun Moshiri, from the collection "Eternal Beauty"
