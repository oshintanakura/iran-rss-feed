🔹 The Powerless Powerful You wake up in the morning, start the usual routine of another day of life. After the bathroom and possibly a morning shower, you go to prepare tea and open the fridge for breakfast. No need to think; the same breakfast as always. Woe to the day when one of the breakfast components has run out and you forgot to buy it. What disaster is greater than this? As you get older, you become more dependent on daily routines. When you are young, you quickly replace bread and cheese with an omelet or fried eggs. You might even call a friend to go out for breakfast. But as you age, changing life routines becomes harder and harder. Because change requires "decision," and what a difficult task that is. The bigger the decision, the harder it becomes, and one of the hardest decisions is the decision to retire. To accept that enough is enough. A decision that changes the entire life plan. Perhaps at 60 you might have the courage to retire voluntarily, but if you pass 80, it becomes impossible. You will no longer have the ability to make such a decision. So if you are still in a position or office after 80, it is not due to your ability, but your inability to decide. https://t.me/RezaEjtehadi
