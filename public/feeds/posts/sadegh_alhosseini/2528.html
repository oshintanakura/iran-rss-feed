Trump voters' remorse: what's the real number? A new Navigator poll says 18 percent of Trump voters in 2024 regret their vote. But that's not the politically meaningful number: only 8 percent are willing to change their vote. That's about 3.5 percent of all voters, which isn't small in the American system but is far less than the headline suggests. The erosion is from the margins of the coalition, not its core. Among Republicans, only 9 percent of MAGA are remorseful, versus 24 percent of non-MAGA and 22 percent of younger voters. The reason for the remorse is also clear: unfulfilled promises (30 percent), war and foreign policy (27 percent), and the cost of living (25 percent). In the closed-ended question, 45 percent directly named the war with Iran. A more interesting point: in this same sample, Trump voters who are not remorseful have a positive economic assessment. Two groups in a single economy report two completely opposite economic experiences. Economic perception is a function of political identity, not merely of prices.@sadegh_alhosseini
